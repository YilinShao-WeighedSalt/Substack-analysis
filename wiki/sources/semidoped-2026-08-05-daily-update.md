# Daily Update — August 5th, 2026

## Highlights
- **US drafts ban on Chinese datacenter components; optical stocks whipsaw.** Landed 5 days after Innolight's **$6.81B Hong Kong IPO**. Chinese optical names (ZJ Innolight) slumped; Western rivals **[[LITE]] Lumentum, [[GLW]] Corning, [[MRVL]] Marvell surged** on the substitution trade. FCC weighing separate curbs. People's Daily warned Washington. Complication (Austin): the expensive silicon inside an Innolight 800G module is usually a **Broadcom or Marvell DSP** — ban the module, hit AVGO/MRVL volume too; and you can't instantly ramp non-Chinese alignment capacity. [[COHR]] Coherent may face its own residual-China-exposure complications.
- **AMD [[AMD]] Q2:** record **$11.5B (+50%)**, datacenter now **58%** of company, told analysts 2027 estimates too low, demand "unforecastable" — but **weak forward AI guide** cooled a stock that had run +7% day / +13% week into the print. Austin: credible rack-scale #2 exists now ($6.7B/qtr DC vs Nvidia $75B); **Helios (MI455X) ships end of Q3**; robbing-Peter (consumer) to pay-Paul (datacenter); AI silicon lower margin drags blended %.
- **Anthropic commits $10B compute to Volta Infrastructure** — a 6-month-old London startup ($2.4B val, Nvidia+Dell backed, JPMorgan credit backstop), DCs in Norway, €8.6B. Vik: "do companies even do DD anymore?"
- **FMS 2026 NAND density:** Kioxia+SanDisk **332-layer QLC @ 4,800 MT/s** (claim highest bit density); Samsung **zHBM / zNAND-O** concepts; SK Hynix+SanDisk HBF spec (up to 512 GB). Silicon Motion (KV-cache controllers), Marvell, Rambus (DDR5 RDIMM) all present.
- Sector: Tower guides Q3 $520M; TSMC Kumamoto resumes; Lattice [[LSCC]] record +62%; Aehr follow-on SiPho order; Furukawa $635M fiber capex; Ciena/Toshiba 1.6 Tb/s quantum-safe; Everspin+MaxLinear MRAM; FuriosaAI 7–8.8k NPUs in Sweden.
