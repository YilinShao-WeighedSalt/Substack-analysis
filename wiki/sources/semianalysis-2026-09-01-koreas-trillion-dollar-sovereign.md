# Korea's Trillion-Dollar Sovereign AI Investment

Deep dive on South Korea's sovereign-AI program and the two investor implications: **why Nvidia is sovereign AI's biggest supporter**, and **why Korea's ambitions may not be aligned with Samsung/SK Hynix shareholders**.

**Framing — dependency risk drives sovereignty.** Access to frontier models sits at the mercy of Anthropic, OpenAI, and the USG (Fable 5 temp-banned; GPT-5.6/Astra delayed). Open source is no silver bullet — licenses tightening (Kimi K3 needs a Moonshot deal above $20M MaaS revenue), and today's open model may not be open in 2028. The only full fix: pretrain your own model on your own GPUs — a calculus that will soon confront every major nation-state.

**Korea's "Squid Games" AI tournament.** June 2025 "Independent AI Foundation Model" project runs as an elimination tournament: govt subsidizes compute/data/researchers, culls losers every 6 months, reallocates to winners. Started 15 consortiums → 5 (Naver, LG, SKT, NC, Upstage). ~$350M total budget (a rounding error vs US labs). Twist: the two tiny startups (Motif, Upstage) crushed the chaebols — **Motif 3** (sub-30-person startup, $17M raised, 768 B200s, ~$15M total train cost) is the **best non-Chinese open-source model in the world**, ahead of Inkling and Nemotron 3 Ultra. Yet Motif was baffling­ly *eliminated* (dead last on opaque "expert review"/"user testing" scores) — SemiAnalysis reads it as bad govt judgement; Motif may now leave Korea. Key takeaway: **training a near-SOTA open model from scratch is far cheaper than most think** — within budget for any nation-state.

**A trillion dollars of datacenters.** July 2026: $919B plan — 8.4GW by 2029, 18.4GW by 2035 (SK 5GW, GS 2.4GW, Naver 1GW in phase 1; SK the +10GW phase 2). SK committed 2GW of Rubin; Naver +200MW. Much capacity will be sold to Anthropic/OpenAI, but Korea keeps optionality to scale sovereign efforts.

**Why Nvidia needs open source & sovereign AI.** Jensen's open-source manifesto = "commoditize your complements." Selling frontier tokens at API prices is the highest-ROI use of compute, so Anthropic+OpenAI take a rising share of net-new GWs — leaving Nvidia on track for a world of **~2 real customers** (7 with hyperscalers, all of whom except SpaceX are building XPUs to cannibalize Nvidia margins). A vibrant, diverse ecosystem (open models, sovereign buyers, the $500B "AI Factory Compute as an asset class" MOU) is existential for Nvidia's TAM. Sovereign AI diversifies the customer base — expect more Jensen country-deals.

**The memory tension (the bear nuance).** Korea can finance land/power/buildings but **cannot manufacture a competitive accelerator ecosystem by spending more** — so it needs deep Nvidia alignment (roadmap visibility, allocation). This is why Nvidia's expanded Samsung/SK Hynix relationships matter: Samsung to build a >50k-GPU Nvidia AI-factory; SKT's 2GW DSX factory on Vera Rubin + SK Hynix HBM4. But SemiAnalysis believes **Nvidia has likely secured a long-term SOCAMM agreement plus favorable HBM pricing and large 2027 volume commitments from SK Hynix** — good for volumes, but "favorable pricing" is a margin give, and using the memory giants as national champions in a capex-heavy sovereign buildout may not maximize shareholder returns.
