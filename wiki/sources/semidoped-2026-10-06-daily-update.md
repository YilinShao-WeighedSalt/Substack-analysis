Tuesday daily, power-dominated. Headline: **Google locks up 3.6 GW of nuclear from Constellation** — the largest single corporate clean-energy deal on record, baseload across multiple plants into the next decade, valued in the billions (vs 690 MW Amazon-Constellation last week, so 5×; Oracle lining up Point Beach too). **Morgan Stanley now calls power availability — not silicon supply — the binding constraint** on AI expansion; argues Nvidia/Broadcom are relatively insulated while hyperscalers face the ceiling. Vik: it's a response to PJM's "bring your own power" proposal but still grid-reliant and long-dated; near-term BTM is gas turbines, not solid-oxide fuel cells (SOFC has raw-material availability issues). **Marvell raises FY28 guide to ~$20B and sees $70-90B revenue by FY31** (analyst day). **Tokyo Electron forecasts op profit >¥1T** on surging deposition/etch orders; Korea subsidiary opened triple-digit hiring (19 roles, Jan start) = service backlog into 2027 (Vik: WFE 🚀). **TDK weighs selling its HDD magnetic-head unit**; Seagate and Toshiba bidding (Vik: TDK is the only independent head supplier; if Seagate wins, Toshiba's sole source sits inside a rival).

Sector watch: **AMD CEO guides substantially higher 2027 chip supply** on "very high" demand; **MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro on 2nm in India** (first 2nm flagship, agentic AI, 2027 handsets); Teradyne Titan HP burn-in for high-power accelerators; **YMTC raises NAND prices + warns shortage persists to 2029** (most bearish supply timeline from any flash maker); **CXMT begins mass production of first DRAM platform** + recruits 27 Samsung/SK Hynix engineers; **Hanmi Semiconductor wins ₩24.5B SEMCO AI-substrate post-processing order**; **DISCO Q2 parent sales +39% to ¥118.5B** (AI-packaging dicing/grinding); SUSS MicroTec enters wafer cleaning (GreenTec/GT200); **SEMCO secures customer backing for $5B substrate expansion**; Magnachip Gen6 super-junction MOSFET; Infineon completes C2i (AI-DC power); Microchip+Navitas 800V-to-6V reference design; Infineon Thailand hub (~1,000 workers); MaxLinear Topaz PON; Nokia selected by Brazil's RNP; Applied Digital 1GW Finland; Oracle Point Beach nuclear offtake; DayOne Data Centers files US IPO.
