A more constructive follow-up after pushback on the prior night's note. IA separates "noise" (COGS synergies = accounting; Marvell 1.6T DSP "crap"; regulators will likely force a **Cloudlight spin-out** to keep customer trust) from signal. The signal: **this deal is about uVCSEL slow-and-wide scale-in, nothing else.** Argument: Lumentum has only uVCSEL manufacturing/packaging/fiber-attach and **zero electrical/analog capability**; Marvell has all the DSP/equalizer/SerDes/SiGe IP. Smaller VCSELs are far more sensitive to crosstalk, chirp, RIN and dispersion — and **uVCSEL over ~10m hits the same dispersion problems as ZR optics over ~100km**, which is exactly the C/O-band correction math Marvell already owns (COLORZ/ZR). So the combination is a genuinely complementary skillset to "speedrun" uVCSEL NPO/CPO; Hurlston likely couldn't acquire the electrical skillset fast enough. Also notes **NPO is standardized/pluggable** (OpenCPX MSA) — rebutting both CEOs' "NPO isn't standardized" framing — and that in CW-SiPho CPO Lumentum's ELSFP role needs no vertical integration. Net: the merger is maybe not completely stupid after all.
