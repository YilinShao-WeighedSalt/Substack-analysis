Packaging cracks first in a downturn and ASE says nobody has cancelled; Tesla's AI5 waits behind cloud CPUs for 3nm; Google may design the bottom of its own HBM5; TrendForce dates the first DRAM price declines; the Dutch plan to fight the MATCH Act.

- **ASE: no cancellations, doubles down on 2027.** At Citi Taiwan Corporate Day ASE reported no cancellations from the Nvidia turmoil, new US offshore-compute limits, or AI-financing worries; some China business delayed on export permits, capacity re-handed to others. Leading-edge advanced packaging + test to top $4B this year, ~double to **$8B in 2027** across several GPU/ASIC customers; full turnkey $300M→$1B; **CPO ~$500M in 2027**. 2027 capex above this year's ~$11.5B, 22 construction projects, factory space the tightest constraint. KYEC (tests Nvidia GPUs) sees strong AI-test demand, filed for a 2nd larger Singapore site. → **LONG [[ASX]]**. → [[advanced-packaging]].
- **Tesla AI5 waits behind Google & Microsoft for 3nm** — GUC (TSMC design-services affiliate) gives limited 3nm to Google Axion 2/3 server CPUs + Microsoft in-house chips ahead of Tesla AI5; large-scale AI5 in cars may wait to 2028; first AI5 goes to Optimus (cars stay on AI4). Cloud CPU orders carry higher margin/visibility → they get the slots. → **NEUT [[TSLA]]** (silicon queue a mild negative); read-through LONG Google (Axion priority). → [[custom-silicon-asic]].
- **Google wants a hand in its HBM5 base die** — pursuing custom HBM5 for its 10th-gen TPU; **MediaTek the leading candidate to design the HBM base die + SerDes** (~512GB HBM5). The base die is the logic chip at the bottom of the stack; if Google/MediaTek design it, Samsung/SK build DRAM stacks to someone else's spec (Nvidia took the same step in August). Samsung created a Logic Development Team inside memory for custom HBM. TPU v10 volume ~2029. → **LONG [[2454.TW]]**. → [[hbm-memory]].
- **TrendForce dates DRAM declines** — base case: conventional DRAM increases narrow through 2027, flatten 2H, turn to declines across all types from Q1 2028; bear case Q4 2027. Demand-side: Chinese cloud cut server-DRAM orders this quarter, phones/PCs can't absorb more, buyers wary of midterms/Nvidia-neocloud-financing/new compute limits; supply grows through 2028. A 2028 problem — today's shortage still severe (WSJ: procurement "war rooms," some prices up 10× in a year; Samsung's Lee told Musk everyone offers LTAs). → [[memory-dram-cycle]].
- **The Dutch plan to fight the MATCH Act** — Netherlands working with EU/Germany/France to stop the MATCH Act (may attach to the Dec defense bill), which would force allies to match US export controls or face unilateral US tool limits. For ASML it risks immersion DUV scanners + servicing of installed China tools (~20% of ASML revenue this year). → **NEUT [[ASML]]**. → [[geopolitics-export-controls]].
- Chatter: Goldman — 0.5-0.75GW of AI capacity tied to Chinese customers under the remote-access rule (~263-395k Blackwell-class GPUs needing licenses/new tenants/buyers in 90 days, ~$17-25B/yr rent to re-lease); BofA — DRAM op margins (now >80%) still 50-60% in 2028 even if prices fall 40-50% (HBM + LTAs); Yuanjie not supplying Eoptolink (CW-laser short), InnoLight locked ~80% of Sumitomo InP substrate, Yuanjie's high-power lasers mainly to Nvidia. Sector: TSMC 75% of pure-play foundry rev in Q3; Formosa Sumco 2027 12-inch wafers sold out, prices rising; Simmtech ₩300B perpetual bonds for SOCAMM + mSAP; Nomura raised 2027 AI-server sales, sees TSMC CoWoS +80%; **JERA in talks with Anthropic/Meta** to build DCs beside its Tokyo-area plants; Singapore working with Washington to keep secure chip access; Schneider doubling liquid-cooling CDU output in Italy; **TDK $635M to double TMR-sensor output** (robots + AI DCs) → **LONG [[6762.JP]]**.
