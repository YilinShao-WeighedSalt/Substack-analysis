# Daily Update — October 2nd, 2026

A news-dense daily (Austin & Vik). Headline: Broadcom's **$60B debt pile** for Anthropic chips; record DRAM/NAND prices; Snapdragon X2 pressuring Intel.

## Key items
- **Broadcom $60B debt for Anthropic chips.** Per Bloomberg/SEC filing: AVGO lends Anthropic up to **$42B to lease chips** (incl. Google TPUs flowing through AVGO's balance sheet) vs Anthropic convertible notes; Anthropic's 10-yr infra ambition = **$518B**; pre-IPO investor day Oct 14. *Vik: "circular financing in a bow… one of the horsemen that pops this buildout. But I don't bet against Hock, his ASIC book pays for all of it."* → largest single AI-infra financing disclosed. ([[ai-debt-financing]])
- **DRAM/NAND record highs + NVHBM margin claim.** TrendForce: 8Gb DDR4 contract **+4% MoM to $26**; NAND parallel move. Micron CTO DeBoer says **NVHBM improves profitability despite the base die being built externally** (TSMC). Record FY: **DRAM rev >$100B first time, op margin >70%**. *Vik: "Micron cope" — a Nvidia-designed, TSMC-built base die sends value to Nvidia via NVLink Fusion, not Micron.* ([[memory-dram-cycle]], [[hbm-memory]])
- **Snapdragon X2 pressures Intel.** X2 Elite/Plus benchmarks beat Core Ultra at comparable/lower prices (Snapdragon Summit, Maui); Linux upstream preview for Arm laptops; **Apple license renewal = near-term revenue lifeline** even as iPhone volumes soften; agentic-AI the theme. *Vik: edge devices may be the central AI interface, but doubts meaningful on-device LLMs.* ([[qualcomm-mobile-rffe]], [[arm-ecosystem]])
- **Amazon securitizes ~$8B Nvidia chips** (FT) — shifts GPU inventory off balance sheet; separately **"Built Together" $1B/5-yr** community fund + doom-blog warning towns against blocking data centers. *Vik: the fund and the blog are the same move — permitting is now a real brake (100+ moratoriums).* ([[ai-debt-financing]], [[ai-infrastructure-capex]])
- **Huawei: Ascend "passed Nvidia" in China** (Ken Hu, unquantified) + **DeepSeek+Huawei open-source** compute/comm libraries + TileLang for Ascend = credible CUDA-alternative without Nvidia's toolchain; **Kirin 9050 Pro LogicFolding** (vertical transistor stacking, sidesteps node ceiling). *Vik: Nvidia China share already low, but the ecosystem hardens.* ([[geopolitics-export-controls]])
- **Core Scientific terminates its $9B merger with CoreWeave** — "kind of a big deal." ([[CORZ]], [[CRWV]])
- **Ciena + CtrlS**: 100 Tb/s metro optical across five Indian cities. ([[CIEN]])
- **ASML**: China stockpiling hundreds of immersion-DUV tools worth billions → louder US calls for a complete tool export ban. ([[ASML]])
- Smaller: Tavus "Griffin" video-AI (48% Turing pass); Nvidia DGX Spark 64GB; Crusoe $4.8B Jayton TX campus permit; $300M Nvidia-server smuggling arrest.
