Building-level census of China's datacenter fleet (1,000+ facilities, 60+ operators) — the China counterpart to the flagship SemiAnalysis Datacenter Model. **Thesis-level piece, no priced equity call.**

**Headline numbers:** China alone is **>24GW** of live capacity (bigger than EMEA at ~14GW, bigger than APAC-ex-China ~15GW; US leads at 56GW), excluding ~20GW dated pipeline + ~30GW announced. 2Q26 combined BAT (Alibaba/Tencent/Baidu) capex hit $20B (>2× YoY, all three FCF-negative for the first time). BATB capex trajectory: ~$35B (2024) → >$50B (2025) → ~$100B (2026). **ByteDance** (private) alone occupies ~1/5 of delivered capacity and rents nearly all of it — the single most important wholesale-colo customer in the country; listed landlords GDS + VNET capture only ~1/3 of ByteDance+Alibaba orders.

**Four-era history:** (1) carrier hosting pre-2015 (telecom real-estate, low-density retail racks — now the vacant legacy stock); (2) cloud land-grab 2015-21 (Alibaba ~110% CAGR, brutal price war); (3) digestion 2022-23 (music stops, power rates halved from ~$80/kW/mo); (4) AI supercycle 2024- (wholesale returns at record scale). Explains the paradox of ~50% headline vacancy *alongside* an AI-capacity shortage — the vacant stock is physically unsuitable for H20/Ascend densities.

**Eastern Data, Western Compute (EDWC):** the binding mechanism is the **energy-consumption quota** filed with the local DRC (>10MW needs review). Quotas stopped construction flowing to non-hub locations (Guangdong froze quotas 2021-22, reopened only for the Shaoguan hub; Tencent's Qingyuan campuses found running 13-14× approved scale, ordered to rectify). **Inner Mongolia = China's Johor** (own Mengxi grid, power ~½ Tier-1 price). Shaoguan fiber investment (~$600M, 400G routes, 1.3ms to Guangzhou) makes a western rack functionally a Tier-1 rack.

**Speed & cost — a different sport:** US is power-gated (interconnect queues, transformer lead times); **China is chip-gated** (power abundant, permitting a policy funnel not a bottleneck, labor plentiful). 100MW facility delivery compressed from ~18mo (cloud era) to ~12mo; permitting 3-6mo (vs US 12-13mo that can't overlap construction). Alibaba's CUBE 5.0 "100-day datacenter" (30d factory + 50d install + 20d commissioning); Tencent T-Block full-modular. Full-modular container campuses are NOT the national standard because (a) Chinese site labor is cheap/abundant so the marginal speed gain is small, and (b) colo developers pledge real buildings to banks as collateral (a yard of containers appraises poorly). Capex a fraction of comparable US wholesale. Reinforces **[[modular-datacenter-construction]]** (China does the "LEGO datacenter" playbook at scale the West is only now adopting).
