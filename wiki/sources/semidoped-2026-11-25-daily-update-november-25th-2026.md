The AI trade hands back Monday's Jensen rally, but Dell adds $10B to its year; the memory squeeze reaches PCs and phones; AOI signs a second CPO-laser hyperscaler; Korea's memory giants license YMTC's wafer-bonding IP; Canon returns to ArF lithography.

- **Dell raises FY27 to $202B (from $192B); 2027 "tighter still."** FQ3 rev ~doubled to $54.9B; $60.2B AI orders booked, record $132.3B AI backlog; AI-server outlook lifted to $78B. COO Clarke: no slowdown, demand further ahead of supply in CY27 than CY26, every order vetted for capital/land/power. Infrastructure rev nearly tripled; PC +3%. Board added **$25B** to buyback. → **LONG [[DELL]]** (first SD priced DELL call; stance mixed→long).
- **The memory bill reaches consumers.** HP FQ4 PC rev +14% but units −29%, segment op margin 3%; **Ambarella** guides to $70-75M (from $115M) as the memory spike "intensified," customers cutting outlooks → **NEUT [[AMBA]]** (new; margin squeeze, SD doesn't short it). Xiaomi cut 2027 phone target to ~90M from ~120M (Samsung/Apple/Oppo/Vivo trimming). **TrendForce cut 2027 DRAM & NAND revenue** after Chinese cloud trimmed memory orders; sees NAND prices falling in 2027, DRAM still tight but increases narrowing/flattening 2H. AI servers take the memory; consumer devices pay in lost units. → [[memory-dram-cycle]].
- **AOI signs a second hyperscaler** — 4-yr deal with an existing Tier-1: ~$1.5B of 800G transceivers in 2027 + ~$0.7B laser diodes 2028-29, some for **co-packaged optics from 2028** (a CPO light-source deal as much as transceivers); US-made product from Q1-27; CFO sees >$10B demand over the term (beyond the $2.2B floor depends on added capacity/commitments; customer put up no capital). → **LONG [[AAOI]]**.
- **Samsung & SK hynix license YMTC's Xtacking wafer-bonding** (Nikkei, 3 sources) — build NAND array + control circuits on separate wafers then bond; the direction for high-layer NAND. Executives say the Koreans signed "to avoid legal disputes" — YMTC's >12,000-patent portfolio now hard to design around; SK hynix is the new name (Samsung's hybrid-bonding deal reported early 2025). Top-two memory makers licensing from a US-Entity-List Chinese rival shows how fast 3D-NAND IP shifted. → [[nand-flash-storage]].
- **Canon returns to ArF lithography after 24 years** — FPA-6300AS6 dry ArF scanner (0.93 NA lens on its KrF platform, ~65nm), orders early December; targets memory/power/sensor chips. Nikkei: the opening is ASML's supply crunch (ASML CFO said in Oct it can't fully meet demand). Not leading-edge, but a second source for mature layers while ASML's lines are full. → [[semiconductor-equipment]].
- Sector: **ADI** record FQ4 +42% to $4.37B led by data center, guides flat → **LONG [[ADI]]**; **AIXTRON** repeat Nokia orders for G10-AsP InP photonics tools (deliveries thru 2027) → **LONG [[AIXA]]**; ams OSRAM — Samsung Display €210M to become largest holder, co-develop microLEDs; Avicena $100M Series C (laser-free microLED interconnect, first product 2027); Nscale can stay private as the selloff clouds its $35B IPO; Fluidstack "Blackbird" factory-built DC modules to 400MW in 6 months; Huawei convicted on 10/12 counts (sanctions + trade-secret conspiracy). Chatter — B300 lead times 26-40wk / GB300 14-26wk; Terafab recruiting memory engineers (Musk may want own memory); Rubin ~160B transistors/die (2 dies/package). ISSCC Feb: Samsung 48GB HBM4E @17Gb/s, Huawei custom HBM w/ built-in controller, CXMT vertical-gate DDR5.
