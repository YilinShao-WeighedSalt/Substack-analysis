**Field report (body truncated at paywall — ~2,640-word free preview).** SemiAnalysis toured **Galaxy**, an 800MW-IT campus in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia — "China's West Texas," the fastest-growing AI DC region (25GW announced, no Texas-style backlash). First **120MW phase delivered in <12 months at ~1/5 US cost**; ~30k construction workers across 22 projects in the Chahar zone. Owned by **Envision Group** (>100GW wind installed), one of the most vertically integrated DC players seen: builds its own wind farms + dedicated power line, a blade factory across the road, designs/integrates the low-voltage modules and **800V DC system**, makes the battery storage (affiliate AESC, cells→power-conversion→software), and co-designs the prefab substation (<3 mo vs ~6).

**Key mechanisms:**
- **SGLS (source-grid-load-storage):** build own wind/solar + battery + private line instead of public grid; fixed long-term power + green credit. Inner Mongolia = ~57% of China's usable wind; Ulanqab capacity factor 35–40%. Envision targets **>60% of first two phases' load from own wind** via windy siting (two farms 80km apart), oversizing (420MW wind vs 240MW load, 1.75×), and 4-hr battery shifting.
- **800V DC / SST:** solid-state transformers take 10kV AC → 800V DC straight to rack, removing LV transformers/UPS/rectifier stages. Same architecture Nvidia pushes for 600kW+ Kyber racks; US is early (97% of US DCs still AC UPS vs ~25% of Chinese DCs on DC over the past decade). Galaxy's live 5MW SST pilot already runs 800V to the rack at a hyperscaler site.
- **Cooling:** liquid + air share one water loop, no central chiller (Vera Rubin can run on 45°C water), common in northern China.

**Comparison to Abilene (Stargate):** both energy-rooted, planned around own power; Galaxy ≈ Abilene's ~820MW core, could reach ~1GW if the adjacent turbine factory converts. China DC economics brutal: rents ~halved in 5 yrs to ~$40/kW/mo (ex-power), capex −25% to <$3mm/MW, Inner Mongolia power ~$0.055/kWh (among world's lowest) — vertical integration (BYD-style) a way to differentiate. Parallel path: **VNET–CATL–Zhongheng Electric** tie-up (CATL became VNET's largest shareholder + 49% of Zhongheng's controlling holder; CATL also a top DeepSeek investor), covering DC + green power/storage + in-hall HVDC.
