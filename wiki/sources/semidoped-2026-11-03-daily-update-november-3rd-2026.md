**Anthropic's amended S-1**: **$895.4B total cloud/data-center commitments** vs $33.2B 9-mo revenue; Q3 rev ~$17B but swung to a **$567M operating loss** as short-term compute hit $5B (the filing says short-term compute costs far more than multi-year capacity — why long-term commitments keep climbing). Growth decelerated (Reuters: financing risk). **DRAM spot at an all-time high** — 8Gb DDR4 +5.77% to $27.50 in October — while NAND flatlined after 21 months; Apple flagged a holiday-quarter margin hit from memory; Shin-Etsu raising wafer prices (reprice late-2027); **SK Hynix says the DRAM shortage runs through 2030**; TechInsights: memory = 62% of semi revenue by 2027. **Qualcomm validated first-gen HBC silicon** (compute in the base die under an LPDDR stack; claims 6x bandwidth/watt vs HBM; ships in Dragonfly AI250 mid-2027). **Infineon** put its AURIX safety subsystem on Qualcomm **Snapdragon Ride**. **Lattice** record 3Q26 (+118% YoY); **Tower** record $526M (+33%), guides Q4 $600M → $2B run-rate. Sector: Ibiden record H1; onsemi AI-DC +175% + buys Ferric; GE Vernova backlog $197B; Lumentum 2nd NPO/CPO win (>$5B commitments); TrendForce 2027 pluggables +76% to $84.1B; TD Cowen downgrades Marvell.
