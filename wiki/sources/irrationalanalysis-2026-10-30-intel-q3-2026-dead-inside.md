# Intel Q3 2026: Dead Inside

A short, furious live-reaction memo written during Intel's Q3 call (author stepped out of a
meeting with Avicena to watch the stock implode). Core charge: Intel's "we're limited by
substrates / EMIB" excuse is **bullshit** — they are obviously limited by **leading-edge logic
wafers** due to **planning incompetence**, the same ~6-month client→datacenter wafer-pivot
excuse given two or three quarters ago.

Key arguments:
- **Datacenter ran AHEAD of expectations; client ran BELOW.** A once-in-a-lifetime datacenter
  CPU shortage, and Intel still couldn't supply it — a basic business-planning failure, not a
  parts shortage.
- **The yield-harvest tell:** Granite Rapids has a massive SKU stack that yield-harvests every
  wafer. If Intel were truly substrate-limited it would hold partially-defective dies and ship
  only the top-ASP SKUs. That it can't implies a **logic-wafer** shortfall. "If they say there
  are inventory issues with client, that gives away what's really going on."
- **Why 1H2027 is worse than 2H2027:** because leading-edge wafers take ~6 months start-to-finish;
  substrates don't. The timeline itself fingers wafers, not substrates.
- Praises analyst **Stacy** (likely Rasgon) for boxing management into a corner on the wafer-vs-substrate
  question; **Vivek** went "in for the kill."
- Side notes: author is **fully de-levered** and listed **Intel as a liquidation target**; laid off
  (potentially all of) Intel's **quantum process-node group** — now at Classiq.
- **Livid at Marvell trying to buy Lumentum** ("my beloved Lumentum") — vows to "do everything
  possible to kill this deal"; a fuller take promised later.
