**"Black November."** Nasdaq −5.5% (worst day since Apr-2025), into correction (~12% below the Oct-6 record); **SOX −12%** to a 6-month low; NVDA −9%, AMD −17%, SanDisk −22%; Nasdaq-100 shed ~$2.5T. Trigger was political: **Democrats swept Congress and flipped Texas and other datacenter-state governorships** → traders priced permitting/power/local-opposition slowing buildouts (UBS: ~30% of 2028 US capacity additions at risk); neocloud financing costs up, **CoreWeave 5-yr CDS at a record 1,267.9bps** (Intel's hit 149.7). Asia took the second leg: **KOSPI halted 20 min** then −6.02% (SK Hynix −10.4%, Samsung −6.35%), Advantest −12.2%, SoftBank −11.6%; **TSMC opened limit-down** then closed −4.69%; **Taiwan armed its National Stabilization Fund** (10th time, up to NT$500B) as foreigners dumped a record. But **orders ran opposite to fear**: **ASE +$3.7B** MOEA-approved packaging expansion (+ Nvidia reportedly funding SPIL CoWoS); advanced packaging to pass traditional in 2026 (~$28B capex). **AOI** record Q3 $269.9M, back to GAAP profit, **1.6T Tier-1 deal $1-1.5B** + a 5x-larger deal "within weeks" (caveats: locked pricing, slow US ramp). **NTT Data 900MW Bloom** fuel-cell deal. TrendForce 2027: AI servers +36%, cloud capex +60% to $1.52T (prepared before the shock). Chatter: Anthropic run-rate >$80B but IPO investors push back on $1.5T; KYEC/Rubin HBM4-yield delay rumor; JNTC glass-substrate TGV to Nvidia; Lam authorizes another $25B buyback.
