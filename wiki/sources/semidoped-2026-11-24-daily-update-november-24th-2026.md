Jensen is back and the market hands Nvidia nearly $1T in one session; the memory peak debate gets a bank's numbers.

- **Nvidia reinstates Huang; the investigation stays.** Board voted unanimously Monday to reinstate Huang (president/CEO/director), <1 week after saying it wouldn't, after "input from senior levels of the US government" (Trump pushed publicly). Added a National Security & Export Control Committee + searching for a Chief Compliance Officer; the foreign-intel probe continues; the **SEC accounting inquiry + circular-financing questions stay open**; Congress testimony next week; California AG still probing. NVDA **+31.5% to $165.05** (biggest day since 2003), SOX +14%, TSMC/Foxconn limit-up in Taipei. → **NEUTRAL [[NVDA]]** (reinstatement euphoria vs open investigations; whipsaw).
- **Commerce blacklists Megaspeed + Alibaba's Singapore cloud unit** (7 entities MY/SG/TH, presumption-of-denial). Enforcement moving to the resellers/hosts, not just chip shipments; Malaysia (>half of SE-Asia DC capacity under construction) told operators not to serve the listed firms. → [[geopolitics-export-controls]].
- **TSMC files for a $22.6B 2nm Texas campus** (Brazos County, >3,200 acres, 2nm+, construction 2027-31), Arizona the fallback; property-tax terms "determinative", not committed (land under contract, agreements non-binding). → **LONG [[TSM]]** (also limit-up on Jensen; Cliff Hou: no client order cancellations).
- **HPE: record AI orders, memory shortage through 2028.** FQ4 rev +50% to $14.5B, server rev ~doubled; AI orders $4.5B (+130%), AI rev $2.5B; raised FY27 growth to 27-35%; Neri sees memory/NAND constraints through ≥2H28, no change in customer behavior. The catch: Cloud & AI op margin guided to ~14% (from 18.4%) as low-margin AI servers mix up; shares slid. First earnings check on the server-repricing report. → **NEUTRAL [[HPE]]** (new). Dell reports Tuesday.
- **Goldman lifts 2027 HBM to $200B** (from $60B this yr; $237B '28; custom chips 44% of HBM by '28) **but cuts Samsung & SK hynix PTs by up to 43%** — HBM growth won't offset the profit hit when conventional DRAM/NAND roll over in 2028; tight through '27, easing after. → **NEUTRAL [[005930.KS]] / [[000660.KS]]** (peak debate; the Nov-23 "no cuts/sold-out-'27" bull vs this bear).
- Sector: **Semtech** record FQ3 $423.0M (+58%), guides FQ4 ~$480M → **LONG [[SMTC]]**; **Amazon** buys Decart's chip-opt software/team (~$7.5B) ahead of selling Trainium outside AWS → **LONG [[AMZN]]**; SemiAnalysis previews **AMD MI550x** multi-rack Ethernet scale-up; Fujitsu 2nm Monaka demand beats; Arm's Haas signs to 2033 + SoftBank vice-chair; Korean substrate makers announce ₩2.8T expansion as shortage spreads to **SOCAMM2**; Applied Digital signs 18-yr 150MW ~$5.4B hyperscaler lease; chatter — AMD Venice 6M '27 target "too high" (2.5D packaging the bottleneck).
