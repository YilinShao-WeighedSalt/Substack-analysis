# Semi Doped — Daily Update, September 7th 2026

News-dense daily. Lead items: AMD's Threadripper Halo Station, Kioxia's flash-for-DRAM
CXL module, and Anthropic's $517B compute disclosure ahead of a mid-October IPO.

## Key items

- **AMD Threadripper Halo Station** (IFA keynote): 96-core Threadripper Pro 9995WX + 2-4
  liquid-cooled Instinct MI350P (144 GB HBM3E each; 576 GB in a 4-card build) — a direct
  answer to Nvidia's DGX Station for local AI development, built from off-the-shelf parts.
- **Kioxia XL1 CXL module**: first flash-for-DRAM product from a major NAND maker aimed
  at *production* AI workloads — a three-tier architecture placing NAND-backed CXL
  capacity *beneath* DRAM. Confirmed ties to **Nvidia's CMX platform**; an Apple NAND LTA
  (TrendForce) gives volume backstop. Synopsys updated CXL 4.0 IP in parallel.
- **Anthropic discloses $517B in compute commitments** across nine corridors in 11
  months (>$100B to AWS; FluidStack leading a $50B slice; Nvidia as supplier + "landlord"
  on corridor nine); revolver upsized to $15B; **IPO tracking mid-October**.
- **Foxconn record August**: $29.14B sales (+52% YoY), all-time monthly record on Nvidia
  AI-server assembly; Q3 outlook raised above consensus; Europe now ordering too.
- **TSMC 3nm nears NT$400B** H2 output — set to overtake 5nm as its single largest
  revenue node (a first for any sub-5nm process); >70% advanced-node share; fabs tight.
- **Nscale** seeks $3.5B pre-IPO (Nvidia-backed) after a reported $45B contract.
- **Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6** (Sep 22) with side-mounted stacked DRAM + higher
  prices; Galaxy S27 design win.
- **Bloom Energy** confirmed for **S&P 500** (Sep 21 open); Q2 rev +166% YoY; Oracle DC
  fuel-cell project in New Mexico.
- Power: **Infineon** dual-phase smart power stages (2 A/mm² benchmark) for AI
  accelerators; **GE Vernova** medium-voltage UPS for AI datacenters.
- Sector watch: Wistron $1.5B GDS; TCS $7.4B 1 GW Hyderabad campus; Cerebras CS-4 (750
  PFLOPs, 30× predecessor); SKC/Absolics ₩281B glass-substrate; Belgium chip-espionage
  arrest; White House "Foundry School" w/ ASU; Google AI-chip design center in Israel.
