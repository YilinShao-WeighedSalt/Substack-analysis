**ASML enters hybrid bonding (echoes [[semianalysis-2026-10-15-asml-hybrid-bonding]]):** per SemiAnalysis, ASML is developing a hybrid bonding tool — a direct move onto **Besi's** core turf. Besi had operated this high-growth equipment market without a credible threat from the litho giants; that changes if ASML qualifies customers. ASML arrives with an installed-base relationship at every major fab. *Vik:* this is a **logic play first**, not the HBM wave the framing suggests — SA says D2W, and D2W only runs in volume in logic (TSMC/Intel), exactly where Besi sits; HBM-grade bonding is still too slow/expensive at high stacks. *Austin:* Besi isn't alone — **Applied owns 9% of Besi** and the two launched **Kinex** (integrated D2W bonder) together last October, so ASML is lining up against Applied too.

**CoreWeave default odds >60%:** CDS now imply a **better-than-60% chance of default** (all-time high) as high-yield bonds slide; >$10B market value lost across four sessions (Deutsche Bank called it a buying opportunity). Jensen Huang publicly said CoreWeave is improving and needs **no Nvidia backstop** — steadied the stock, not the bonds. Core anxiety is structural: a loss-making neocloud financing GPUs almost entirely against hyperscaler contracts. *Austin:* Nvidia already signed a $6.3B backstop (buy unused capacity through Apr-2032) in Sept 2025 — read this as no NEW backstop needed.

**TSMC:** confirmed on its Q3 call that **Arizona Fab 2 construction is complete** (moving to tool install/ramp); evaluating Texas as a second US site, with active **Terafab** discussions ("won't operate any fab it doesn't control"). Brokerages now project **2028 capex up to $105B** (UBS, most bullish; ~1.7× this year's ~$62B). Q profit +68.7%.

**Schneider Electric:** lifted **2026 targets for the second time** this cycle as data-center demand drove sales well above expectations; order visibility now into 2027-2028. *Vik:* power and cooling are the binding constraint now — triple-digit DC demand in a single quarter, record backlog. Shares slipped 1.53% (good news already priced).

**SK Hynix at OCP:** presented **3D FeNAND analog compute-in-memory** — runs AI multiply-accumulate inside NAND flash via ferroelectric material, cutting the round-trip to DRAM/HBM; framed memory-centric servers as the organizing principle for AI infra.

**Sector watch:** Volvo deepens Nvidia Drive reliance; **Arm rack-level CPU benchmark claims 2× AMD** per rack in agentic inference; Li Auto raises equity for in-house auto silicon; **Japan offers GlobalFoundries up to $21B** for US expansion; LG Innotek targets 2027 substrate growth; Unimicron halts Yangmei after a fatal accident; **CXMT targets DDR5 RDIMM by end-2026** (+ SiCarrier equipment); Samsung flags limits of AI-memory standardization at JEDEC; **Kioxia sheds 2/3 of peak market value** as Chinese NAND rivals close in (YMTC #3); SEMCO on track to ~double Q3 op profit on MLCC; Lite-On $1.11B sustainability loan (140% oversubscribed); **Infineon+Wolfspeed extend SiC supply to 200mm**; Venture Global signs GE Vernova + Baker Hughes gas plants; Delta standalone microgrid; **Nvidia next-gen CPO on the interposer**; KT 800Gbps AI fiber; Ericsson/Verizon first 6G outdoor call (7GHz); **Taiwan agrees to curb AI-chip exports to China**; China proposes export controls on advanced AI models/chips/agents; Qualcomm-Transsion patent cross-license (Africa/EM); **Nscale $35B IPO** but Arctic DC 6mo late (fires, labor disputes); OpenAI names Uday Ruddarraju (ex-xAI) head of infra.
