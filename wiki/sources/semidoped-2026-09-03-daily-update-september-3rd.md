# Semi Doped Daily — September 3, 2026

News-dense daily. Lead items: Nvidia confirms $12.93B Hugging Face acquisition, Broadcom's bullish two-year AI-chip forecast (>$30 EPS by FY28) clouded by Google defection fears, TSMC holds at microbumps for HBM (defers hybrid bonding), Samsung pushes vertical 3D-stacked HBM, Phison warns 2027 NAND shortage worst ever.

- **Nvidia acquires Hugging Face for $12.93B** (down from the $14B earlier cited) + up to $1B equity retention. Jensen frames it as scaling HF's infra + expanding developer access. *Austin: buying access to developers while ensuring open-model distribution — Microsoft/GitHub vibes. Vik: open models feed compute demand → Nvidia; a good move to foster the ecosystem.* Subject to regulatory review. → [[nvidia-gpu-platform]].
- **Broadcom's bullish AI-chip forecast clouded by Google defection fear.** Guides to top **$30/share EPS by FY2028** (ahead of Street; BI's Bloxham), forecasting ~$115B of chips in FY27 and ~$230B FY28. Stock fell anyway — Google's shift toward fully in-house TPU (4× rollout acceleration, partly via MediaTek) raises single-hyperscaler concentration risk. *Austin: Hock says XPUs cost <½/GW of an Nvidia GPU and are more performant for their target LLM workloads; second-sourcing/CoT are price negotiations, nobody walks away from Broadcom. Vik: never bet against Hock.* → [[custom-silicon-asic]].
- **TSMC holds at microbumps for HBM, defers hybrid-bonding capex.** Sits at 6µm hybrid-bonding pitch but won't commit capital to hybrid bonding for HBM near-term — sticking with microbumps while yield matures. Hands Samsung/SK Hynix a window to push hybrid bonding into HBM roadmaps first. Intel EMIB-T gaining traction as a CoWoS alternative puts TSMC packaging revenue under scrutiny. *Vik: bearish hybrid bonding for HBM — high stacks too expensive; bandwidth matters more than capacity.* → [[advanced-packaging]].
- **Samsung pushes HBM into vertical territory (zHBM / 3D-stacked).** Architecture that vertically stacks memory + compute to raise capacity in the same footprint while cutting data-transfer distance; targets planar-scaling limits. No named product/date — a public staking of ground vs SK Hynix's advanced-HBM lead. *Vik: like the zHBM idea (DRAM-on-logic is logical) but thermally hard; Samsung says HBM5 uses hybrid bonding — "going to be a while."* → [[hbm-memory]].
- **Phison warns 2027 NAND shortage worst ever; SK courts Kioxia.** Phison CEO tells Bloomberg 2027 will bring the worst NAND shortage yet. Chey (SK) calls joint production with Kioxia "one option," decision on an overseas (Japan) plant possibly this year. Kioxia+SanDisk (~$31B) doesn't resolve the timeline. *Vik: the Phison CEO has said this for a while; bearish messaging from a controller maker — wait and see.* → [[nand-flash-storage]].
- **Sector Watch:** Dell lifts annual sales forecast +$25B (5th straight quarter); ByteDance $29.6B loan (Inner Mongolia DC); Fluidstack $18B valuation (Google/Anthropic compute); Simmtech ₩400B substrate expansion; Hanmi 2.5D packaging gear; Onto Innovation Dragonfly G5 metrology; Hitachi + Bloom Energy fuel cells (Japan DC); LS Cable superconductor DC power; Qualcomm Dragonwing Q-2390 (infra beyond mobile); RISC-V RVA23 named Hot Chips standard; Nokia Network-as-Code; Korea sets ₩9.4T 2027 AI budget (~half compute).
