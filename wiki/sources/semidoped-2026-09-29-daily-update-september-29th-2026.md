# Semi Doped — Daily Update, September 29th 2026

Marquee: **Anthropic's S-1**. Also AMD's largest-ever acquisition.

## Summary

- **Anthropic files S-1:** $4.6B revenue, $8.1B operating loss, **$518B in non-cancellable compute commitments**, targeting ~$2T valuation; spent $7.3B on compute; warns AI may pose "existential risks"; founder-LLC control structure. *Vik: Anthropic takes all the duration risk — 7–10yr non-cancellable cloud contracts while its own customers are bound to nothing; the clouds get paid either way.* → [[ai-debt-financing]].
- **AMD buys Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B all-stock** (largest-ever AMD deal) — spatial-intelligence models/team fold into AMD AI systems; Li joins as chief scientist under Lisa Su. *Vik: talent + model-layer grab, but robotics hasn't had its ChatGPT moment — $8.2B is a long-dated bet.* → [[AMD]], [[robotics-embodied-ai]].
- **Samsung Electro-Mechanics:** ₩290B MLCC/inductor AI-server supply deal + **₩6.78T (~$5B) committed to package-substrate capacity** (Korea + Vietnam). *Vik: 5th MLCC LTA this year, cumulative ₩3.6T — the bottleneck trade sitting on single passive parts.* → [[009150.KS]].
- **Synopsys/Cadence/Siemens all certify AI design flows on TSMC nodes** (chiplets, 3D-IC); **Synopsys debuts AgentEngineer** (autonomous chip design, GA end-2026). Cadence CEO Devgan defends domain depth vs general-purpose models. *Vik: they call these agents L5 but human approval checkpoints remain — watch whether they name a production customer.* → [[SNPS]], [[CDNS]], [[TSM]], [[eda-chip-design]].
- **AT&T commits $3B to Corning** in a multi-year fiber supply pact (makes AT&T one of Corning's largest fiber customers). *Vik: this is FTTH access fiber (60M Americans by 2030), not the DC-interconnect story — but a binding commitment says fiber supply is tight.* → [[GLW]].
- **Meta brings Muse personal AI to small businesses.** → [[META]].

### Sector watch (call-relevant)
- **Memory & test:** Teradyne launches Magnum E2 HBM test system; Samsung projects **HBM ~30% of industry DRAM capacity by 2027**; **Netlist files a new ITC action vs Micron over HBM patents** → [[MU]].
- **Foundry:** VSMC (TSMC-NXP JV) opens its first Singapore 300mm fab, already sold out; SMIC completes $6.1B SMIC North buyout; **Qualcomm confirms working with Samsung Foundry on 2nm** → [[QCOM]].
- **Compute/edge:** Nvidia launches Open Agent Safety Platform + OpenShell 0.1.0; Korea launches SNU AI-chip lab with LG + FuriosaAI; **Gimlet Cloud + Cerebras to deploy 100MW of wafer-scale inference** (CS-4 launch partner) → [[CBRS]].
- **Power:** Schneider + AMD fast-deploy power/cooling for Helios racks; Microchip 48V power-monitor chips; Navitas picked by US gov for 10kV SiC.
- **Packaging/equip:** Tata + Besi (India packaging); Resonac 4×-yield larger wafers; ISC ₩100B test-socket expansion.
- **Optics:** **Jabil raises FY26 to $35B, AI rev $13.6B**; CableLabs 25-Gig DOCSIS 4.0; Mubadala + Together AI open Abu Dhabi office.
- **Policy:** Jensen & Lisa Su join Tsinghua advisory board; **CXMT plans $5.2B DRAM expansion** leaning on domestic Chinese toolmakers.
