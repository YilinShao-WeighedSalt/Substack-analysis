**Nvidia governance shock.** On Sunday Nvidia's board said a committee of independent directors + outside counsel will investigate reports that **Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign (Chinese) intelligence effort**, following NYT reporting (Oct-29) that Alice Huang — a former exec at Singapore's **Megaspeed** (an Nvidia cloud partner under US investigation for routing restricted chips to China) — was concluded by US authorities to be a Chinese intelligence asset. Huang emailed staff admitting an extramarital affair, will cooperate; board says no reason to believe US national security or Nvidia secrets compromised, retains confidence in Huang. Trump said Huang told him he had "no idea she was a spy." The real risk is Washington (a CEO lobbying for looser China rules now tied to a suspected asset; House Republicans sought a briefing) and **no clear CEO successor**. Markets split: **NVDA −4.3% (−~$230B, worst in Dow)** while Nasdaq +2.7%, Dow +2.2%; **TSMC +4.68%, Taiex +4.94%** (2nd-largest point gain, Taiwan market-support pledge). **FQ3 earnings Nov 17.** Vik declined to comment on the personal angle; "nothing changed overnight" on the hardware.

**Mitsui Chemicals buys JSR for ¥1.84T (~$11.6B)** from JIC Capital — **Japan's largest-ever chemicals deal**. Pays for JSR's **EUV-photoresist** position: #2 worldwide and the only supplier of both chemically-amplified and metal-oxide EUV resist (semi-materials sales ¥196.0B last FY). Mitsui's own chip-materials book is much smaller (EUV pellicles, ICROS tape, one film). DBJ puts in ¥200B preferred; ¥1.64T bridge loan + up to ¥150B new shares; closes Oct-2027–Mar-2028. Targets +¥40B annual profit by FY2035; weighing a petrochem/mobility spinout.

**TSMC says it stays a logic foundry.** Responding to The Information (TSMC drafting plans to make **3D DRAM at Nvidia's request**, led by Hsu Kuo-chin, ex-Micron Taiwan): "TSMC focuses on our core business... dedicated IC foundry." Didn't confirm Nvidia asked. Winbond chair: process/patent barriers make direct DRAM entry hard. 3D DRAM is years from volume at Samsung/SK/Micron, so wouldn't ease today's shortage; a DRAM arm would also complicate TSMC's HBM base-die role. Signal: how hard Nvidia is pushing for memory supply.

**Terafab (Musk) wants ~1M wafers/mo from 2032** (700k memory / 300k logic ≈ Samsung's entire DRAM output today; ~40× Rapidus). Procurement team pressed Japanese toolmakers for delivery "within months," mass production 2H28. Jiji: **Intel coordinating 2nm/1.4nm logic support**; Terafab weighing a Taiwanese memory partner (none confirmed; no DRAM process source named). Vik skeptical.

**Microsoft hands Oracle back older GPUs** — winding down a 2023 AI-compute rental deal (older Nvidia GPUs that ran Bing AI), not renewing; part of a review of >$80B of outside capacity (CoreWeave, Nscale, Nebius). Oracle: demand "remains strong, including earlier-generation GPUs." IREN also delayed deliveries after MSFT asked for network changes. Nebius reports Nov-12, CoreWeave this week. Rented GPU economics assume chips keep earning for years — a top renter walking away is a tell.

**Industry chatter (unconfirmed):** neocloud — only **30–40% of first-phase MOUs from newer GPU-rental customers convert to binding POs**; hyperscalers (own cash) convert very high. **Vera Rubin volume ramp slips Dec → Q1 2027**, ~5–6k Rubin racks in 2027 at ~$8M each (WK channel check). **Samsung expected to move wafer capacity HBM → conventional DRAM in Q4** as DRAM margins nearly catch HBM → **memory prices level in Q1 2027, ease in Q2**.

**Sector watch:** Rebellions R200 NPUs + NVLink Fusion/MGX with SK Telecom (prefill on Nvidia GPU, decode on NPU, 2028); Groq pitching Series B at $8B pre (2.5× Aug); **Quanta Oct rev doubled to record NT$353.25B**; **Wiwynn record Q3 NT$434.6B (+62.9%), profit +51.6%**; **Kokusai Electric fiscal-Q2 adj op profit +56%**, guidance held; SMEE showed mature-node KrF/i-line (>90% domestic parts, no EUV); **Samsung Taylor TX trial runs mid-late Dec** (Tesla AI6, 2027 MP); TSMC Tainan 3nm into Southern Taiwan Science Park (NT$30B land).
