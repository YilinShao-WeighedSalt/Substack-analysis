# Semi Doped Daily — Sep 17, 2026

## Highlights
- **Huawei pulls Ascend 960DT forward 9 months to Q1 2027** (2× perf over predecessor;
  demand already outstrips supply). Bigger: the **Peerium/UnifiedBus** interconnect claims
  to bind **up to 1M processors** into one logical computer (nested parallelism, unified
  memory addressing, P2P fabric). Debuts **Hi-ONE near-packaged optics (NPO)**: 7.2 Tbit/s
  per engine, "industry's first NPO ready for mass production" with a **built-in light
  source**. (→ [[silicon-photonics-interconnects]], competitive read on Nvidia/China.)
- **Generac +33% premarket** on a **$2.4B (Reuters) / $8B (Bloomberg)** multi-year backup-
  generator supply pact with **Amazon** — hyperscalers moving to committed, multi-year firm
  power procurement. Google/Nvidia/Emerald found the **AI Energy Management Alliance**
  (grid demand-response). (→ [[datacenter-power]].)
- **SK Hynix** lays out HBM4 + CXL roadmap at AI Infra Summit 2026 — but OpenAI's Daniel
  Morris on-panel: **"I cannot find a use case for CXL"** in live model operation (only
  cold/inactive data). Recurring CXL-skeptic datapoint.
- **Georgia Tech open-sources GT2N** — a 2nm-class GAA nanosheet PDK w/ backside power
  (BSD-3, 72 cells, OpenROAD flow). Lets universities/startups research angstrom-era design
  without a foundry NDA.

## Sector watch
- **Optics:** Tower + NewPhotonics begin **HVM of laser-integrated, serviceable optical-
  engine PICs** for AI scale-out/up ([[TSEM]]); Lightmatter joins Open CPX MSA (first
  bidirectional CPX engine); **GlobalFoundries + Marvell** expand optical-connectivity
  collaboration.
- **Memory:** **Kioxia halts NAND price hikes** to protect demand after customer resistance
  ([[285A.T]]); CXMT wins DRAM share among Chinese smartphone brands + plans HBM entry
  ([[000660.KS]]).
- **Power:** onsemi Embedded Power Platform (AI racks); **Bloom Energy launches 800V DC**
  architecture ([[BE]]); ROHM SiC design-win in BMW Neue Klasse.
- **Compute/DC:** Anthropic's first Australian lease (Zerra DC, 2.16 GW Queensland); Apple
  weighing Nvidia server tech for a server-market return; ADI adopts Cadence Tensilica DSP.
- **Foundry/finance:** ClassOne follow-on orders for 6-inch InP gold-plating tools; $22B
  chip-industry loan tied to Blackstone/Alphabet; Modi draws $30B chipmaking pledges at
  SEMICON India.
