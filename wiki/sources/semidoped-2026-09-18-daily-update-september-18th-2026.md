# Semi Doped Daily — Sep 18, 2026

## Highlights
- **Crusoe closes $3.9B Series F @ $30.9B** (Atreides/Mubadala/Valor) — one of the largest
  single raises by a non-hyperscaler AI-infra operator; funds "AI factories" (campuses down
  to modular **Crusoe Spark**) + Crusoe Cloud. Gulf sovereign capital via Mubadala.
  (→ [[ai-debt-financing]] — neoclouds convert watts→tokens fastest in a compute crunch.)
- **CXMT stands up a NAND flash R&D line in Beijing** vs YMTC — a second competitive axis
  across the full memory stack. **Barron's: pressure falls harder on SK Hynix than Micron**
  (Hynix's segment mix more exposed). CXMT also disclosed HBM intent. (→ [[memory-dram-cycle]],
  [[nand-flash-storage]].)
- **Jensen: Nvidia will sell ~2× as many chips next year.** **Nebius raised Nvidia GPU
  rates up to 21% in October (2nd hike)** as demand outruns supply — stock rallied. A
  consistent picture: demand still outpacing capacity. (→ [[NVDA]], [[NBIS]].)
- **Solidigm (SK Hynix unit) weighing a US NAND fab** — onshoring under political pressure.
- **SoftBank piles on ~$21B fresh borrowings** in a week + Arm margin loan to **$25B**;
  **Nvidia commits $2B into Brookfield's AI-infra fund.** Neither firm treats capital costs
  as a reason to slow. (→ [[ai-debt-financing]].)
- **SEMICON India 2026:** Tata Electronics signs **binding** agreements with **Nexperia,
  Fujifilm, and Besi** (converts $12B pipeline to commitments; Nexperia's India pivot after
  the Wingtech break). **ASML deepens Tata fab support; sees India semi market $120B by
  2030.** (→ [[ASML]], [[BESI]].)
- **CCL/MLCC bottleneck:** **Doosan commits ₩968.4B to copper-clad-laminate** capacity for
  AI servers (CCL lead times ~1yr); **Samsung Electro-Mechanics** unveils high-cap MLCCs for
  AI-server power delivery. Two Korean suppliers, one shared constraint. SEMCO on track for
  a **record Q3** (KB maintains ₩3M target). (→ [[009150.KS]].)
