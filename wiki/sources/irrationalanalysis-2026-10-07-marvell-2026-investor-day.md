A slide-by-slide, deliberately snarky recap of Marvell's 2026 investor day, from an author who has been "consistently ultra Broadcom bull and moderate Marvell bear" for ~18 months. Framing confession: he **sold his [[CIEN]] position to buy more [[AXTI]]** (levering optics/InP) the day before — and then Matt Murphy's event "sent Ciena to the moon" while only mildly moving Marvell, which he reads as "cosmic justice" for his anti-Marvell trash talk. He notes Broadcom is used by the market as a funding short while Marvell "has gone to the moon" ("meth for the pod monkeys").

What he genuinely praises: Marvell's **dense SRAM** IP ("excellent," A+ Hot Chips data a year ago); the **die-to-die PHY** (bidirectional, "very impressive," though he doubts it direct-drives optics in bidi); and one **1.6T coherent constellation** that is "genuinely impressive" — pre-FEC BER 3e-6 with post-FEC 1e-11 (target ~2e-13). "5G/telco is dead. Love to see it."

What he derides: far-out **$70-90B-by-FY31 / ~$20B-FY28** targets as a bubble tell ("when semiconductor companies give far-out targets like this, it indicates bubble"), and "half the deck" as CAGR bar charts over substance. On optics he is scathing: **TFLN is a "meme material" pitched by PhDs** with no real-world understanding; **µLED** he's long dismissed; the **GeSi EAM** pivot to NPO/CPO wastes the shoreline-saving point of putting modulators under the silicon; EAM is C-band only while OCI-MSA is defined at O-band; "rectangular optical lenses"; the out-of-spec concat FEC implemented for Google; repeated jabs at Innolight's view of the Aria/Ara 1.6T DSP respin (a Marvell VP fired over it). The HBM-as-cache memory-expander product is "cool" but he questions whether Marvell can win HBM allocation for it over XPU needs, and the latency (<100 ns would be good). Publishes his **Irrational Analysis public-company SerDes rankings (Oct 2026)** (Broadcom top).

## Calls
- [[MRVL]] NEUTRAL $287.01 — moderate bear maintained; engineering wins (SRAM, die-to-die, one 1.6T constellation) real, but optics roadmap (TFLN/µLED/EAM-to-CPO) derided and FY31 guide a "bubble tell."
- [[AVGO]] LONG $375.81 — reaffirms ultra-Broadcom-bull; "engineering delta is quite wide," SerDes #1.
- [[AXTI]] LONG $84.06 — added (sold CIEN to fund it); levering optics/InP substrate.
- [[CIEN]] NEUTRAL $443.65 — sold the position after the event "sent Ciena to the moon" (profit-take); still doesn't understand why the Marvell event moved Ciena so much.
