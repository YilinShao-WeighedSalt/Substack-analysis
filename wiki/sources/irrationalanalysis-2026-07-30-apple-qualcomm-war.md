Reaction to Qualcomm's Q3-2026 earnings call. Author holds QCOM puts (small). Core read: **Apple is preparing for war** — silently starting FCC qualification of an iPhone lineup that gives QCOM low-single-digit share (foldable only) vs the prior ~20% expected, ahead of the **April-1-2027 QTL licensing expiration**, and waited until the last minute to inform Qualcomm to "maximize pain" (the "capacity constraints" excuse in the slides was called out by Stacy Rasgon; Akash folded). Verizon likely green-lit next-gen iPhones without mmWave now that its C-band (bought Mar-2021) is deployed — clearing the path to a global Apple modem. Selling modems to Apple is QCT's highest-margin business, so a small new-business revenue line "severely harms QCT gross margins."

Second leg: the **memory crisis is obliterating Android** — as DRAM eats more of the BOM, OEMs down-spec (this year's or last year's premium chip), and once consumers don't notice, they won't go back; a structural dilution of Qualcomm's value that could run 2+ years. Handset gets worse in 2027, possibly 2028. On the datacenter pivot: QCOM had a big investor-day event with "fake racks" but **no test silicon — just RTL and Virtuoso simulations** ("Change the brand name from Dragonfly to Vaporbullshit"). Argues QCOM deserves a lower valuation than MediaTek ([[2454.TW]]), which has a real, accretive datacenter business.

## Calls
- [[QCOM]] — SHORT (px@call $151.60): Apple modem design-out + memory-crisis Android implosion + datacenter vaporware; author holds puts.
- [[2454.TW]] — LONG (relative, px@call NT$3,235): "real datacenter scale, real accretive GM" vs QCOM's slideware; QCOM should trade below MediaTek.
