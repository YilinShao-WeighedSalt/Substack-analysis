# Daily Update — August 28th, 2026

News-dense daily. Threads the custom-ASIC proof point (Jalapeño benchmarks) against a memory-cost margin squeeze and a widening TSMC foundry lead.

## Summary

- **Marvell.** Beat FQ2 (rev **$1.87B**) and **raised** the annual forecast, **yet shares fell** — investors fixated on *when* the reported **$120B Google ASIC** commitment shows up in revenue. The Next Platform: the $120B is a **multi-year cumulative estimate, not a near-term booking**; a sizable gap vs anything Marvell can recognize soon. CEO Murphy cited accelerating custom-silicon demand; analyst Qs kept returning to Google deal pacing. *The beat was real; the timeline is genuinely murky.*
- **Anthropic / Nscale / MatX.** Anthropic signs a **6-yr $45B** compute deal with **Nscale** (~460MW Vera Rubin at the Monarch Campus, WV) — guaranteed GPU access without owning silicon. Consequently, **talks to buy MatX (~$7B) collapsed** → partnership instead. IPO prospectus expected to file after Labor Day.
- **SK Hynix — US HBM fab.** Groundbreaking Aug-27 at Purdue (West Lafayette, IN) on its **first US AI-memory hub** (~133 acres, HBM line + advanced-packaging R&D). CEO Kwak: output starts **2029**; **memory shortage expected to persist through 2030**; **$4B** price tag (Bloomberg); exploring deeper Kioxia NAND cooperation.
- **Nvidia margins.** Paused **revenue-sharing** arrangements with AI cloud partners (WSJ/Reuters); lowered FQ2 gross-margin outlook citing **surging memory-chip prices**; warned revenue growth trails demand growth on supply bottlenecks (foundry, power, sites).
- **OpenAI Jalapeño benchmarks.** First independent benchmarks show **1.5–1.9× better efficiency/watt** than Nvidia's current inference hardware (EE Times). A **clean-sheet general-purpose accelerator**, not a derivative — makes the efficiency figures meaningful. Georgia PSC **approved Georgia Power's contract** for OpenAI's $20B DC (consumer-protection: OpenAI absorbs overruns).
- **Foundry.** Global foundry market **+29% in Q2** on AI-silicon demand; **TSMC widens its share lead**; **Samsung ~7% share** — wide enough to raise advanced-node roadmap questions. TSMC leverage into 2027 capacity negotiations.
