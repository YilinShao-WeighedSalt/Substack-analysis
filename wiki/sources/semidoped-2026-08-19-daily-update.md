- **Analog Devices record $4.02B quarter** on datacenter+industrial; rev +40%, EPS +163% YoY, $1.7B returned. Austin's angle: **Empower** ($1.5B, closed Jul-7) = in-package power delivery for sub-1V, kilo-amp AI chips (the I²R "physics wall").
- **SK Hynix $29B (₩40T) treasury cancellation** — Korea's largest ever; return target lifted "within 50%" → **"over 50%" FCF**; management says price underrepresents intrinsic value.
- **Marvell grants Google an option to buy $12.2B** (~7%, ~59M sh) tied to a TPU supply deal; vests one tranche per **$500M custom-silicon revenue** (full vest ~$120B to FY2033, ~$18B/yr). Positive for Marvell CXL too.
- **Samsung hikes foundry prices up to 15%** as TSMC CoWoS fills; overflow reroutes to Samsung + Intel **EMIB-T** (Unimicron/ASE gain substrate/assembly); TSMC developing **COUPE** as CoWoS successor.
- **Cerebras CS-4** launch — Austin: "thermal/power/systems iteration, not architectural" (same 44 GB SRAM/900K cores/5nm; 3 wafers/rack vs 2, +50% SRAM density/rack). "Still an improvement!"
- Sector: **Cadence** CEO — 45% of revenue now from own-chip designers; **Etched** doubles to $21B; **Velaura** >$1B; **Socionext taps Intel 18A-P**; NXP doubles Malaysia 300mm back-end.
