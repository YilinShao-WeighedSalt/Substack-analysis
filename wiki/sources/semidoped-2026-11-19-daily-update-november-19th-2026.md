Intel Foundry's long-awaited external-customer proof points land the same week Micron says the memory squeeze only tightens.

- **Intel Foundry lands Microsoft + a first 18A customer.** Microsoft will package its first-party Azure silicon with Intel Foundry **EMIB** under a long-term deal that also locks multiyear Xeon (incl. next-gen Diamond Rapids). Separately Lip-Bu Tan said Intel "just signed the first customer" for **18A** (on 18A-P, the improved variant in risk production since June), unnamed. EMIB gives hyperscalers a second big-AI packaging route while TSMC CoWoS stays stretched; a leading-edge wafer customer is the harder prize Intel has chased five years. 18A yields reportedly ~70% (chatter), a sharp turn. → **LONG [[INTC]]**.
- **Micron: memory tightens from here.** CEO Mehrotra — 75% of 2027 production already sold, customers now negotiating 2028; '27/'28 "even tighter than 2026". 26 supply/collaboration agreements, many 5yr+, backed by **$32B upfront** + take-or-pay + pricing bands; memory now "sets the economics of AI factories". **Stacked 3D DRAM** penetration 2028-29, every major customer asking to co-develop. → **LONG [[MU]]**.
- **OpenAI's models are redesigning AMD's GPUs.** OpenAI models rewrote the SystemVerilog behind AMD's flagship GPU IP for up to **14% PPA** gains (some already taped out); single-digit gains on a next CPU; −13% standard-cell area in OpenAI's own Jalapeño vector unit. Every rewrite checked with **Synopsys**; OpenAI now training a Synopsys model to drive EDA like an expert designer ("~a year of progress in <5 months"). → **LONG [[SNPS]]** (agentic-EDA demand); constructive [[AMD]] (offset next day by the Hunterbrook short).
- **Anthropic prices the largest IPO ever** — $83/sh (above range), **$107.3B** raised incl. $26B private placement, ANT on Nasdaq; ~$900B compute committed (Amazon/Google); $567M Q3 op loss; filing flags the soft spot — providers lean on third-party financing, and if it dries up contracted capacity "may be delayed, repriced, reduced, or not delivered." → [[ai-debt-financing]].
- **Export controls** — remote-access rule effective (Alibaba admits a compute-access hit even as capex +60%; Tencent slower model; Hygon/Cambricon slide). → [[geopolitics-export-controls]].
- Sector: **Kulicke & Soffa** FQ4 rev doubled to $410.3M; **Soitec** ~$1.2B photonics-SOI deals; Lenovo record $30.2B (+48%) but $640M net loss; JEDEC previews **DDR6**; ASE breaks ground on NT$248.9B Kaohsiung test plant; SK hynix installs Besi/AMAT hybrid bonders for 20-high HBM5; Nvidia CEO search approaches Rene Haas.
