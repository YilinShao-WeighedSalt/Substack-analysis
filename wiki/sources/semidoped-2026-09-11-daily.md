# Semi Doped Daily — September 11th, 2026

>$1.5B poured into inference silicon in a single day; Oracle's backlog explodes; Microsoft commits to a decade of buildout.

## Lead items
- **Oracle FY27 Q1:** revenue $19.3B (+30% YoY), **OCI +121%**; **RPO backlog $664B** (record); **300K GPUs delivered in the quarter** (hyperscaler-class, not neocloud); raised $20B fresh capital; FY guide ~$90B; shares +7%. *Austin: "Delivered 300K GPUs in a single quarter — clearly a hyperscaler." Vik: watch the single-supplier substrate/capacitor bottlenecks that could kill the golden goose.*
- **Positron AI $875M @ $5B** valuation + **Ayar Labs $150M** (ODM Wiwynn joins as strategic; $650M raised in 2026) → >$1.5B into inference silicon/CPO in one day. (Both private — no priced call.)
- **Microsoft: 38 GW of DC capacity by 2032** (~3× current fleet), computing-as-a-utility. *Austin: at ~$40B/GW Vera Rubin that's ~$1.5T of compute capex over 5 yrs if all-Nvidia (it won't be).*
- **Pentagon weighs a $5B direct loan to Fluidstack** (private) — to shore up US supply chain, no clear precedent; Fluidstack broke ground on a $4B Cameron County TX campus.
- **OpenAI eyes Samsung as a second fab source** alongside TSMC for its Broadcom-designed custom chips (Tom's Hardware); wafer volume exceeds any single foundry. *Vik: TSMC capacity constraints drive customers into Intel/Samsung's arms + gives OpenAI pricing leverage.*

## Sector watch
- **Foundry:** TSMC accelerates A14/1.4nm Taichung by 6 months, mass-production 2027; Samsung Foundry–Qualcomm hit a **2nm pricing impasse**; **Synopsys cuts NPU die area 22% on Samsung 2nm** via AI-assisted physical design.
- **Memory:** Phison record August; Micron awards Taiwan staff bonuses up to 68 months' pay (retention); SK Hynix splits bonus cash/stock.
- **Packaging:** Orchem dual TGV plating + wet-adhesion for glass substrates; Kolon mPPO low-dielectric resin capacity; Haesung DS full substrate portfolio (all KPCA 2026).
- **PCB/Components:** TLB SOCAMM/CXL PCBs; **Renesas raises prices again from Jan-2027** (2nd hike in a year); ASPEED BMC price hikes in December.
- **Power:** **Eaton grid-to-chip 800 VDC** architecture (removes AC↔DC stages); **Bloom Energy launches Power Connect** (>40% faster fuel-cell deploy; record backlog); HD Hyundai commits >₩1T to land-based generation + SMRs.
- **Optics/Networking:** Kyocera+Tohoku integrate optical isolators onto silicon photonics (removes a CPO hybrid-assembly bottleneck); **Lightera (Furukawa) expands optical-fiber capacity**; FCC's final equipment-authorization update **stops short of blocking Chinese transceivers** (near-term reprieve for Chinese optics; removes the Western-substitution tailwind).
- **Datacenters:** Nscale adds OpenAI CFO Fidji Simo to its board pre-IPO; Blackstone buys Flow Control Holdings (liquid cooling); **Core Scientific wins conditional ERCOT approval for up to 1,402 MW**.
