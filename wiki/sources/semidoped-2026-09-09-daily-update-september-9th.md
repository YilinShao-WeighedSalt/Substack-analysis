Headline: **ASML secures formal High-NA EUV commitments from TSMC and Samsung**, plus a new 12-inch photomask platform — converting a distant roadmap item into contracted infrastructure. **TSMC targets 2030 (A10/A11 nodes)**, the first confirmed process anchor; **Samsung is more aggressive — High-NA in high-volume DRAM by 2028** (an industry first). ASML broke ground on expanded manufacturing. Austin: **Intel Foundry has a lead here — already running layers of Panther Lake in HVM** while TSMC waits for the 6×12-inch masks. **Kioxia CEO Doi rejects any SK Hynix production tie-up** (SK is Kioxia's largest shareholder) and vows NAND price restraint — reinforcing Kioxia as frontrunner for Apple's long-term NAND deal. **OpenAI + Samsung expand their custom-chip pact** (Samsung fab + packaging; OpenAI used its own models to design Jalapeño); CFO Friar says **Luna undercuts DeepSeek on price**. **Google commits €13B to Finland AI + a 22-yr nuclear PPA** (longest-duration clean-energy DC contract). **CoreWeave hits $5B revenue**, claims GB300 NVL72 lead (6.5x DeepSeek-R1 throughput at TP4), first Nvidia Exemplar Cloud, and a CoreWeave Interconnect built with Google Cloud. **ADI buys Alif Semiconductor** — edge-AI MCUs (Cortex-M55 + Ethos-U55 NPU) for always-on sensor-edge inference (Vik: a robotics/analog play). **Meta launches Muse** personal AI agent on an isolated Linux VM (WhatsApp, one-time card at checkout).

Sector: Synopsys+A*STAR IME packaging R&D; LG Innotek FC-BGA + glass substrate (2028); **Samsung Electro-Mechanics vs LG Innotek** substrate rivalry at KPCA; SK Hynix Future Forum (HBM primary growth vector); CXMT R&D +60% YoY, claims 10% DRAM share but trails on HBM3 TSV; OIF 1600ZR coherent IA; Semtech 224G optical chipset; Meta+Panmnesia CXL-as-single-memory-domain in Nature; Groq 3 LPX; JD Cloud 100k Moore Threads GPU cluster; Nvidia-backed Sievert Indonesia $3.1B GPU loan; Massachusetts clean-energy DC mandate.
