# Semi Doped Daily — Oct 30, 2026

- **Nvidia weighs credit guarantees behind Nscale's $45B Anthropic compute deal** (WV data center);
  Nscale separately pursuing a $35B IPO. Vik: "not a good look when NVIDIA needs to backstop purchases to
  NeoCloud… bandaid measures." Austin: a parent co-signing a mortgage — fine if the neo can make payments.
- **Samsung record Q3** (rev ₩195.3T / ~$146B, op profit ₩107.4T / ~$80B) **driven almost entirely by AI
  memory**; **device division loses money again**; chip div still missed some estimates. Management warns the
  **AI chip shortage is worsening** — HBM pricing power unlikely to ease. Still no shareholder-return commitment.
- **Intel −23% after hours** on a revenue-forecast miss: weak PC demand + **supply-chain problems squeezing
  data-center components** (the segment that was supposed to be the good news). Cash burn mounting; AMD + Arm
  circling both client and server. Vik: "AI PC" isn't real. Austin: server-CPU shortfall has gone on for many quarters.
- **TSMC near deal to buy two AUO display fabs for CoWoS conversion** (adds to AP7/AP8); fast cleanroom floor vs
  greenfield. Kinsus flags 3 scaling challenges, exploring **aluminum nitride** vs glass substrates (low loss).
- **Murata + Samsung Electro-Mechanics race on AI-server MLCCs:** SEMCO now **8 LT MLCC agreements, cumulative
  >₩5T** (latest ₩879.3B, ~23% of a quarter's sales); AI customers locking 2027 supply. **Murata commits ¥1T
  ($6.4B)/5yr to >2× MLCC output.**
- **Hanmi Semiconductor** hits **50% op margin** on HBM **TC bonders** (op profit ₩90.8B, +33.9% YoY; −30% QoQ);
  SK Hynix plaque for decade-long HBM alliance. Vik: TC-bonder margins hold only while HBM stays on
  thermocompression and not hybrid bonding (near-term, high hybrid-bonded stacks cost too much).
- Sector watch: Ibiden ₩1.28T Gama substrate expansion; Unimicron warns PCB equipment lead times 30 months;
  Foxconn+Nvidia 8-week NVL72 deploy + OpenAI partnership; Huawei Ascend 950DT 1,024-NPU SuperPoD; Sandisk/Seagate
  NAND+HAMR booked to 2028; Infineon EU €319M GaN aid (Villach); Tokyo Electron + Advantest raise FY26 outlooks;
  Vertiv/Delta/Generac power; Corning AI-fiber profit; Wolfspeed–BorgWarner SiC; Cohere Series E C$7B.
