# Semi Doped Daily — Oct 29, 2026

- **Marvell nears deal to buy Lumentum** — advanced talks; would fold optical-transceiver hardware
  into Marvell's DSP portfolio for a vertically-integrated **1.6T co-packaged-optics stack**, in direct
  competition with Broadcom. No terms disclosed. (SD recorded a podcast on it with Cignal AI.)
- **Alphabet + Samsung earnings miss, shaking AI-payoff confidence.** GOOGL −~10% on weak search/ad
  sales (is AI a threat to core search?); Samsung chip op-profit missed, stock −6.6% on no shareholder-
  return plan (but capex +60%, HBM sold out through 2027; Google Cloud rev doubled to $30.7B, total
  capex $51.4B). Infrastructure spend accelerating; profit conversion lagging.
- **Microsoft Azure +48%** ($33.17B) — strongest cloud growth in years, cleared consensus; capex pulled
  forward, tightening GPU allocation through 2028; FCF fell to $18.6B. A direct rebuttal to Alphabet's miss.
- **DOJ scrutinizes Nvidia's $13B Hugging Face acquisition** — vertical move into the open-model software
  layer draws antitrust attention.
- **SK Hynix crosses ₩100T revenue** (₩104.62T / ~$78B), record op profit ₩83.33T; new CEO Kwak Noh-jung
  outlines ~₩100T dividend+buyback to rebuild investor confidence after IPO-unit pushback.
- **KLA** fiscal Q1 — first major equipment earnings of the AI-capex cycle; process-control intensity the
  variable to watch. **GE Vernova** buys **Trench Group** $9B (HV transformers, grid bottleneck), raises outlook.
- Sector watch: FuriosaAI raises $620M (val $2.9B); Moore Threads rev +1,774%; Kioxia LD4 QLC SSDs; Apple
  2nm A20 Pro in iPad mini (first N2 tablet); CXMT tops Shanghai index; Lam+imec 3D-integration; UMC rev
  +25.6% record shipments; Vertiv +61% sales / orders +100%; Generac record; Altera Agilex 2X; ST Q4 growth.
