A furious reaction to the $115B Marvell–[[LITE]] all-stock merger. IA's steelman of the deal slides finds **zero integration in the ELSFP (standardized external-laser) case** and only a *small* amount of co-design on the transceiver side (EML/DSP/driver/TIA) — and even there argues Marvell mgmt is so inept that **Innolight will out-execute them anyway**. The sharpest point: Lumentum's transceiver business (**Cloudlight**) is NOT being spun out, so Marvell now **competes with its own customers** (Innolight, Eopto, Accelink, [[AAOI]]) — expect them to shift business to **Broadcom/Credo/Semtech/Macom**; Coherent's "fragile neutrality" is a bridge too far. "Conspiracy Corner" theorizes Murphy wants to become Broadcom (internal InP+GaAs), that Celestial failed so he's collecting optical components before the talent leaves, or that he sees optical DSP peaking (NPO/CPO) and is M&A-ing out. On Hurlston: he may think this is the **top for optics** and wants out, or fears the Chinese crack a 300mW/<1MHz-linewidth laser in 12-18mo (which would crush Lumentum's 80-90% margin products). Long rant on Marvell's engineering diaspora (Innovium → Astera/MatX/Broadcom) and botched **Ara 1.6T DSP** (paid fines to Innolight). Discloses: held LITE for LTCG timing (Nov-24), **shorted MRVL as a hedge**, ~$360K cash set aside for tax prepay; weighing holding LITE to run an activist campaign.

## Calls
- **[[MRVL]] SHORT (hedge)** — deal is value destruction, channel conflict, incompetent mgmt.
- **[[LITE]] LONG (hold)** — standalone optical moat; weighing activist campaign vs Murphy.
- Read-through beneficiaries of Marvell/Lumentum channel conflict: **[[AVGO]]/[[CRDO]]/[[SMTC]]/[[MTSI]]** (customers defect), **[[COHR]]** neutrality strained.
