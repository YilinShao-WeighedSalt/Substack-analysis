# Semi Doped — Daily Update, November 13th 2026

First full day of Nvidia without Jensen Huang. Hosts: Austin Lyons (Chipstrat) & Vik Sekar (Vik's Newsletter). Dominant frame: **the physical supply chain keeps posting records (WFE, networking, cooling) even as the AI-credit / neocloud names get repriced — and Washington piles on Nvidia.**

## Summary

- **Washington piles on Nvidia.** Multiple DOJ probes now circle Nvidia per Semafor — one antitrust probe aimed at Nvidia itself, two others on Megaspeed/Supermicro focused on ex-Megaspeed exec Alice Huang (Manhattan prosecutors examining Supermicro-server smuggling into China). No sign of criminal charges against Nvidia/Jensen. Congress is moving faster with Nvidia's loudest anti-controls voice gone — Speaker Johnson cites a "developing consensus" on limiting chip exports; ≥4 bills (incl. blocking Chinese firms renting Nvidia GPUs in overseas DCs, pressuring allies on tools); Republicans want curbs on the must-pass defense bill; Huang testifies before Thanksgiving. SemiAnalysis estimates **>1.5GW of restricted Nvidia chips reached China anyway**; Chinese optics (Innolight, Eoptolink) fell >8%. Nvidia reports Nov-17 with Colette Kress interim CEO. *Vik: controls get much tighter both ways (DeepSeek will withhold open models). Austin: Nvidia must keep investing in American open models.*

- **Applied Materials — record year, +30% WFE call.** Record FQ4 **$10.41B (+53% YoY)**; chip-equipment +~64% to $8B; guides next Q ~$11.1B (> $10.89B consensus). CEO Gary Dickerson expects **WFE spend to rise >30% in 2027**, customers asking for tools "sooner, not later." China fell to 25% of rev (from 29%). $2B buyback in the quarter. *Vik: WFE rising ⇒ chip demand strong. Austin: WFE is the bullwhip tail — a demand air-pocket shows here a quarter or two late, but demand looks real & sustainable.*

- **Cisco — doubles down on AI networking.** Record FQ1 **$18.5B (+24%)**, fastest growth in 15+ years; networking +43%; FY guide raised to $74.8–75.8B. **AI-infra revenue from hyperscalers now guided $9B** this FY (up from $7.5B three months ago; >2× last year). Three new hyperscaler design wins (2× Silicon One **P200** for DC interconnect/scale-across, 1× 1.6T client-side optics for scale-out); **G300 shipping into hyperscaler labs**; 18 AI wins over 5 quarters. Non-GAAP product GM −3.5pts YoY. *Vik: time to go deeper on Cisco. Austin: Silicon One is scale-out/across only today, not scale-up.*

- **Neoclouds — demand, not buildings.** **Nebius** rev **+571% to $981M** but trimmed 2026 rev to $3.0–3.2B (third-party DC capacity slipped to 2027); raised 2026 capex to ~$27B, plans $40–45B next year for $12–13B rev; stock −17%. Prepay/financing should cover 75–85% of next year's capex. **Cerebras** beat (core rev **$231M +70%**), added **Microsoft as 3rd hyperscaler** (after AWS, Oracle), but **admitted it is behind on OpenAI's first capacity tranches**, back on schedule 2H27. Both blame the same bottleneck: **land, power, construction — not chip supply or demand.** *Vik: every approved/built GW of power becomes extremely valuable under the new anti-buildout sentiment.*

- **Samsung eyes Modine to cool AI data centers.** Samsung in talks to buy **Modine Manufacturing** (~$5.6B pre-report; shares +28% AH), a Wisconsin DC-cooling maker — a N. American cooling base after buying Europe's FläktGroup (€1.5B) last year. Modine DC rev +95% last Q to ~$440M; could be Samsung's largest acquisition ever. *Vik: downstream of higher rack density is cooling — a space to watch 2–3 yrs.*

### Industry Chatter (unconfirmed whispers)
- **Intel Crescent Island** inference GPU — Lip-Bu Tan told the SGLang Summit it already has ~"$2B" of customer purchases (currency/terms unspecified).
- **Intel EMIB-T** — sell-side notes ~7 external customer engagements for the advanced-packaging platform.
- **HBM4 pricing** — SemiAnalysis: Nvidia pays ~19–20% less for HBM4 than Google/Amazon/AMD/Broadcom; a Jensen-era edge the chain now questions.
- **Samsung memory** — told BofA it has seen **no order cuts** tied to a possible US AI DC slowdown post-midterms.
- **Broadcom** — Damnang's sources say it has already secured 2027–28 supply as custom silicon eyes Nvidia's leadership vacuum.
- **Nvidia staff** — Wired: employees pushing for Huang's return (internal suggestion-box post >1,600 comments).

### Sector Watch (factual, no SD stance)
- **SMIC** record Q3 +34% to $3.19B; AI chip supply tight through 2027. **TSMC** leaked employee data traced to an outside cloud provider (no own-systems breach). **Foxconn** Q3 profit +34% record (AI cloud+networking 54% of sales). **Compal** rev +30%, board OKs $486M for US AI-server capacity. **Nscale** amended IPO filing: rev up ~14×, widening loss. **Advantest** pulled its 10k-testers/yr target forward to end-2027. **ams OSRAM** Q3 €829M on ~€1.3B new semi design wins. **JNTC** to supply HDD glass platters to Seagate from 2027 (challenging Hoya). **Penguin Solutions** CXL card reuses DDR4 as extra server memory amid shortage. **Marvell** adds 3 independent directors incl. Lam CFO Doug Bettinger. **AMD** takes equity stake in **PsiQuantum** (utility-scale quantum co-design).
