A macro/sentiment memo written into "a rather violent few weeks in the market" — author judges we're ~70-80% of the way to peak fear, one more panic + a clearing event (election + Anthropic IPO?) needed to break the doom loop. He is **fully de-levered and happy**; advice is to "dance in the warm rain." The substance is a bubble-complex teardown:

- **Valuation contraction:** OpenAI rushing a $30B raise at **$1.25T** (down from $1.4T) ahead of Anthropic's audited S-1; **Anthropic IPO whisper cut to ~$1.5T** (from $2T, raising $60-100B). A 20% gap between them "does not make sense" given share/vibes.
- **"Alcoholic ARR" (distillation):** a large, "dirty" slice of Anthropic/OpenAI revenue is rival labs (Chinese, Meta-accused) paying to **distill** them at ~1/10th the R&D cost on a 1-3 month delay. The labs either can detect it or are lying; they took the money to juice ARR and now Anthropic is trying to back it out as a "temporary 6-8% headwind" — the revenue simply migrated to OpenAI. (→ [[ai-software-models]])
- **Circular token spend + Dot-Com detour:** Craig Moffett's rebuttal to Gavin Baker's "no dark GPU/ASIC" comfort is "compelling"; ~75% of Anthropic+OpenAI spend is *training*, so financial stress → a flood of inference supply; distillation/competition could obsolete models before ROI.
- **Subscriptions / monetization:** heavy subsidies, users refuse to spend more; math-breakthrough hype is open-sourced (⇒ little economic value); **drug discovery** as the "next big thing" is the worst near-term revenue industry (compresses discovery but ~10yr to approval) — "a very bad time in 2028" if that's all there is.
- **Portfolio philosophy:** trading account net -$1.07M withdrawn (taxes); long-only book in "years I can be unemployed"; driven by "arrogance, spite, disgust" toward "liar corpo scum."
