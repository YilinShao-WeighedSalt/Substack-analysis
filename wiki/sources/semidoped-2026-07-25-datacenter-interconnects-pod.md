# Semi Doped Podcast — Datacenter Interconnects

Educational episode (Austin Lyons + Vik Sekar): data movement is the single biggest bottleneck in
modern AI — compute has outgrown the ability to hook chips together, so datacenter performance is
now interconnect-bound rather than silicon-bound. Covers the **three network tiers (scale-up,
scale-out, scale-across)**, the **copper-vs-optics** trade-off by reach, **pluggable optics → co-packaged
optics (CPO)**, **NVLink vs open UALink**, the role of **DSPs and transceivers**, and the speed
ladder from 400G → 1.6T. No stock calls — this is a concept/teaching source feeding the
[[co-packaged-optics]] and [[serdes-high-speed-connectivity]] themes (context for the InP/optics
and Helios-networking stories this run).
