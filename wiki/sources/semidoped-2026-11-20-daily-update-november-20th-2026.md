The remote-access rule reaches the fab door; a short seller turns the China lens on AMD.

- **TSMC & Samsung stop taking Chinese chips on trust.** TSMC told affected Chinese designers to halt orders ≤16nm absent US licenses (limited to 28/40nm, no HBM/advanced packaging); the spec-check-and-ship route is gone. Samsung clients caught include ByteDance, Baidu Kunlunxin; even Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo phone chips (licenses likely). Overseas GPU detour closing too (Moonshot scraps $150M Indonesian Blackwell rental; ByteDance Malaysia $480M loan paused). → [[geopolitics-export-controls]]; TSMC compliance (small China mix).
- **Hunterbrook points the China lens at AMD.** Alleges blacklisted IEIT Systems kept loading newer AMD firmware (≥6 versions); three IEIT-linked vendors (incl. Instinct partner Aivres) posted near-identical firmware. AMD says the claims "misrepresent" its practices; stock −7.7%. Same day House Foreign Affairs released data: Nvidia+AMD sought 3.4M AI chips for China Dec-May, >879k approved; California AG subpoenaed Nvidia. Net with Nov-19's OpenAI-RTL positive → **NEUTRAL [[AMD]]** (whipsaw).
- **SK hynix becomes Kioxia's largest shareholder** — converts ¥129B CBs → indirect 14.1% of Kioxia (ahead of Toshiba's 10.46%); votes capped 15% to 2028. A NAND rival that already owns Solidigm now holds the biggest single Kioxia stake. → [[nand-flash-storage]]; mild-positive [[000660.KS]].
- **SpaceX sells $32B of GPU debt** (part of $40B+) to buy ~$47.3B of Vera Rubin NVL72, deliveries from January; Apollo-trust lease keeps it off-balance-sheet, pay-even-if-GPUs-fail. Largest tranche yielded **8.26%** vs 5.95% IG avg — the market pricing used-GPU risk. → [[ai-debt-financing]].
- **Samsung hands memory to its HBM developer** — Hwang Sang-joon promoted to president running the memory business (kept DRAM dev); all three new presidents from the chip division. → mild-positive [[005930.KS]].
- Sector: **Keysight** FQ4 orders +53%, rev +41% to $1.99B, commercial comms +73% → **LONG [[KEYS]]**; **Coherent** board authorizes $2B buyback; Samsung buys 4 Rockley silicon-photonics patents; Micron Singapore HBM plant tool-in; Google picks Blue Origin New Glenn for Project Suncatcher; Google tested Intel EMIB-T for next TPUs (yields short, asked TSMC again).
