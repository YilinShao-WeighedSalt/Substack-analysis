**ASML Q3:** €11.7B revenue, €5.0B net income; FY guide raised to €44-45B. Demand will **exceed capacity through at least 2028**; inquiries now stretch to 2030; 2027 and 2028 EUV allocations **already sold out**, leaving TSMC/Samsung/Intel competing for slots. SEMI separately forecasts global 300mm fab-equipment spending at **$227B by 2029** (from $149B this year). *Austin:* sold-out = pricing power; does ASML raise EUV prices next?

**Nvidia / KYEC prepay:** King Yuan Electronics secured a **~$1.4B (NT$45B) advance payment from Nvidia** to build a US (Texas) chip-testing facility — reserving capacity before a machine is installed. *Vik:* same prepay playbook as Amkor in July ($1.5B); as Rubin's power draw climbs, **burn-in + liquid cooling become the choke point**, and KYEC's in-house burn-in is why Nvidia locks them this early. Back-end capacity now as scarce as the front.

**Arm vs Qualcomm verdict:** a Delaware jury found **Arm breached its license** with Qualcomm but awarded only **$2 nominal damages** ($1 on each of two claims). Arm had sought to void Qualcomm's license over the 2021 Nuvia acquisition; Arm prevailed on four other claims. The breach finding gives Qualcomm a legal basis that its **Nuvia-derived architecture falls within its existing license**, undercutting Arm's core argument. *Vik:* Qualcomm wins the principle, not a payout.

**SK Group:** Chairman Chey Tae-won sold a **$406M stake to MSD Partners** (Michael Dell's family office); *Austin:* funds a property division in Chey's divorce (per SK Inc. Oct-2 filing) — a seller who needs cash.

**Sector watch:** SEMI says bridge tech leads next-gen large-format packaging; JCET pivots Korea fabs to AI packaging; **YMTC files landmark IPO** (now #3 global NAND, DRAM next); Huawei ships 100M Kirin devices, Kirin 9050 Pro +53.5% density; **Rambus** launches industry-first PMIC for LPDDR6 SOCAMM2; Siemens Tessent Silicon Executable; **Tower+Semtech** expand SiGe partnership; Bolt Graphics Zeus bring-up; **Wiwynn/Ayar/GUC** demo first multi-vendor rack-scale CPO at OCP; **Cisco+Nvidia USFP** 400G-SerDes pluggable; **Space Norway picks Ciena** for Svalbard/Arctic Way subsea; Mainspring+AEP 1.6GW linear-generator; LS Materials ₩69B ultracap deal; **Adani+Jabil 50:50 India JV** (Jabil's first India AI-infra); CoreWeave targets insurance capital; China IC exports +136% YoY in Sept to $45B; LX Semicon exits SiC (60+ patents for sale); K&S wins SEMI award for fluxless TCB; Hanmi shows HBM4 bonding + 2.5D at Semicon West.
