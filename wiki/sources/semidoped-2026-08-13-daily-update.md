News roundup with Austin/Vik takes.

- **Neocloud supercycle intact.** CoreWeave revenue backlog **$104B + $25B** new commitments; shares +18% after raising prices 25% while sold out; signing A100 contracts to **2029** (6-yr-old GPUs still earning). But debt at **$35.6B**, and it warned that moving off Nvidia would take significant time/capital. Nebius **+514% YoY revenue**, deferred rev ~$6B, H1 infra spend >$8.1B, shares +34%. Super Micro beat on margins/AI-server demand. The Information: both benefiting from *rising* AI compute prices even as capacity grows. Vik: A100s alive to 2029 → imagine Blackwell longevity (bullish for GPU depreciation debates).
- **YMTC grabs #3 in NAND** at 14% share Q2 (past Kioxia/Micron/SanDisk); AI servers = 48% of flash shipments. Caveat: YMTC cut off from US market. CXMT also pushing into mobile DRAM — two fronts against the incumbents. Vik: NAND *type* matters (Kioxia XL-Flash high-IOPS commands higher ASP), so bit-share ≠ revenue-share.
- **Cisco** Q4 rev $17.3B; FY AI-DC forecast $7.5B drew a cool reception vs $9.3B of AI orders already booked; "broad-based" demand. **Lenovo +43%** revenue (best in 5 yrs) on hybrid-AI.
- **Cerebras −17% in late trade** after results disappointed: **hardware revenue contracted QoQ** ("lumpy" demand); raised FY targets but still short of buy-side. Order-momentum-vs-reported-revenue gap the shared theme with Cisco.
- Sector watch: AMD MI455X (CDNA5) deep dive; Nvidia serves Alibaba Qwen3.8 (2.4T params) on GB300 NVL72; Aehr $22M follow-on burn-in order; TSMC board approves +$29.44B capex; Foxconn AI servers >50% of rev (CoWoS the 2027 constraint); Rambus HBM4E controller to 16 Gbps/pin; Meta cuts server count 25% via CXL DDR4 reuse; Anthropic priced at ~$2T ahead of IPO.
