# Daily Update — August 4th, 2026

## Highlights
- **First OCP High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) spec** published by **SK Hynix + SanDisk** (Aug 3), backed by **Google + Tenstorrent** — formalizes HBF as a memory-storage tier between DRAM and NAND for AI inference. Kioxia counters with GP1 (XL-FLASH Gen2, 10M random-read IOPS) + PCIe 6.0 NVMe. Austin: blocker isn't NAND supply but **CoWoS/packaging allocation** (HBF sits on the accelerator interposer); Intel EMIB-T could package it — SK Hynix already testing EMIB, Google has booked Intel to package TPUs.
- **Tower [[TSEM]] record Q2:** revenue **$460M (+24%)**, guide Q3 **$520M (+31%)**. SiPho run-rate **$680M → $1B+ by Q4** (from $180M yr ago). Net profit ex-items $91M (20% margin, +95% YoY). CEO **raised 2028 model to $3.6B rev / $1.2B profit** (from $2.8B/$750M) — "fully spoken for by our customers." An $800M rev raise / +60% profit target in one quarter.
- **onsemi [[ON]]:** Q2 rev $1,604M (+9%), non-GAAP GM 39.3%, FCF $425M (4× YoY). CEO: **"AI data center… we now expect revenue to more than double in 2026."** Q3 guide $1.65–1.75B; expanded Nvidia MGX role, EliteSiC/MOSFET wins, launched **GaNEXUS** (40V–650V GaN).
- Sector: CXMT DRAM into HP/Asus/Acer consumer PCs; DRAM/NAND at fresh record highs; ZJ Innolight HK IPO debut; CoreWeave–Leidos (US intel workloads); NTT Data eyeing $9B DC capex.
