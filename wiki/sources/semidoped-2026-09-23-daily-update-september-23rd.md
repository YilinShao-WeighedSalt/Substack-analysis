- **OpenAI + Anthropic model-cost race:** OpenAI added cheaper GPT-6 variants **Sol & Luna** on Amazon Bedrock; **Anthropic Claude Opus 5.5** designed for lower inference cost, dropped ahead of a Wall-Street debut — Vik reads the same-cycle price cuts as "competitive theater," Anthropic's timing as IPO positioning (also in talks to take direct DC capacity). (→ [[ai-software-models]])
- **TechInsights confirms Apple A20 Pro on TSMC N2** — first independently-verified 2nm chip in a shipping consumer device; establishes N2 yielding at commercial scale with Apple absorbing early volume, clearing the runway for every fabless customer behind it. Apple spent the density on an extra GPU core + neural area, not a smaller die.
- **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 (5GHz, agentic AI) + MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro (2nm-class)** land together — Dimensity 9600 Pro in OPPO Find X10 Pro Max; Samsung validated **LPDDR6** (10.7 Gbps) for Qualcomm ahead of Galaxy S27 Ultra (but may stay LPDDR5X on cost). Most symmetric QCOM/MediaTek flagship cycle in years.
- **Huawei Kirin 9050 Pro** (7nm-equivalent, no EUV) tops Apple A17 Pro in Geekbench multicore per Bernstein (gap ~4yr → ~3yr; still ~30% behind A20 Pro); **DeepSeek orders 160,000 Ascend 960DT units** for Q4-2026, targeting an 8T-param model. (→ [[geopolitics-export-controls]])
- **Qualcomm acquires PickNik Robotics** (MoveIt open-source manipulation) to add a motion-planning layer to Dragonwing edge-AI/robotics. Vik: robotics still hasn't had its ChatGPT moment. (→ [[robotics-embodied-ai]])
- Sector: **Supermicro ships first Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 racks** (first production Rubin systems); ABB Infinitus DC power; Schneider Motivair CDU; **Semtech 50G mobile transport into full production**; CXMT triples factory commitments; ASM flowable-carbon sub-2nm; Nexperia-Tata India fab; BOE joins glass-substrate race.
