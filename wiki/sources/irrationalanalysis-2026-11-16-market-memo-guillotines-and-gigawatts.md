A one-off **political/macro memo** — no engineering, no priced ticker call. Thesis: the Jensen saga is a **distraction** from a coordinated, deep-state-led move to **kill open-source/open-weight AI** and drive regulatory capture. The author maps US politics into three factions (Grassroots/populist, Establishment, Deep-State) and argues a **rare "unholy alliance" of all three** is forming against AI — the same pattern that produced the Patriot Act after 9/11. Grassroots hates AI out of job-loss fear (Dario's "AI will kill us / unemploy you" messaging backfired into populist backlash); Establishment Democrats will make Jensen a piñata as the anti-industry play post-midterms; the deep-state silently allies to concentrate power. Evidence cited: **Susan Collins (R-ME) lost re-election to the AI backlash**; the author's non-tech social circle (D&D group) is virulently anti-AI — the semis/AI "bubble" is a *social* bubble and readers are **"almost certainly offsides."**

**Market stance:** fully **de-risked / de-levered**, about to go on a one-week vacation; the "party" analogy = "do you want to stay or go home, sleep it off, and come back later? There will always be other parties." Explicitly declines to discuss the usual red flags (RASA, neocloud backstops, vendor-financed shitcos, circular deals, slowing Anthropic ARR) — says others cover those — and instead warns the **political front** will break CapEx projections that the sell-side is still modeling as a mere 4-5 GW 2028→2029 slip.
