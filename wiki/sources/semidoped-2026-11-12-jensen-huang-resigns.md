Austin Lyons (Chipstrat) and Vik Sekar recorded ~2 hours after Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO (Thu Nov-12 AM). A newsflash episode, lightly argued.

**Governance vs shareholder value.** Austin: a board's first duty is governance, not share price — even a legendary founder must go for breaking conduct rules (the first Megaspeed/Alice-Huang affair he initially denied; then a second affair with an internal report), because keeping him signals lying/rule-breaking is acceptable; "Jensen is not above the law." Vik's counter: the CEO job is uniquely brutal (cites a Bloomberg interview where Jensen was hesitant to wish it on his kids, and foresaw a successor "in 20 years") — but agrees the standard must be highest for the hardest job.

**The AI trade near-term.** Both: nothing fundamental changed overnight — demand is real, roadmap (Rubin Ultra, Feynman) intact, Colette Kress as interim CEO should run "maintain mode" and just execute for the next year. Next earnings **Tuesday Nov 17** (Jensen was to keynote Tomorrow/x; likely replaced).

**The real long-term risk — bureaucracy.** Jensen's **flat org (~60 direct reports, founder-driven "go get it done")** is impossible for a successor to replicate. Vik's fear: a leadership vacuum invites infighting/politics and a slide into **spreadsheet-CEO, red-tape culture** (the thing that hobbled Intel) where budget decisions stall across VPs. Founder-CEOs (Elon, Zuck, Jensen) take long-term bets a professional manager won't; a Satya-Nadella-style turnaround is the hopeful case.

**Succession / who's next.** No succession plan is itself a failure. Internal: **Ian Buck** (father of CUDA, Stanford PhD) is the only plausible internal name; Jonah Alben respected but unlikely to want it. External floated: **Rene Haas** (Arm CEO, Nvidia alumnus — credible), Lisa Su (both reject — her move is to attack, not switch teams), Tim Cook / Lip-Bu Tan (dismissed). Madison Huang (Jensen's daughter, a VP) mentioned.

**AMD angle.** Both agree Nvidia's weakness is **AMD's opening** — Lisa Su should "put my foot on the gas" (AMD crushing on server CPU after Intel's bad quarter), not defect to Nvidia.

## Calls
- **[[NVDA]] NEUTRAL** — near-term demand/roadmap intact under interim CEO Kress; no succession plan (only Ian Buck internal) + long-term risk of decay into managerial bureaucracy is the overhang.
- **[[AMD]] LONG** — Nvidia's leadership vacuum is AMD's moment to attack; strong post-Intel CPU momentum (see Nov-11 daily).
- **[[ARM]]** — context: Rene Haas a credible external Nvidia-CEO candidate.
- **[[INTC]]** — context: cited as the cautionary tale of founder-culture decaying into process-driven bureaucracy.
