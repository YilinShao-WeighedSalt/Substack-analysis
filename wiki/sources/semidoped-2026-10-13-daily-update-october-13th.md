OCP Global Summit (San Jose) daily — "the conference is PACKED, lines out the door." Hardware disclosures dominate.

- **ASML unveils Hyper-NA EUV** — 5nm single-exposure patterning, roadmap beyond High-NA; removes multi-patterning at sub-2nm. Timed to ASML earnings this week (capacity-buildout guide the investor test).
- **Microsoft Maia 300** — multichiplet inference accelerator, **multi-GW Azure deployment by 2027**, explicit cost-reduction mandate to cut Nvidia dependence; ~40% more tokens/s, **7× rack throughput over Maia 200 on N2P**. *Vik: SK Hynix already locked the memory on the whole Maia fleet (since Maia 200) — "the memory trade doesn't care which ASIC wins."* *Austin: multi-GW is small next to MSFT's 38 GW-by-2032 target; room for both.*
- **CXMT claims denser 4F² DRAM cell** (adopted *because* EUV is out of reach), targeting **server DDR5** — collision course with Samsung/SK Hynix in memory's highest-margin segment. Both Korean names + Micron fell; some analysts call the selloff overdone. *Austin: "Announced, not shipping. But with Samsung ~80% sold out for 2027, does CXMT even need great yields to find China DDR5 buyers?"*
- **OCP floor:** Nvidia opens **Vera Rubin** specs to the ecosystem; **AMD debuts Helion at full rack scale** (agentic AI); Wiwynn shows built Vera Rubin NVL72 + AMD Helion racks; **Nvidia + Cisco introduce USFP pluggable optics**; **Meta discloses IcePack liquid-cooled racks, the 102.4T IceCube switch, MetaRoCE transport + Open Switch Platform Architecture.**
- **Applied Materials opens $5B EPIC Center** (largest-ever capex; world's largest collaborative process-R&D facility) — compresses AI-era dev cycles; targets **doubling sales without proportional headcount**.
- **Credo** ships **1.6T ZeroFlap Active Light Cable** + launches the **SRMO (Short-Reach Micro-Emitter Optics)** OCP standardization workstream (ams OSRAM, Lumentum, Corning, Astera founding). **Lumentum + Corning + Astera** separately demo **1060nm-VCSEL PCIe-over-optics** at OCP. *Vik: micro-emitters = wide-but-slow, closing the 50m copper-to-pluggable gap; SRMO means Credo wants an ecosystem, not a one-off.* *Austin: Credo closed the Hyperlume (µLED) buy a year ago but has pivoted to µVCSELs — µLEDs falling behind.*
- **Sector:** d-Matrix Squadron multi-rack clusters (Nvidia ~$5B stake rumor); **Marvell 2nd-gen Structera X CXL 3.2 — industry-first 6-channel** memory expander; Intel names Crescent Island inference GPU "GPU D-Series"; **SK Hynix H-TPU controller** (100+ small processors, in-memory transaction processing); Kioxia CM9-DLC liquid-cooled SSD; **global smartphone Q3 worst in 14 years on memory shortage**; Hua Hong specialty foundry; **Bloom/LS Electric $138M order → cumulative >$500M** (+LS Materials $51.5M ultracapacitors); Wolfspeed up to $1.5B DoW loan; Infineon 800VDC hot-swap controller; Celestica DS6005 CPO switch; Qualcomm Dragonwing/Wi-Fi 8; Murata MLCC expansion; Intekplus record substrate-inspection order; Tower Q3 earnings Nov 2.
