**Micron halted nearly all construction on Fab 11E (Taoyuan)** — a $3-4.5B DRAM fab (ASML tools, 2028) — over a union pay fight (smaller Taoyuan union wants 15% of op profit, all cash; October's warning strike was Taiwan's chip-industry first). Taiwan makes ~60% of Micron's conventional DRAM → delays capacity in a short market. **Qualcomm** fiscal Q4 near top of guide but **handset −26%, stock −9%**; DC ramp now has a calendar: **$650M+ Q1 FY27 → $7B run-rate end-FY27; $5B FY27, $15B+ FY29**; wins at Amazon (connectivity+accelerators), Meta (CPU); GW-scale deals in talks; raising prices on input costs; **SpaceX Starlink Dragonwing** 5G win. **Arm** rev +27% to $1.44B, licensing +50%, royalties missed (+8%, smartphone weak) but **data-center royalties doubled** (server share >50%→60% by end-2026); own **AGI CPU** POs into 2027, ~$2B FY27-28. **Samsung 'Project Kairos'** — a new 2nm/4nm foundry fab at Giheung for **HBM logic base dies** (2nm for HBM5), ~₩25-30T; the base die is now a foundry part and **Samsung is the only HBM maker that owns a leading-edge logic fab** (SK Hynix uses TSMC). **Coherent** beat + **5-yr ~$20B PhotonLink** supply deal with an unnamed AI company (rev from early 2028). Sector/chatter: AT&S record (AI substrates ~$200/unit); China tungsten drill-bit squeeze; Huawei Kirin 9050 Pro hybrid-bond teardown; YMTC pauses $4.9B IPO; Siltronic raises guide (AI servers 3-4x silicon); **MSFT does NOT double its Nscale Ward County contract**.
