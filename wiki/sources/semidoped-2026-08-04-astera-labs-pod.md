# 🎙️ Semi Doped: Astera Labs (recorded 2026-07-29)

**Educational podcast — no fresh equity call.** Background primer on Astera Labs (IPO'd March 2024 at $36 → $499 by June 2026, joined Nasdaq 100, 93% YoY growth at 76% GM).

## Key concepts
- **The copper problem inside the server:** PCIe signals degrade over PCB traces between GPU↔CPU↔NIC; not a good conducting medium at high speed.
- **Retimer vs redriver:** a **retimer** ([[ALAB]] Aries) fully recovers the clock and *relaunches a clean signal* mid-board (vs a redriver's analog boost). This won the Nvidia H100 socket and put Astera in every AI server.
- **Expansion:** **Scorpio** switch (challenging Broadcom/Marvell in the PCIe/Ethernet switch market); **Leo** CXL memory controller (early design wins); **Taurus** AEC line (active electrical cables — lower margin, using the same signal-conditioning IP, like [[CRDO]] Credo's AECs).
- **UALink vs Ethernet:** UALink is a purer low-overhead scale-up protocol (GPUs access each other's memory); Ethernet-derived fabrics carry legacy overhead. Astera positioned across both. Signal integrity / eye diagrams framed throughout.
