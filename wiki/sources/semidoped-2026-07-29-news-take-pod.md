18-min podcast (Austin & Vik) on the volatile week. (1) **Credit default swaps** — hyperscalers exhausting free cash flow, taking on debt / off-balance-sheet vehicles (Meta/Blue Owl) to fund Nvidia rack buys; widening CDS spreads signal debt-market nerves about **circular financing**. **Gavin Baker counter-take**: spot GPU rental is up to 2x contracted rates (hyperscalers under-earning; contracts will re-price higher as they roll), and end-users (Anthropic first, OpenAI catching up) now make high-margin inference money — so ROI exists and they can self-fund. (2) **SK Hynix carnage** — record profit, ~76% margin, but a modest miss triggered a ~20% drop and dragged the KOSPI; both hosts call it an overreaction — DRAM/HBM demand (bigger models, longer context) is not going away. HBM-vs-DRAM profitability dynamic discussed. (3) **China DUV** — a domestic immersion tool ~20 yrs behind ASML that still can't do leading-edge without extreme tricks (logic folding); the long road to EUV remains. Concept/color episode; the tradeable take is the SK Hynix "overreaction" long.
