Two dailies + a new "News Take" podcast format (Austin Lyons / Vik Sekar).

- **Intel $20B raise:** first public equity sale since 1971, upsized from $15B on demand, priced at $95 (dilution -4-5%). Hosts bullish on the *upsizing* — "think like a VC, they have customers who want wafers; build baby build." Also: **MediaTek commits $5B to Intel Foundry** (Aug-10 daily).
- **TSMC:** record July rev NT$467.58B (+44.7% YoY, +5.6% MoM); 2nm sold out through 2028 at $30k/wafer; CoWoS at Longtan near-balance (Vik confirms from OCP APAC keynote); +$6.3B Sony image-sensor JV.
- **Nvidia $500B "banker" platform** (Apollo/BlackRock GIP/Blackstone/Brookfield/GS/KKR) shifts AI financing onto the financial system; Lambda/JPM debt deals; SEC exempts DC bonds from securitization rules. Vik: "getting out of hand."
- **News Take pod:** the Chinese-transceiver ban is technically bogus (a transceiver is "just a USB cable" — no attack vector; Chinese modules are ~50% of supply and can't be backfilled fast; would *cause* the disruption it claims to prevent), won't stick, and wouldn't immediately lift LITE/COHR (fixed supply). **AMD** Q2 record ($11.5B, +50%; DC $6.7B +107% = 58% of rev) but stock fell on an $800M capex surprise (fabless firm funding substrate/OEM allocation) + GPU-accel-cadence doubts (Stacy Rasgon). **Volta Infrastructure**: Anthropic's $10B deal with a 6-month-old London balance-sheet co (operator ~Bitdeer, Nvidia allocates GPUs) — infra-debt "token factory" financed like a toll road.
- Other: Apple testing CXMT DRAM (DDR5-8800 on AM5); Anthropic chip team (Samsung foundry, ~$1M/yr to teach AI chip design); SK Hynix–SanDisk first HBF spec; Navitas sues Renesas over GaN; SEMCO MLCC +30%. Pub-quiz (Aug-9) = no calls.
