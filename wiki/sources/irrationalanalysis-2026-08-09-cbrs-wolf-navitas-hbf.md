Multi-topic engineering memo.

- **Cerebras (activist SHORT):** WSE4 launch this month is a "gen 3.5" (power delivery + clocks, not a node jump). Feldman bragging to finance people about "doubling clocks with new power delivery" is a tell — the real problem was never defect yield (routing solves that; ~100% functional) but **parametric yield** (hitting target speed at acceptable V/power). IA models ~20% final yield and suspects it was never fixed; asks finance friends to interrogate Feldman on voltage ripple/domains. Holds one precious uncovered share + covered calls (5%/wk on insane IV).
- **Wolfspeed vs Navitas suit:** six patents look weak (first is a GaN patent expiring in ~4mo; Wolfspeed sells no GaN); but Navitas SiC is "utter dogshit" so Wolfspeed is "fine."
- **HBF base-die spec:** softens from "garbage" to "solution looking for a problem that might work but isn't optimal." No UCIe 64G option (NAND PHY is the limit → slow 8G); strange clocking; spec dodges reliability. Same anti-stacking argument as HBM.
- **Aeva:** the LiDAR "shitco" IA mocked launched an optical-connectivity business with a first customer (SOA for NPO/CPO; likely Innolight, hyperscaler maybe Amazon). "HINT HINT LONG LUMENTUM" — high-power laser shortage is real.
- **Taalas (AMD just bought):** burns model weights into upper metal masks (~2-3mo turnaround, custom EDA tooling). IA reads the buy as FPGA/embedded IP, not datacenter disagg-inference.
- **AAOI earnings:** done trading it; CPO high-power-laser talk is cope. High-power CPO laser is a Broadcom+Lumentum duopoly; AAOI "has nothing," is 2-3yr away, and won't show phase-noise/linewidth data.
- **Tower earnings (Openlight):** InP-on-SiPho PDK content rises as InP tightens; Russell dunks GloFo on waveguide insertion loss; 300mm SiGe expansion → "hyper bullish" Semtech (best TIA/driver/CTLE).
- **Micro-LED Jihad:** scorched-earth rebuttal of a SemiAnalysis note — micro-VCSEL is real (32–64G NRZ, ~50% cost cut), micro-LED is "a hoax" (incoherent light = catastrophic phase noise; Avicina fails at 4G, worse than heavy-FEC links). Credo micro-LED reportedly struggling, evaluating micro-VCSEL pivot.
- **Trading account (8/8):** wants to add AEHR/WOLF/AXTI but capping leverage; small NVDA long-calls "in solidarity" with Gavin Baker.
