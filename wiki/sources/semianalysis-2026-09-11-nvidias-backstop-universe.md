# Nvidia's Backstop Universe — Heads I Win, Tails Who Loses?

A balance-sheet forensics piece on Nvidia's off-balance-sheet guarantee stack, from SemiAnalysis's Compute, Capital & Markets desk.

## The disclosure
- As of 2Q F1/27, Nvidia disclosed **$530B of gross off-B/S guarantees** across six line items — up from $184B the prior quarter. Drivers:
  - **Supply/capacity commitments $119B → $279B** (mostly memory; 96% due by F1/29).
  - **Guarantees $3.5B → $108.5B** — SB Energy PORTS-Pike (Ohio), 4.25 GW leased to OpenAI for 20 yrs, Nvidia guaranteeing land/power/shell.
  - New: **$36B AI cloud agreements** (take-or-pay floors under the AICP program) + **$20B datacenter leases** Nvidia signed as tenant to reassign.
- vs **$91B on-B/S liabilities** ($33.4B debt, $25B senior notes issued last quarter). Cash $12B→$22B; investment book $45B→$128B; $99B equity book threw off $23.7B of H1 gains.

## The mechanism & the thesis
- Gigascalers gatekeep the IG capital the buildout runs on; Nvidia manufactures **alternative credit anchors** so neoclouds/neolabs borrow at IG pricing — revenue floors (AICP ≈ $2.35/hr/GPU GB300), landlord guarantees, or signing the lease itself.
- **"Heads I win"**: demand holds → Nvidia earns twice (GPU sale at full margin + rev-share above the floor). **"Tails"**: only bites if a downcycle is steep enough to overwhelm Nvidia's own cash generation *while* backstopped neoclouds fail — correlated but requires both. Risk/reward asymmetric in Nvidia's favor as long as the balance sheet stays strong.
- Firepower is enormous: consensus ~**$441B EBITDA F1/28**, cash modeled to $1.4T by F1/31 vs ~$11T cumulative industry capex CY24–29. Nvidia backstops ~**6.5 GW** today (mostly unbuilt) — dwarfed by Gigascalers leasing ~15 GW (2026) → 35 GW+ (2028), the far larger *implicit* backstop. "Cisco PTSD" is overdone.
- Cost per GW enabled: AICP $59B/GW, PORTS-Pike $25B/GW, but the new **residual-value-guarantee** structure (≤25% RVG under the >$500B PE capital partnership, modeled after the Google-Broadcom-Anthropic-Apollo TPU SPV) only $9.4B/GW. AICP reportedly paused ~2 weeks ago (~$51B to close F1/27).

## Calls
- **[[NVDA]] — LONG @ $218.29.** SA's read is structurally constructive: the backstop firepower is real, the risk asymmetric in Nvidia's favor, and vendor-financing fears overstated vs the Gigascaler-implicit backstop.

Feeds [[ai-debt-financing]] and the bull side of [[buildout-vs-monetization]] — the model's own "tails, we all lose" scenario is the standing demand-risk caveat.
