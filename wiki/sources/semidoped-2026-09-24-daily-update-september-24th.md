- **TSMC locks 2027 wafer price hikes of 3-6%** while pushing A14 toward a 2028 ramp. **Cadence & Synopsys certify A14 flows** (Synopsys adds A14 IP + CPO + agentic-AI design tools); Siemens certifies too; IC-Link/imec joins the OIP Cloud Alliance. Vik: "nobody's pushing back — order visibility runs to 2030, TSMC raises because it can," which hands Samsung/UMC/VIS cover to raise too.
- **Qualcomm renews Apple patent license effective Apr-2027** (revenue floor) and splits its flagship: **Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 + standard Elite Gen 6**, distinct dies, both on TSMC. Amon introduced **High Bandwidth Compute (HBC)** — DRAM stacked on the logic die, low pJ/bit, skips CoWoS but moves the pain to 3D die-stacking. (→ [[advanced-packaging]])

(→ [[custom-silicon-asic]], [[geopolitics-export-controls]])

(→ [[ai-infrastructure-capex]])
