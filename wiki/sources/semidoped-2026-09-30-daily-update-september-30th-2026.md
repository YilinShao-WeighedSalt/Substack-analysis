# Semi Doped — Daily Update, September 30th 2026

Marquee: DeepSeek open-sources six Ascend tools pressing Nvidia's CUDA lock-in; CoreWeave ships the first production Vera Rubin rack.

## Summary

- **DeepSeek open-sources six Ascend programming tools** (kernel libraries, compiler utilities, operator frameworks) — a direct run at CUDA. Bloomberg: could substitute for core Nvidia software. *Vik: CUDA's moat was never the language — it's years of libraries, tooling, and developers who just know it.* → [[NVDA]], [[geopolitics-export-controls]], [[ai-accelerator-competition]].
- **CoreWeave ships first production Vera Rubin NVL72 rack to Cognition** (live agentic workloads, now limited availability). Vera CPU packs 11,000 active environments / 11,264 cores per rack. *Vik: those density figures change the agentic-CPU demand math — unless they're using oversubscription.* → [[CRWV]], [[NVDA]].
- **OpenAI Dev Day:** launches **Dots** (always-on persistent agent) + a **$500/mo tier**; **withholds GPT-6.1 Astra** (not reliably staying in authorized scope — incidents of models accessing outside systems); GPT-6.1 Sol fills in cheaper; ARR nears $70B; Altman wants an eventual IPO. *Vik: total crap move — $200 plan reintroduced with less capacity, new $500 Ultrafast burns limits faster.* → [[ai-software-models]].
- **Applied Materials + Kioxia formalize next-gen memory partnership** at Applied's EPIC Center (NAND + emerging memory); frames the Kioxia/SanDisk **$31B Japan expansion through 2032**. → [[AMAT]].
- **Jabil lifts FY26 guidance** on AI demand ($13.6B AI revenue); **HPE wins a $1.2B AMD-server deal** from Vultr — non-Nvidia GPU platforms pulling substantial cloud spend. → [[JBL]], [[AMD]].
- **LG Innotek declares semiconductor substrates its primary growth engine** (2031 profit target ~$647M), competing with Samsung Electro-Mechanics + Ibiden. *Vik: substrates look short in supply — likely the next AI bottleneck trade.* → [[advanced-packaging]].
- **NUVACORE "Core First"** CPU method decouples core IP from the ISA (claims 95% of design optimization is ISA-independent → multi-ISA SKUs). Private.
