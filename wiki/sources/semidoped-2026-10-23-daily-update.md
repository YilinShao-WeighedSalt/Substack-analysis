A news-dense daily where the AI-bubble tape and the physical supply chain pull in opposite directions.

- **Meta's $34.9B quarter:** single-quarter capex blew past estimates, pushed **FCF negative for the first time in years**, shares −7% (dragging the Nasdaq); 2026 capex guided as high as **$145B**, which drove Meta's credit-risk gauge to a fresh high (spreads widening). **$825B total future spending commitments** disclosed. The debate has shifted from "will AI spend arrive" to "can monetization keep pace with a burn this size."
- **Tesla & Nvidia take equity in SEMCO substrate lines:** Tesla ₩1.1T + Nvidia ₩2.6T (~₩3.7T total) co-funding **dedicated FC-BGA lines** at Samsung Electro-Mechanics' Sejong/Busan plants → allocated output, not queue slots. *Vik: Ibiden still 70%+ of high-end; SEMCO buying up the share table on customer money; Tesla's allocation only lands 2029.* (→ [[advanced-packaging]])
- **TSMC asks suppliers to drop Chinese rare-earth magnets:** first tool-level request of its kind, after Beijing's rare-earth export controls. NdFeB permanent magnets drive wafer-handler/litho motors; China ~95% of finished magnets; requalifying the whole tool base is a multi-year, costly slog. (→ [[semiconductor-equipment]])
- **SK Hynix scraps Solidigm's $10B US IPO:** after Seoul regulators + shareholders pushed back (intended to fund a US NAND fab). SK Hynix shares rallied on the removed dilution; no replacement financing named.
- **JCET $2.73B into advanced packaging:** China's largest OSAT targets chiplet/heterogeneous assembly, into ASE/Amkor's lane for AI-SoC contracts. (China private — not logged as a US ticker.)
- **Short-sellers bank $6B on CoreWeave/neocloud debt fears:** CoreWeave + IREN hit new 2026 lows (CRWV −10% after Jensen flagged an AI funding crunch); long-dated GPU leases on short-term debt; AI-sector credit spreads at a **17-month high**. Blackstone's Jon Gray: can "find a way through." (→ [[ai-debt-financing]])
- Sector watch: **WDC raises dividend + solid AI HDD/flash outlook**; YMTC mass-producing LPDDR5X @9,600 MT/s; Micron unveils global profit-linked bonus after Taiwan union pressure; Daeduck +₩615B substrate capex; Nebius $3.5B Citi bridge; Rapidus courts US AI customers for 2H27 2nm; Merck extends MoO2Cl2 precursor from NAND to DRAM/logic.
