**Nvidia board hires a prosecutor.** Independent directors retained **Joon Kim** (ex-acting US Attorney, SDNY) and **Wachtell Lipton** to run the Huang investigation announced Sunday (a one-sentence statement, no scope/timing). Sen. **Kevin Cramer** (R, Senate Banking) said Huang should resign over extortion risk. Reuters: **Northwestern Polytechnical University** (US Entity List, military-linked) bought two H200 chips in October via a reseller + rented H200 servers. Outside-prosecutor choice signals the board wants distance from Huang's circle. **Earnings Nov 17**; House chairs seek a closed-door briefing. *(This daily predates Huang's Nov-12 resignation.)*

**CoreWeave beats, raises, gets a reprieve.** Q3 revenue **+168% to $3.66B** (vs $3.55B est); FY26 revenue guide raised to **$13.0–13.3B**, adj operating income guide +26% at midpoint; **backlog $132.5B** (from $104B in June); expects **~$9B customer prepayments/deposits** next three months (answers the funding question). Still **lost $709M**, net interest expense **$846M**; FY26 capex guide $37–39B; CFO says build-only-for-signed-contracts would keep 2027 capex ~flat as revenue grows. Stock **+13% premarket**, lifted other neoclouds. Vik: beat/raise quelled cost fears, but **CoreWeave bonds >16% yield = firmly junk / high default risk.**

**Anthropic sets IPO terms.** Amended S-1: **$70–78/share for 825M shares**, raising up to ~$64B, valuing it up to **~$1.4T**; trades on Nasdaq as **ANT**. The filing is a compute ledger: **~$900.9B committed spending**, almost all compute/infra; Microsoft commitment up to **≥$47.2B through mid-2033**; its largest Lambda deal (~$34.9B Nvidia GPU capacity) is now cancellable by either side after Lambda missed a lease deadline (Anthropic says it won't cancel). Vik: datacenter-build backlash could pressure actually securing the leases behind $900B of commitments.

**TSMC buys into Anthropic and OpenAI.** Board approved **up to $500M for Anthropic shares at IPO** + **up to $500M for OpenAI preferred**, plus a separate **$500M** investment company for semi-ecosystem companies. Neither lab buys from TSMC directly (demand flows via Nvidia/AMD/Google/Broadcom), so these are equity stakes at the top of its demand chain with no supply terms. Same meeting: **~$40.9B new capital appropriations** (advanced nodes, packaging, fab build), dividend raised to **NT$9 (from NT$7)**; co-COO Kevin Zhang: leading-node demand **"insatiable."** Vik: rare move; Austin: gives TSMC shareholder facetime to pressure-test end demand.

**AMD 6th-Gen EPYC (Zen 6) shipping.** In systems from server makers now; Azure/OCI instances soon, **Meta the lead customer** at scale. Top SKU **EPYC 9996: 256 cores, 600W, $14,904.** AMD pitches EPYC as its **own AI product** (GPU host nodes, agent sandboxes, general servers); claims up to **3.7× an Nvidia Vera** (AMD's own estimate) and 2.2× Intel Xeon 6980P on an integer benchmark; built on **TSMC 2nm** (ramp started May). Vik: AMD's chiplet fungibility between Ryzen/EPYC lets it actually supply agentic-AI CPU demand — "high hopes for AMD."

**Chatter:** SpaceX **"Kessel" rack** (mini-Kyber) — SemiAnalysis says SpaceX designs a custom Vera Rubin NVL72 rack swapping NVLink cable cartridges for a passive 84-layer PCB midplane, buying chip packages from Nvidia and sourcing the rest. **Nscale** mulls pushing its $35B NY listing to 2027. **Sector:** ASE 4th straight record month (Oct NT$88.6B, +47%; pkg/test +58%); Inventec sees high-double-digit 2027 GPU/ASIC server growth but Q4 shortages (memory/CPU/HDD/passives); **Alibaba T-Head** filed a HK IPO; **OpenAI Q3 rev ~+50% to ~$10B, op loss widened to $10.9B** (WSJ); Korea chip exports **>4× YoY** in first 10 days of Nov to $16.6B (half of all exports).
