Educational episode (Austin Lyons / Vik Sekar) — no fresh priced ticker call; concept color for [[foundry-process-node]] and [[semiconductor-equipment]].

**Core.** After TSMC, Samsung and Intel all publicly committed to a new **6x12-inch photomask standard**, the panel explains why the coordinated move was essential for High-NA EUV adoption. High-NA (0.55 NA) uses **anamorphic optics** (magnifies differently in x vs y), which halves the printable field and creates a throughput problem; the larger 6x12 mask format is the supply-chain fix that made the economics work. Without a coordinated mask-vendor commitment the ecosystem couldn't justify the tooling — hence the joint pledge. Also covers ASML's business model (logic vs memory sales) and the rise of personal AI agents (OpenAI Astra, Instinct, Muse) as a demand driver.
