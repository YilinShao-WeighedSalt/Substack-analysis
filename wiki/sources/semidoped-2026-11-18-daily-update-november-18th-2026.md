**Nvidia beats & raises in its first quarter without Huang:** FQ3 rev **$115.8B (+103% YoY)**, well above ~$109B; DC rev $107.6B; **Vera Rubin ~$20B in its first production quarter**; Q4 guide **$130B ±3%** (extra week, no China DC compute); interim CEO Kress **raises FY28 growth to ~75% from 70%** (the number investors feared would be scrapped); GM 74.8%, guides 71-72% next Q as **the trough** (honoring old POs before new pricing) → back to 72-73% FY28. Board chair Harvey Jones: **no intent to reinstate Huang** (Heidrick & Struggles retained); shares +4% AH. **$390B shopping list:** supply commitments jump to **$390B from $279B**, mostly **multiyear memory (DRAM/HBM)**; inventory $39.7B for the Rubin ramp; **Kinsus grows Nvidia-dedicated ABF >3.5×** (Nvidia funding >$1B); **SPIL** multiyear Nvidia-funded packaging expansion by mid-2029; **SEMCO amends its substrate deal + expands into MLCCs**; neocloud support now "**disciplined and selective**." **Nvidia stops shipping H200 to China** ($2.4B of licensed Hopper last Q; won't sell to DCs renting >50% to Chinese firms; Singapore 22% of billings but ~1% used there). **TrendForce:** HBM4 **$3-5.50/Gb in 2027** (from ~$2.05), avg HBM **+122%**, HBM4 = 51% of HBM shipments; **no conventional-DRAM price cut in any quarter of 2027** (HBM eats 3-4× the wafers); **NAND** contract prices start falling Q2-2027 (2028 oversupply risk: Samsung P5, SK hynix Yongin, CXMT). Chatter: White House presses FCC **ban on new Chinese optical transceivers** despite hyperscaler lobbying; TSMC per-customer credit limits / prepay; **Google moving to buy HBM directly** (bypassing Broadcom's bundle); Honam fab split (Samsung/SK hynix); **SpaceX ~$4B bonds** (first slice of ~$40B Nvidia financing, Goldman/Apollo); Matt Murphy floated as a possible Nvidia successor. Sector: AMD closes $2.9B **Taalas**; MSFT **Cobalt 200** Arm VMs GA (+50% vs 100); Cerebras **disaggregated inference** (AMD prefill + CS-3 decode); CXMT tier-1 long-term deals; Samsung picks 3 DRAM tracks (wafer-bonding HBM5); Lam Zhunan TW center; Siemens buys Breker (EDA); **Credo settles AEC patent dispute with Marvell**; AOI $550M ATM.
