# Are Open Models Catching Up?

The past two months were a breakout for open-source AI: unlike the Jan-2025 "DeepSeek moment" (R1, little real economic use), **GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 do the same coding/agentic work that took Anthropic past $65B ARR**. Fireworks alone processes >40T tokens/day (2× the OpenAI API at end-March). The FUD: if open models stay near-frontier at a fraction of the cost, does the **model layer commoditize** and crush frontier-lab margins?

## Method & finding
SemiAnalysis evaluated models **per era** (benchmarks are era-specific and saturate) and normalized to a composite:
- **Era 1 (early scaling, 2022–24):** Llama-2-70B lagged GPT-3.5 Turbo by 35.8 pts; Llama-3.1-405B closed it (Jul-24); DeepSeek V3 matched GPT-4o (Dec-24).
- **Era 2 (reasoning, 2024–25):** o1 reset the bar; DeepSeek R1 opened only 12.1 pts behind; R1-0528 closed it in 8.5 months.
- **Era 3 (agentic, 2025–now):** Opus 4.5 marks the start (reliability, not raw benchmark — GPT-5.2 scored higher but felt worse; **model + harness** is what matters). Kimi K2.6 passed Opus 4.5 in **4.8 months**; GLM-5.2 cleared GPT-5.2 in 6 months.
- **The trend: open-source catch-up time *halves* every era.** OpenAI/Anthropic now ship a model every ~51 days (vs 213/120 in eras 1–2).

## The "less bearish than you think" caveat
Benchmarks ≠ real work — SemiAnalysis still prefers **Fable 5** day-to-day over higher-scoring Kimi K3 (Claude Code/Claude Tag productization; public benchmarks are hill-climbable via RL environments). Closing time is partly inflated by frontier labs' longer safety-testing windows (GPT-4 finished training 218 days pre-release). Directionally supportive of the closed frontier retaining a productization/harness edge even as raw-capability gaps compress.
