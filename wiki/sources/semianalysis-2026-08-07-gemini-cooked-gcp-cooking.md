Google overhauled DeepMind leadership: Demis Hassabis out of day-to-day; Jeff Dean (started the TPU program) leaves to found neolab **Discovery Loop** with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, Oriol Vinyals; Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over Gemini. SA: **DeepMind is no longer a frontier lab** — Gemini 3 Pro (Nov 2025) was the peak; 3.5 Flash flopped, 3.5 Pro silently canceled, now ~8th–9th on quality. Root cause is culture (bureaucratic, timid), not the departed people.

**The bullish twist for the stock:** GCP won the internal compute war. Thomas Kurian, explicitly not AGI-pilled, sells compute to Gemini's competitors — >20% of 3Q26–4Q27 TPU shipments go directly to Anthropic; hundreds of thousands more committed to Anthropic/Meta. GCP grew **82%** last quarter; TPU-system sales to external SPVs (~$35B/GW, gross-basis) plus >$150B backlog push 2027 GCP growth into the **mid-100s vs 64% consensus**, at mid-to-high-30s EBIT margins. Gemini ARR ~$12B (2Q26) vs GCP's projected >$73B 3P AI IaaS/TaaS + $120B TPU sales by end-2027. Estimated **~+$3 to Google EPS in 2027**; >$250B more TPU bookings could hit RPO.

- [[GOOGL]] LONG $343.80 — GCP financialization; TPU-system sales drive growth/EPS re-rate even as Gemini fades
