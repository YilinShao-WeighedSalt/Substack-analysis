Wednesday Oct 28. Austin & Vik.

- **Broadcom's Hock Tan personally closed the $63B XPU financing for Anthropic** — an SPV funded by global institutions buys the chips and leases them back; banks eye ~$21B in bonds. Converts the S-1's compute-dependency risk into bond-backed hardware with **Broadcom guaranteeing residual values**. Anthropic carried $417B minimum compute obligations at end-2025; Digitimes: ASIC demand favors Broadcom over MediaTek. Austin: a GPU is real collateral (resale market); a custom XPU less so (few buyers) — "rather be in Jensen's shoes if things go south." → NEUTRAL [[AVGO]].
- **SK Hynix record profit + $75B shareholder-return plan** (Bloomberg parses $45B), partly Kioxia-funded; shares +9-10% in Seoul, pulled Samsung up. Memory equities still struggle to hold post-earnings gains. → LONG [[000660.KS]], LONG [[005930.KS]].
- **CXMT starts HBM3E at 20-30% yield** (vs ~60% for profit) — the cost of being cut from leading-edge (EUV) tools; pursues 4F² density while SK Hynix doubles down on EUV-extended 6F². Austin: less embarrassing than the headline (Samsung needed a redesign + most of 2025 for 12-hi HBM3E qual, with every tool it wanted).
- **Oracle loads OCI with Google TPUs, Cerebras, SambaNova** (Cerebras/SambaNova 2027; Cerebras shares +~10%) + expanded Vera Rubin; OpenAI separately weighing TPU buys. Austin: Oracle already lives inside rivals' clouds, so it sells whatever the customer wants. Vik: "swell news for SambaNova (and Intel, CPUs)." → LONG [[GOOGL]] (external TPU pull).
- **ASM International pulls 2030 revenue target to 2027** (+~24%), Q3 beat + Q4 above consensus, on GAA + advanced-packaging ALD demand; Yole: WFE $129B(2025)→$220B(2031). → LONG [[ASM.AS]].
- **SEMCO record Q3 op profit +165% YoY** on AI-server MLCC + ABF substrate; AI-MLCC demand guided 8x by 2027. Vik: intersection of two biggest AI bottlenecks. → LONG [[009150.KS]].
- Sector watch: AMD+Meta strategic partnership ($1B into Meta share sale + warrants for 160M shares); Anduril+Qualcomm "BlueChip" rugged laptops on Snapdragon X; Nebius Q2 rev +454%; Microsoft demos Maia 300 at OCP; Malaysia seizes $12.1M AI chips.
