**TSMC Q3:** net income +69% to NT$763.1B ($23.6B), revenue +51% to NT$1.49T — **sixth straight record quarter**; 2026 revenue-growth forecast **raised to 44%**, capex guide unchanged. C.C. Wei sees no demand slowdown and confirms active **Terafab** (Musk, Texas) talks but drew a firm line: any partnership "**has got to be run by TSMC**." *The Information* adds a planned new 3nm fab. *Austin:* Musk said last week there's zero doubt his companies build+run Terafab; both can't be true — subleasing part of Terafab the likely middle ground.

**ASML:** confirms **first-ever EUV price increases** and is **withholding 2029-2030 order slots**; a 'T'-titled late-decade customer hinted (Culpium → Terafab). Zeiss optics supply chain is the binding constraint (Intesa Sanpaolo); ASML weighing a 2028 facility expansion. *Vik:* when the monopoly starts raising prices, that's your signal; Korean makers already swallowed a 10% parts hike from January.

**Anthropic:** pre-IPO quarterly revenue **$16.8B** (from $11.5B in Q2, ~+46% QoQ) presented at roadshow (branded "thinking" caps, dismissed extinction risk); NTT in talks for a **¥1T Osaka data center** (~500MW) to serve Anthropic Japan — its second overseas site after Australia. Bloomberg: results help the IPO valuation more than the broader AI trade.

**ByteDance / MediaTek:** ByteDance near a chip-supply deal with **MediaTek** for AI data centers — a marquee custom-silicon design win and a non-Nvidia procurement path around US export rules. *Austin:* MediaTek leading-edge comes from TSMC (which stopped ≤7nm AI shipments to China Nov-2024), so likely older-node parts or chips in ByteDance DCs outside China.

**CXMT 4F² DRAM:** China's CXMT to unveil **4F² DRAM before year-end** (mass production after 2027); **Samsung confirms it completed its own 4F² development** — both stake a no-EUV cell-layout approach. Samsung + SK Hynix shares dropped on the combined news (one analyst: "broadly expected"). *Vik:* word is the 4F² cell area ≈ **Korea's current 6F²**, so not the density leap you'd assume. CXMT also won a pledge of official government support.

**CoreWeave:** bonds yielding **>15% (distressed)** after shares fell ~15% (Musk: "Yikes"); CEO Intrator's internal memo calls the selloff irrational, pointing to backlog. DA Davidson's Gil Luria: CoreWeave is "destroying value at every turn" if it borrows at 9% to earn 5%. Gartner warns >$3.5T in AI-infra spend faces write-down risk by 2030.

**Sector watch:** **AEP+Bloom 20-yr 702MW fuel-cell PPA with Google** for a Wyoming DC (AEP $4B investment) — **largest single fuel-cell power deal on record**; Delta 13.8kV SST traction; **Flex closes EPC Power**; IBM+Siemens nanostack 3D transistor; UMC declines Samsung Z Fold8 DDI order (GFS backup); Thintronics targets Ajinomoto ABF film; **SEMI projects DRAM capacity +41% by 2030**; Samsung OCP memory roadmap; SK Hynix conditions new US fab on more China equipment-export approvals; **Salience Labs $125M Series B** (photonic networking); DigiKey $100M; **Lite-On $348M Texas 800VDC** power+cooling.
