Short editorial (~420 words) framing a structural divide in AI business models. **Meta** released **Muse Wonder** (#2 on Artificial Analysis, behind Opus 5.5, ahead of GPT-6 Astra/Gemini 4 Argon/Fable 5.1) and open-weighted **Muse Spark Open** (816B-param MoE, 42B active) that **runs free on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios**. Meta can give it away because it monetizes via **ads** (free on-device inference costs it nothing, wins developers; API + $20/$100 subs are upside). On-device fits **Apple** too — a frontier model needing two top-config Mac Studios *sells Mac Studios* (Apple CEO John Ternus thanked in the launch post, first public Apple-Meta collab since 2017). **OpenAI** is on the other side (earns by serving inference) and its edge device is in federal court (Judge Davila "highly likely" to grant Apple's injunction; dueling trade-secret filings). Treated as a light but directional source.
