# TIL — Dawon Kahng and the first MOSFET

**Educational history — no calls.** ~99.9% of the ~13 sextillion transistors ever made are MOSFETs. **Dawon Kahng** (Bell Labs, b. Seoul 1931) built the first working one in **November 1959**, months out of grad school, using **Mohamed "John" Atalla's** May-1959 fix: grow a clean **SiO₂** layer to quiet the surface traps that had defeated the field-effect idea (Lilienfeld, 1925) for 34 years. Metal-Oxide-Silicon → **MOSFET**. Bell Labs passed — it was ~100× slower than the bipolars going into the phone network — but the device was cheap, few-step, and **tileable side-by-side with no isolation moat** (the property that made integration possible). Kahng later (1967, with Simon Sze) invented the **floating-gate** cell behind every EPROM/EEPROM/flash chip. Jargon-decoder color for the foundational device under the whole stack.
