News-dense daily (Austin & Vik). Marquee items: Amazon–Decart, TSMC cost pass-through, the Vicor ITC expansion, and Nvidia's inference-startup maneuvers.

- **Amazon in talks to buy Israeli AI startup Decart for ~$7B** — model-optimization software that runs on **Trainium**, to squeeze more tokens out of AWS silicon (software-utilization, not hardware). Anthropic walked away from Decart at ~$6B after diligence last month; Amazon now in at $7B. *Vik: "Hesitation is costing them $1B."*
- **TSMC cost increases push Intel CPU prices +20%, Qualcomm ~+10%** (TrendForce); AMD expected to follow — first coordinated CPU hike tied to foundry cost pass-through. *Vik: the 20% lands on Panther Lake parts with the **TSMC N3E** high-end GPU tile, not Intel's own 18A compute tile; TSMC has full pricing power across mobile + AI.* *Austin: "TSMC is charging for its value. As they should."*
- **Vicor expands ITC Section 337 to ~20 respondents** — Foxconn, Luxshare, Quanta, Delta and a broad swath of the AI-server chain — seeking import bans over **vertical-power-delivery (feed from directly under the chip)** patents. Named Infineon and MPS too. **Quanta PT cut to NT$427.84.** Luxshare says its parts aren't in mass production (limited near-term bite). *Austin: an opening for IVR startups (PowerLattice, Ferric) or ADI's Empower?*
- **Nvidia pursues Reflection AI** (American open-weight model dev) + weighs a stake in **d-Matrix** (inference silicon, ~$5B val). *Vik: same NVLink-Fusion playbook — keep inference rivals plugged into the rack; d-Matrix already said it'd hook Raptor up via it; Reflection is probably an acqui-hire.*
- **Super Micro contractor pleads guilty** in the $2.5B AI-chip-diversion-to-China case — first conviction; co-founder Liang's trial Nov 2 (overhang, no logged call).
- **Penguin Solutions** record quarter on AI-memory systems; BofA initiates Buy (module-level memory testing; earnings memory-price-driven).
- **Sector:** Rapidus CORE ecosystem (17 design partners, 2nm) + MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro (2nm) in OPPO Find X10 Pro Max; ASML to raise litho-spare-parts prices; Infineon 27kW 800VDC PSU + SolarEdge/Infineon solid-state breakers; Murata Vietnam inductor expansion; SEMCO AI-server component design-win; Unimicron Hukou substrate expansion; **CXMT 4F² DRAM-cell progress**; TDK weighing HDD-head-unit sale; SK chairman to accelerate Gwangju cluster; **Openreach deploys Ciena WaveLogic 6 for 1.6 Tbps wholesale**; Nokia GPON/AI-RAN; Tata+Besi India packaging; Tencent $5B AI-capex bond, Bridge Data Centres upsizes to $6.6B, Nebius buys Inferize; **Broadcom CEO: the binding constraint is capital availability, not memory supply**; Qualcomm–Arm jury deliberations extend.
