## Summary
News digest built around **hyperscaler earnings silencing AI-capex skeptics**. Microsoft FQ3: **Azure crossed $100B annualized (+43%, fastest in 4 yrs)**, stock +~17% (best day in ~18 yrs), +~$450B market cap in a session (beat Nvidia's one-day record). Amazon: **AWS +37%** (fastest in 18 quarters), total investment plan raised to **$220B**. Both rattled the supply chain up: **SK Hynix +25%, Samsung +20%**, Nvidia/Micron higher. Nadella/Jassy both stressed custom silicon (Maia 200 "+30% tokens/$", Cobalt CPU; Trainium multi-GW commitments from Anthropic + OpenAI, and AWS may *sell* Trainium chips standalone).

**Memory:** Samsung Q2 operating profit KRW 89.5T, chip earnings **~250x YoY** on HBM + enterprise NAND; mobile slipped to a loss; will commit **60-70% of output to LTAs**; second Taylor TX fab this year; tightness to 2028. **Kioxia** 45x profit surge but *missed* + 3:1 split, received coolly. **Qualcomm** FQ3 rev $9.947B with weaker earnings (memory-price inflation on smartphones), raising prices portfolio-wide from September — the **mobile-vs-datacenter barbell**.

**Packaging:** TSMC developing an **EMIB-like organic-substrate bridge with Kinsus** to counter Intel packaging + CoWoS tightness; Vik skeptical on capacity/timing (Intel EMIB-T mass-prod 2027). Samsung reportedly in a **$200B foundry deal with Broadcom**, HBM4 base-die as its differentiator.

**China memory:** **CXMT $590B Shanghai debut** after a $4.4B IPO (MSCI fast-track), ~**7.67% of global DRAM**; Senators Schumer/Cotton warned Apple off sourcing CXMT/YMTC; Chinese chip stocks sold off on rotation. Hosts: export-control blockade *prevents* the feared price flood → big-3 keep their stranglehold.

**Power:** **Bloom +25.3%** on a Q2 beat + raised guide (fuel-cell AI-DC contracts); Crusoe+Aalo building first nuclear-powered AI DC (Idaho, 2027 demo); Schneider raised DC outlook 2nd time this year; Delta lifted capex; Antora raised $550M. **MPWR** Q2 adj EPS +54% YoY, Q3 guide ~$1.14B.

**Sector watch:** Renesas DDR5 MRDIMM growth; Seagate 50TB HAMR 2027; AMD 15-yr $14B Core Scientific lease; UMC mature-node fab expansions; SEMCO/SoulBrain glass substrate; Corning targets $10B CPO/silicon-photonics TAM.
