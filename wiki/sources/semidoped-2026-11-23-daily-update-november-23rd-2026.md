Nvidia's week ends with an SEC probe and a weekend of White-House pressure to bring Jensen back.

- **SEC looks at Nvidia's accounting; POTUS asks for Jensen's return.** NVDA fell 14% Friday to $125.53 after Reuters reported SEC enforcement staff are scrutinizing accounting issues. Two areas drawing scrutiny: the switch to reporting revenue by customer HQ (Singapore — ~half of global revenue per FT — dropped out of the geo breakdown) and **circular financing** (Nvidia backing customers that buy its chips; Kress said Nvidia will be "far more selective"). Trump wants Huang to "apologize and go back"; Bessent softened ("Huang is on the outside now"). → [[nvidia-gpu-platform]].
- **Korea's chip exports nearly quadruple** — $38.5B in the first 20 days of Nov (49.7% of all Korean exports), yet Samsung/SK hynix shares ~half their peaks on fears of contract cuts. Hankyung checks: **no cuts** — neither Nvidia nor Google asked to cut/delay '27; both sold out for 2027; >half of 2028 under LTAs. Samsung lifting Xi'an X2 Gen-9 NAND to 50-60k wpm (from ~10k). → supports memory bull but shares price the peak fear.
- **AI server makers want up to 45% more than quoted** — Dell/HPE/Lenovo/Supermicro asking 25-45% above prices agreed months ago; memory costs +400% since early 2024 (JPMorgan). Dell's fixed $549M XTX deal → asked +$62-70M; XTX sued. → [[ai-infrastructure-capex]].
- **SoftBank shopped a ~$1T SK hynix buyout** (no longer active per spokesperson); EE Times reads it as a strategic sounding — memory the one AI layer SoftBank (Arm/Ampere/Graphcore/OpenAI/Stargate) doesn't hold. → strategic-interest datapoint for [[000660.KS]].
- **CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler for $43.9B** (all-stock, 1.135 CRWD/sh ≈ $260.41, 34% premium); >$10B ARR at close. Vik: "good acquisition" (little product overlap). → NEUTRAL [[CRWD]] (off-core; editorial aside).
- Nvidia server partners pause SE-Asia shipments under the new controls; Trump threatens "a tremendous tax" on overseas DCs. Chatter: Google cut '27 TPU-8i ~1M units (~20%), leaning on MediaTek 8t; Broadcom −12% Fri; Apple hiring silicon-photonics/CPO engineers; Tesla memory doubled AI4 cost, cut AI4.5 to 24GB.
