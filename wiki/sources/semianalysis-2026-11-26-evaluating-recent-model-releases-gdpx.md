Educational — no priced ticker call. SA scores the November model wave on **GDPx**, its own frontier-domain benchmark (206 tasks across 11 high-value domains: chip design, cyber, bio, finance, legal, quant, RSI, hardware, materials, alignment, SWE). Difficulty is the point — Opus 5.5 topped release day at just **12%**, leaving headroom to discriminate future models (vs saturated benchmarks).

- **Leaderboard:** OpenAI **6.2 Astra #1 (19.3%)**, Anthropic **Fable 5.2 #2 (16.4%)**, best non-OpenAI/Anthropic = **Gemini 4 Carbon 5th (10.9%)**, Neon 6th (10.1%). OpenAI dominates the cost-performance Pareto frontier; DeepSeek v4.1 Flash sneaks the low end. Qwen4-Max is the top Chinese model (7.44%, ≈ Sonnet 5.5 but cheaper); Kimi K3.1 Pro underperforms vs its Terminal-Bench benchmaxxing.
- **Tiers:** Anthropic + OpenAI still "a tier of their own"; **Google a distant third but its own tier** (DeepMind inflected up — SA walks back its earlier "Gemini is cooked" call); Meta/SpaceX/Moonshot/Alibaba fighting for 4th. Twitter's love of Gemini 4 Neon is "model behavior, not intelligence" (pleasant like 4o); 6.2 Astra is smart but unpleasant/literal and weak at SWE (where X evaluates). → mild positive read-through for [[GOOGL]] (DeepMind resurgence), no priced call.
- **Cost/caching:** Neon is expensive to run because Google's prompt caching lags (Neon GDPx bill $15,286 vs ~32% less at Astra's cache hit rate); Google shipped a caching update narrowing it. Fable 5.2 is the most expensive model (persistence + subagents). → [[ai-software-models]].
- **Safety classifiers** sandbag Astra/Fable in cyber/bio/alignment; domain-specific/custom cyber post-trains (Hinoki HO1, abliterated Kimi/DeepSeek) becoming relevant as labs crack down — abliterated models scored slightly worse than vanilla OSS.
