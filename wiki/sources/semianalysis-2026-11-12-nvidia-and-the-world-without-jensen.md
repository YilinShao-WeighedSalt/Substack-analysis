SemiAnalysis's structural read on **Jensen Huang's forced resignation** (after only four days as the board probe became public). SA frames it as a genuine conduct issue (relationships with a Chinese-CCP-linked customer and with subordinates) **plus** a Washington power play: SA's Accelerator Model tracks **>1.5GW of restricted Nvidia chips** that still reached China, Commerce now agrees, and SA believes the US intelligence community pushed Jensen out to clear the path for **RASA (Remote Access Security Act)** and much harsher China controls now that AI's "biggest defender" is gone. SA argues poorly-shaped controls backfire (cutting China off only accelerates its move to domestic Ascend/DeepSeek stacks, and kills the open-source feedback loop — MSA/DeepEP/DeepGEMM/FlashKDA — that western startups depend on); Biden-style diffusion rules (remote access by trusted clouds, no in-China export) make more sense than RASA.

**The core investment question — what is Nvidia without Jensen:**
- **Built for one king.** ~60 direct reports, no 1:1s, ~42k employees, flat org — fast when Jensen decides, but **no deep successor bench**. Only Ian Buck (father of CUDA) and Jonah Alben (SVP GPU eng) are plausible internal technical CEOs, and SA doubts either can run the whole hardware + supply-chain empire. Colette Kress is interim CEO (a finance CEO "cannot be" the answer). An exec quote: "Ian would rather die than succeed Jensen."
- **Founder bets die under professional managers.** CUDA, tensor cores, Omniverse/AV "zero-billion-dollar bets" needed a founder with board cover; a successor answerable to shareholders takes fewer decade-long risks.
- **"Customers bought Jensen, not just GPUs."** Jensen personally decided hyperscaler/neocloud allocation and backed the **neocloud backstop** ("the Jensen put"): Nvidia disclosed **$530B gross off-balance-sheet guarantees** (up from $184B QoQ), incl. $36B take-or-pay floors, and a $99B equity book. Without the Jensen put, SA sees a possible **large contraction** in the neocloud ecosystem.
- **Supply-chain stranglehold loosens.** Jensen personally locked up Korea HBM and Taiwan logic CEO-to-CEO; SA estimates Nvidia gets **~$3.0–3.3/Gb HBM4 from SK Hynix vs $3.7–4.1 for AMD/Broadcom/Amazon/Google**, with implicit reciprocal GPU allocation. Without him suppliers de-prioritize Nvidia → SA thinks **>70% of total 2028 capacity could be non-Nvidia.**
- **Loses AI's open-source spokesman.** Nvidia propped up US open models (Reflection, Thinking Machines, Poolside, Hugging Face) and quietly co-developed with Chinese labs on Blackwell; a manager-led Nvidia may deprioritize Nemotron and the open ecosystem — good for closed labs, bad for startups.

Enforcement context: two smuggling cases detailed — **SuperMicro/Wally Liaw $2.5B** (US v. Liaw, SDNY; dummy-server audit fraud) and the **Keelung, Taiwan B300 ring** (9 indicted incl. an Nvidia Taiwan senior manager; 130 B300 servers, 74 reached China). SA's "why now": remove Jensen's opposition before a lame-duck Senate push on RASA (passed House 369-22).

Tone on NVDA is **structurally bearish** (not a formal short): Nvidia "would still be materially better off with him there." Read-through mildly **positive for competitors** — AMD, Broadcom, Google/Amazon custom XPUs — as the stranglehold and allocation power loosen.

## Calls
- **[[NVDA]] SHORT** (structural/cautionary) — founder-built company with no successor bench; supply-chain, HBM-pricing and neocloud-backstop advantages were personal to Jensen and loosen with him gone; China hawks/RASA ascendant; SA models >70% of 2028 capacity non-Nvidia.
- **[[AMD]] / [[AVGO]] / [[GOOGL]] / [[AMZN]]** — positive read-through (context, no priced call): competitors ship higher volume as Jensen's supply lock-ups and allocation preferences ease.
- **[[000660.KS]]** (SK Hynix) — context: implicit Nvidia-priority HBM4 pricing may normalize toward Tier-1 levels without Jensen.
- **[[ARM]]** — context: Rene Haas (Nvidia alumnus) floated as a credible external CEO candidate.
