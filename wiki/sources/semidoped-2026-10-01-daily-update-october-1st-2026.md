# Semi Doped — Daily Update, October 1st 2026

Marquee: **Micron's record FY26**; Synopsys' $1B Amazon IP pact + GPT-Synopsys; Google ships Gemini 4 as TPU revenue recognition begins.

## Summary

- **Micron posts record FQ4/FY2026**; current-quarter revenue guide tops estimates; record backlog. **DRAM now commands a ~54% price premium over TSMC 2nm silicon**; Reuters: the supply gap is **structural, not cyclical**. *Vik: fabs steer wafer starts to HBM (~5× DDR5 per-bit), so commodity DDR5 tightens alongside — I don't see the peak yet. Austin: hence Micron's 87% margins.* → [[MU]], [[memory-dram-cycle]].
- **Synopsys lands $1B+ Amazon silicon-IP pact** + announces **GPT-Synopsys** with OpenAI (rev-share "pay for results," engineer-level RTL→physical). Cadence has no announced equal. *Vik: AI-driven EDA is a big deal — repetitive design work gets reshored and automated; bad for offshore design houses, expect layoffs.* → [[SNPS]], [[eda-chip-design]].
- **Google ships Gemini 4 Argon** after delays (Bloomberg: internal skepticism on coding quality); **TPU revenue recognition begins against a $513.9B Cloud backlog**; AI chip capacity already sold out. *Vik: vibes of playing catch-up; model fatigue, I'll pass on Gemini 4. Austin: I'll kick the tires regardless.* → [[GOOGL]], [[custom-silicon-asic]].
- **Nebius buys Inferize** ($100–150M, 10-mo-old stealth inference-efficiency startup) + **12-yr binding 50MW SE-US capacity** (AIB Data Centers). *Vik: hardware utilization is critical while compute is short — direct revenue.* → [[NBIS]].
- **Huawei Mate 90 / Kirin 9050 Pro** uses **Tau logic-folding** to add compute density without leading-edge nodes; Mate XT 2 tracking ~1M pre-orders; flags phone price hikes on tight memory. *Vik: logic folding is really a hybrid-bonding bet — stack a SMIC 7nm-class die on itself, doubling density by burning 2× silicon (not EUV making denser transistors).* → [[geopolitics-export-controls]].
