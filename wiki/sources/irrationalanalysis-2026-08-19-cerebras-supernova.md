Recap of the Cerebras "Supernova" launch event, written while the author's one-share activist SHORT campaign against management continues. He reiterates the core bear point in an exchange with a fellow shareholder: Cerebras solved *catastrophic* yield (100% of wafers function) but **parametric yield** — at what clock/power each wafer runs — is unclear, which is why he models **~20% total compound yield** and posts the editable Excel GM model publicly.

On the new system: three wafers (same WSE-3) in one much more efficient box; **double I/O** (he suspects a doubled NoC/I-O clock, possibly two-edge signaling), latency down from ~5µs but "still terrible, less terrible than before." A comment implied an **IO ASIC replaced the FPGA** (unconfirmed). Doubled clock is "not interesting by itself" but *implies parametric yield is a lot better*. Verdict: "generally happy with the progress," I/O "meaningfully improved," but **not enough info to update the gross-margin model** — cannot say whether 60%+ hardware GM is reachable. Wants more at Hot Chips.
