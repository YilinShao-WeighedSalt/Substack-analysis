## Summary
A market-memo framed around two "degenerate gambler heroes." **Bill Hwang** (Archegos, 2021) opens as the historical template: a family office that used **total return swaps (TRS)** across ~6 prime brokers to build a hidden, hyper-concentrated, levered book that detonated when ViacomCBS/Discovery cracked — the banks never compared notes. The main event is **Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness LP (SALP)**, the AGI-thesis fund that IA argues *caused* late-July's "bubble-pop-level" violence in semis.

IA's mechanics: SALP ran ~4-5x leverage, long AI winners (memory — Kioxia, SK Hynix; Bloom) and short "AI losers" (traditional SaaS), but the shorts were anti-correlated with the longs → not factor-neutral, so they were *more* AI-factor leverage, not a hedge — "pod leverage with a psychotic family-office's risk rules." The community knew SALP was massively long **Kioxia (285A.T)** and **SK Hynix (000660.KS)**. Chronology: rumors week of Jul 22 → Kioxia craters Mon Jul 27 → SK Hynix record Q2 but *misses consensus* Jul 28 and convulses → FT (Jul 29 evening), then CNBC/WSJ confirm steep losses → Citadel buys the book ("hedge-fund seppuku"). WSJ: **SALP -67% in July**. Thursday Jul 30 was a violent green recovery once the forced selling finished; Friday up but faded on Iran-war fears. IA de-levered a bit after Jul 31.

**Trading lesson:** prime brokerage lacks the cross-lender concentration check consumer credit has. IA's own psychotic trading account is **+56.8% YTD** vs the large-cap semi index **+50.1% YTD** (SALP still outperformed IA YTD before the blowup).

**State of book (STONKS):** rebuilding the trading account, "very fluid."
- **Ultra-core:** Lumentum, Intel, Tower.
- **Core:** Bloom, Semtech.
- **AXTI a "giga-long"** — collapsed a month before everything else because China banned TRS, turning HK/China institutions into mini-Leopolds forced to unwind.
- **AEHR** high-beta, "smash-hit" earnings/guide; **Ciena / Nokia** long-haul optics (can't decide sizing); **AAOI** US transceivers, "lots of torque" but violent; **one CBRS share** for an activist campaign (Cerebras "deserves a dedicated post").
- **Long-only accounts:** happy with everything except wants a lot more **Samsung** — prefers it over SK Hynix for DRAM, betting on internal-foundry + design synergy to co-design the **HBM4 base die** better than SK Hynix.
