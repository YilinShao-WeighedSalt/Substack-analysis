News roundup with Austin/Vik takes.

- **Applied Materials record quarter:** rev **$9.12B (+25% YoY)**, non-GAAP EPS **$3.50 (+41%)**; EPIC Center R&D partnerships up to 11 — but shares slipped ("sold the news"). Lam Research committing >$3B to expand its global lab network. Korean supplier ISTE (HBM FOUP cleaning) record rev +401% YoY. Vik: WFE vendors now have an **8-quarter rolling visibility window** to 2030 (unusual — industry normally sees 2–3 quarters). Austin: the window exists so ~2,000 downstream suppliers can add capacity; Applied's CFO: deposits exist but "not across the board," pricing on 2–3-yr project contracts moving slowly. Contrast: Ibiden's ¥500B ABF expansion is *customer-funded* (advance payments) — Austin argues that's a sign of *weaker* power (customer risk transfer), and he'd rather be AMAT.
- **SanDisk Investor Day:** model projects **mid-to-high-teens revenue growth FY28–30**, leading NAND roadmap, committed to returning **100% of excess cash** post-capex. Asian memory names rose on it. BiCS 10 = 332-layer 2Tb QLC; interesting HBF roadmap (slide 83+).
- **China domestic stack posts records:** SMIC first >$3B quarter, net profit +261.7% to $479.2M on mature-node demand, raising wafer prices; Hua Hong Grace 1H net +404%. **CXMT overtook Tencent to become China's most valuable listed company** (~$524B, 17 days after IPO), DRAM yield ~2 pts behind Samsung; China AI-chip market ~90% domestic in 2026 (Huawei on course to displace Nvidia). CXMT market cap now rivals Intel's.
- **Anthropic** in talks to buy Nvidia-backed **Decart AI for $6B** (fast/efficient inference); targeting 77% GM pre-IPO and a record $2T IPO valuation.
- Sector watch: SK Hynix Aug-27 groundbreaking on Indiana packaging fab (Jensen expected); Kioxia PCIe Gen6 optical SSD trials; Samsung Electro-Mechanics delays glass substrate MP beyond 2028; AMD raises up to $5B debt; Cerebras raises 2026 outlook on inference-services mix even as hardware softens; Vantage exploring $100B IPO/sale; L&T deploys 10k Nvidia B300 for Together AI in Chennai; Sivers + SemiNex $3.4M InP light-source program; Google/MSFT/Nvidia back an 800V-DC power standard.
