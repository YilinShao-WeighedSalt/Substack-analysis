# Semi Doped Daily — September 4, 2026

News-dense daily. Lead items: OpenAI ships GPT-6 Astra, a Korean court confirms CXMT's Project Hefei lifted a 620-step Samsung recipe as CXMT nears 10% DRAM share, Ciena +37% rev, Kioxia pitches high-speed NAND as a DRAM substitute, Figure AI commits $3.5B to Nscale for robotics.

- **OpenAI GPT-6 Astra — "welcome to the AGI era."** Claims SOTA across computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, professional work; folds "aligned model" into the top-line marketing for the first time. Safety researchers flag a "significant jump in cyber capabilities." *Vik: skeptical of the Iron-Man/Jarvis framing. Austin: optimistic, has tasks queued to test it.* → [[ai-software-models]].
- **CXMT reaches 10% DRAM share; Samsung theft case exposes Project Hefei.** A Korean court found CXMT's Project Hefei lifted a 620-step Samsung manufacturing recipe (written roadmap to transfer process know-how). CXMT hit **10% of global DRAM revenue in Q2 2026** — a threshold that took Samsung/SK Hynix decades; recruiting DDR5/LPDDR talent in Japan. US separately warns Korea to accelerate the HBM4 pivot (China's gap widest there). *Vik: CXMT's gain came at SK Hynix's expense mainly because SK diverted capacity to HBM — it says nothing about SK Hynix quality or China exposure.* → [[memory-dram-cycle]].
- **Ciena rev +37% to $1.67B, backlog nearing $10B.** FQ3 EPS +215%; DCI + AI network buildout drove one of its strongest quarters. Supply (not demand) is now the binding limit — order backlog to exceed $10B by FY-end as optical-component capacity trails orders. CEO cites a multi-year AI-infra investment cycle. Shares fell ~10% on in-line forward guidance. *Vik: optical demand still strong; the current optical-stock slowdown is supply-limited, structurally more optics ahead.* → [[silicon-photonics-interconnects]].
- **Kioxia bids to replace DRAM with high-speed NAND.** Pitches high-speed NAND as a practical DRAM substitute in AI memory hierarchies (inference where latency tolerance is loose, $/GB matters); Micron + YMTC closing on the same positioning (Digitimes). Together the moves tighten commodity DRAM from demand + substitution sides. *Vik: heterogeneity of workloads means NAND takes a real share of the inference stack alongside DRAM. Austin: "substitute for DRAM" should read "in certain situations" — future bright for both.* → [[nand-flash-storage]].
- **Figure AI commits $3.5B (→$6B+) to Nscale GPU cloud for robotics.** Up to 100k GPUs on Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform to train humanoid-robot workloads in-house. One of the more concrete dollar commitments from a humanoid company to purpose-built AI infra. → [[robotics-embodied-ai]] / [[nvidia-gpu-platform]].
- **Nvidia PAIR beta** routes local AI inference across RTX / DGX Spark / Mac (M4+) into a shared local cluster (Ollama + LM Studio), no internet once models are downloaded. → edge inference, [[nvidia-gpu-platform]].
- **Sector Watch:** SEMI reports global equipment billings +23% YoY Q2 2026 (broad CapEx wave); ASML Japan +40% workforce by 2030 (Rapidus/TSMC); TSMC boosts Kaohsiung testing; SEMCO eyes ~30% MLCC price hikes (ABF also rising); Vishay thin-film RF resistor; POET high-power AI-interconnect lasers at CIOE/IFOC; Flex acquires EPC Power (high-density conversion); Lite-On $170M into DCX liquid cooling; DeepSeek to order 160k Huawei chips (Mongolia DC); Nvidia sets Oct RTX Spark launch; Qualcomm Adreno (Matrix Cores/Neural Fusion); Zeiss SMT: China ~15yr behind in top tools, UBS puts China EUV at ASML's 2004 level; China rare-earth firms halt some US shipments; YMTC steps toward a HK IPO; Crusoe $3B Series F ($30B val); Meta activates $1.2B Idaho DC; HUMAIN weighs $2.5B DC fund; Thailand holds 49 DC projects on power/water strain.
