Educational interview with R K Anand (co-founder/CPO of **Tensordyne**, founding Juniper engineer). Tensordyne builds an inference chip using **logarithmic math** (turning matrix-multiply into add-based ops) for power efficiency, staying **below the reticle limit** to pack more SRAM+HBM on a 3nm chip; a **30 kW, 72-chip quarter-rack** balancing prefill+decode, using **HPE Juniper's** router fabric as a battle-tested low-latency scale-up interconnect (+ Broadcom partnership). Framed via the Cisco→Juniper analogy (startup vs incumbent in a new-market technology shift). No public tickers → **no calls** (HPE/Broadcom mentioned as partners, not investment theses).
