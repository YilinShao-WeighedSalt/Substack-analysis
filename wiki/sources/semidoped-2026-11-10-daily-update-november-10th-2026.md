**Nvidia's lawyer contacted the Megaspeed investigators.** Bloomberg/NYT: Nvidia GC **Tim Teter** called Commerce Under Secretary Jeffrey Kessler (late summer 2025) about the Megaspeed smuggling probe, then emailed BIS investigators directly claiming permission; one declined a meeting, Kessler told staff to go "by the book." DOJ later asked Commerce to pause; it's now a **criminal investigation at DOJ**. Nvidia says Teter didn't know of Huang's affair. Bloomberg: trade records show Nvidia's chip count to Megaspeed came up **~50,000 units short** (Nvidia says it later inspected warehouses). Megaspeed still an Nvidia Cloud Partner. Story shifts from a CEO's private life to **whether Nvidia leaned on the export-control system** — the version Congress cares about. A **shareholder class action** was filed in San Jose five minutes after Monday's close, when **NVDA had fallen 4.3%** (−~$190B market value). House Democrats want tighter China rules.

**Google adds two TPUs; AMD gets the host socket.** Google added **TPU 8tx** and **TPU 8i Max** to its 8th-gen lineup (now **four new TPU systems this year**). 8tx = same silicon as 8t + **hardware confidential computing**, runs on **5th-Gen AMD EPYC host CPUs**; 8i Max = **two TPU 8i dies in one package** (doubles compute, SRAM, HBM bandwidth per rack). No pricing/dates. Google is splitting one TPU generation into four job-specific systems (training → secure inference → long-context agents); 8i Max is also a packaging+HBM story. **AMD says EPYC is now the host CPU at >10 hyperscalers and frontier labs** across GPU and custom-ASIC systems; **AMD +13% Monday.**

**TSMC posts another record month.** October revenue **NT$586.89B, +59.7% YoY, +14.7% MoM** (beats August's record NT$514.81B); 10-month revenue **+43.3%** vs ~44% FY guide — already running above the pace to hit the top of Q4 guidance (NT$1.66–1.73T). Taiwan Oct chip exports **+63% to $31.17B**; total exports a record $96.76B. Vik: with Intel struggling, the world has no TSMC alternative. Austin: "+15% MoM!!!"

**Infineon — DC power sales to nearly double.** Guided **FY2027 revenue ~€20B** (+>20% from €16.35B); **data-center power-supply revenue ~€4B** (from €2.2B, incl ~€500M conventional DC); AI-power revenue alone €1.7B last year (>2× prior, still couldn't meet demand); **>€10B** of signed/negotiating multi-year supply + capacity-reservation deals. Caveat: FCF guide ~€1.8B (below €2.26B est) on €3.0–3.5B fab spend (Dresden/Kulim/Villach). **Shares +9%.** Vik: power semis the next dominant AI-buildout factor.

**Rapidus reportedly behind on 2nm.** Bloomberg: hasn't shown working **SRAM cells** (a summer milestone) → ≥several months late; dust/particle contamination in the Hokkaido cleanroom. Rapidus insists 2H-FY2027 (ends Mar-2028) mass production holds. Japan has committed >¥2T; a long delay risks that support and its planned IPO. Austin: skeptical anyone ramps 2nm from scratch to volume.

**Chatter/sector:** Taiwan sources (Commercial Times) — **Nvidia may slow TSMC/ASE orders** amid the Huang controversy, freeing capacity for **AMD, Broadcom, MediaTek, Alchip**; chassis/rack/cooling suppliers see no order change. Anthropic IPO filing (later corrected) showed $69.3B IPO + $26B private placement from Google/Nvidia/AMD/Broadcom/Micron/Microsoft/TSMC. **Moonshot AI** renting 32 Super Micro B300 servers inside China (Aug, ¥432M; Moonshot disputes). **Samsung 3D Cube-H** hybrid-bonded logic stacking (~6µm pitch) + first design kit early 2027. **SoftBank** swung to a ¥1.1T ($7B) loss on a $3.7B power-equipment writedown. **Onto Innovation** record Q3 $409.7M (+88%), guides Q4 $430–450M. Siemens buys Lauterbach (debug/trace). Unimicron/Kinsus 7th straight record month (+68%/+49%).
