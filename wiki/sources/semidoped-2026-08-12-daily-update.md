News roundup with Austin/Vik takes.

- **TSMC CoWoS yield >99%** (5.5-reticle), 3DIC dev cycle cut to 1 year — but TSMC itself flags **ABF substrate + memory** as the next AI-chip bottlenecks. Nvidia holds ~60% of the packaging queue; Microsoft Maia 300 seeking 300k units. TSMC pursuing two AUO fabs (>TWD 30B) for FOPLP/CoPoS (one Longtan site possibly 1.4nm); $4.7B Sony image-sensor JV in Japan. Austin: substrates clear first (ABF line = a few hundred $M, 18–24 mo, no EUV) vs a DRAM fab ($15–20B+, 3 yrs).
- **Camtek** (adv-packaging inspection) orders top **$600M**; expects adv-packaging revenue +70% Q1→Q4 — the read-through on the packaging ramp.
- **CXMT DDR5 yield >90%** on 17nm (2 pts behind Samsung); fastest-ever MSCI inclusion (14 days). Apple testing CXMT DRAM in iPhone/MacBook amid the DRAM shortage, but export-control + compliance "dual barriers" limit adoption to China/emerging markets. SK Hynix reportedly reviving China NAND expansion; Apple also eyeing YMTC NAND.
- **Foxconn +35% quarterly profit** on AI-server demand (assembles for Nvidia) — a direct read-through on sustained AI infra spend. Rival Compal opens Daxi AI-server plant (doubles capacity, targeting 30–40% AI rev by 2027). Wistron CTO: **power, not GPUs, is now the binding constraint** (standing up 10MW in Tainan).
- **Power politics:** EIA sees record US power consumption 2026 & 2027; Chicago + Salix IA data-center moratoriums; Amazon 7.65GW Texas gas plant drawing fire; Meta launches $1B host-community fund. Austin: rural-town-hall "moratorium" headlines are snowballing — someone needs to own the narrative.
- Sector watch: ChipMOS best revenue since 2014; Samsung/SK Hynix rally on Temasek HBM optimism; Nanya to become ASML EUV customer; Silicon Labs $228M Q2; Avaco TGV glass-substrate pilot line.
