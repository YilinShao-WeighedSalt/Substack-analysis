A 7.3k-word slide-by-slide OCP Global Summit 2026 recap. **NPO in the Open CPX format** stole the show from last year's CPO. Core engineering arguments: integrated-laser NPO is "a terrible idea" (lasers are the #1 link-flap cause; Innolight/Terrahop's Diablo ships 4 cold-spare lasers = tacit admission); **slow-and-wide 32-64G NRZ direct-drive** beats heroic 224G/448G retimed SerDes (448G "a disaster" — package-core/BGA reflections); PAM4 is the practical optical ceiling, IA wants PAM8 + strong FEC. Exposed widespread **TDECQ cheating via waveform averaging** — called out Ciena, Innolight/Terrahop, Marvell, Macom.
