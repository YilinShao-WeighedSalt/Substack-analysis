AI's data glut spills past HDD economics into cheap/slow tape; the kicker is **long-term + cyber-secure offline storage** (properly stored tape ~30yr vs HDD/SSD bit-rot in 3–5yr; air-gapped HDD lube/mechanicals degrade). → [[QMCO]].

Quick opinions ahead of a long-promised **practical parametric-yield deep-dive** (now top of queue because CBRS is disproportionately harmed by the phenomenon): **moderately bearish until end of lockup (~December)**; **potentially extremely bullish H1-2027→H1-2028**; long-term bullish or bearish depending on the 3D SRAM/DRAM program. IA's pointed advice: **pivot from 3D DRAM to 3D SRAM wafer hybrid bonding** (cites d-Matrix's Hot Chips presentation on why wafer-scale 3D DRAM is "suicidal"). → [[CBRS]].
