News-heavy daily by Austin Lyons & Vik Sekar. Three lead stories plus a wide Sector Watch.

**China's chip makers claim pricing power.** SMIC beat Q2; CEO Zhao Haijun says AI-driven demand is "far exceeding" expectations and the foundry may add equipment at existing sites. China revenue +22%; signaling further wafer price hikes as AI-peripheral orders swamp mature-node supply. Hua Hong reading the same signal — Morningstar raised fair value on both Chinese foundries. **CXMT** crossed $500B market cap (past Tencent, China's most valuable public company) and **rejected Apple's DRAM price-cut request**; Commerce Secretary Lutnick publicly opposed Apple sourcing memory from CXMT. **YMTC overtook Kioxia for #3** in global NAND shipments. Hygon rev +67%, Sugon profit +34%. CXMT DDR5 now at 9,000 MT/s / CL28 at DDR5-6000 — narrowing the gap to SK Hynix/Samsung. Micron won dismissal of a YMTC defamation suit. *Austin: can't compare CXMT apples-to-apples with the Big 3. Vik: memory still underpins AI compute (CXL/pooling matters, not just HBM).*

**Nvidia guarantees 4.25 IT-GW at OpenAI's Ohio campus, invests $1.5B in SB Energy.** Nvidia secured land/power/shell at SB Energy's PORTS-Pike campus (Pike County, OH), exclusively hosting Nvidia compute; credit support covers an initial 4.25 IT-GW with an option on the remaining 3.75 — confirming last week's report that Nvidia had **cut its guarantee by >50%** (from 8 IT-GW). OpenAI is the customer for all 8 IT-GW on a 20-yr lease; SB Energy builds/owns/operates. $1.5B Nvidia investment (down from up-to-$3B reported). At least 10 GW generation → 8 IT-GW (a 2 GW gap). $4.2B regional grid infra via AEP Ohio. *Austin: Nvidia is backing the datacenter itself — savvy; if OpenAI falters, re-rent the cluster.*

**Anthropic courts IPO investors at >$2T valuation** on a Reuters-reported $190–200B 2028 revenue forecast; Q2 rev ~$10.9B. In advanced talks to buy inference-accel startup **Decart (~$6B)**; locked a 20-yr ~$9B compute deal with bitcoin miner **Riot Platforms** (repositions Riot as AI-infra provider).

**NAND supply gap widens.** Phison CEO warns NAND won't catch demand until **2030** → SSD prices structurally elevated. YMTC past Kioxia for #3. Kioxia buyback propelled Japan corporate buybacks to a record; **SK Hynix is now Kioxia's largest shareholder** (short of control). Kioxia + SanDisk demoed a chip enabling the **first 1 PB SSD** and a PCIe Gen6 SSD at 10M IOPS.

**Sector Watch:** OpenAI launches Ultrafast inference for **GPT-5.6 "Sol" on Cerebras** wafer-scale, 14× speedup — live commercial deployment outside Nvidia's stack. Google reportedly in talks with **AMD** to co-design next-gen TPU (on-package CPU for RL) — a break from Google's in-house-only ASIC strategy. Biren projects ~22× H1-2026 rev surge. TSMC Arizona H1 profit doubles to NT$36.1B. **Core Scientific** closes $444M Polaris acquisition → 1.5 GW Muskogee campus. Lam Research +$3B R&D over 5 yrs. Intel signals renewed memory push. Jeju Semiconductor Q2 op profit +2,745%. OpenAI delays $20B Georgia DC on power/permitting.
