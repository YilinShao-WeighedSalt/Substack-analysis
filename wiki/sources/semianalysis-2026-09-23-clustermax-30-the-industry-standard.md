The third edition of SemiAnalysis's GPU-cloud rating system, ~30,000 words, covering **77 providers reviewed** (market view expanded to 323, up from 209 in 2.0), 200+ end-user interviews. Ranks **managed clusters** — not powered shells or token-as-a-service — across 10 categories (compute, networking, storage, orchestration, UI, monitoring, support, security, lifecycle, reliability). Testing = a 3-phase Audit / Performance / Reliability protocol on 32-GPU Blackwell/GB200/MI355X clusters (Hopper now excluded, "H100 is over 4 years old").

**Podium / tier moves (the investable signal):**
- **Platinum:** **[[NBIS|Nebius]] joins [[CRWV|CoreWeave]].** CoreWeave still sets the technical bar every other cloud follows; Nebius is now established as a provider that "consistently commands premium pricing," serving a whole class of neolabs "at seller's prices" on strong business decisions.
- **Gold:** **[[GOOGL|Google Cloud]] joins [[ORCL|Oracle]].**
- **Silver:** **[[MSFT|Azure]] moves down to Silver**; Lambda, Firmus, TensorWave remain; GMI moves up from Bronze.
- **Bronze / below:** **Crusoe drops to Bronze**; Fluidstack moves to Unavailable.
- The bar was raised: **only 19 neoclouds globally earn any Medallion**; a new "Participation Ribbon" tier (15 providers) captures the bare-minimum crowd.

**Structural read:** frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) increasingly co-design and self-manage the stack ("the defaults stop working" at their scale; combined K8s-hiring salary bands ~$28–43M), taking larger slices each year — SemiAnalysis models them at **56.3% of all lab compute by YE2027**. But managed clusters as a business are still growing exponentially: a long tail of multi-hundred-million-dollar deals, and open-source serving ("$100M per MW per year selling open-source tokens") could lift the managed-cluster share of the market. A "Matthew principle" via financing lets profitable frontier labs lock capacity while smaller ones eat fat prepays.
