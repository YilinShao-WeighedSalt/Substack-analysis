# Semi Doped — Daily Update, September 28th 2026

News-dense daily (Austin & Vik). Marquee: **Nvidia's largest-ever buyback**; theme running through the issue is "so much capacity coming online in 2028 — nothing helps today's squeeze."

## Summary

- **Nvidia authorizes a record $150B share repurchase** (Reuters: largest in company history, no timeline). *Austin: think about what Nvidia CAN'T do with this money — big M&A is DOA with regulators (Groq probe), ecosystem investments get called circular, it's fabless and not a cloud; every other door is closed, so $150B goes back to shareholders.* → [[NVDA]].
- **Samsung Electro-Mechanics commits $4.4B (KRW 6.78T) to FC-BGA substrate** for AI accelerators — Sejong ₩4.27T (its largest-ever domestic capex) + Vietnam. FC-BGA is a narrowing chokepoint (larger bodies, higher layer counts, finer routing). *Vik: mass production doesn't start until Sept 2028 — does nothing for today's squeeze.* → [[009150.KS]].
- **TSMC scales N2 to 120,000 wafers/mo by end-2026** (one of its fastest leading-edge ramps) + five new advanced-packaging fabs in Chiayi + a Taichung hub; affiliate Vanguard's $7.8B Singapore fab w/ NXP adds mature-node capacity. *Vik: N2 tape-outs already run 4× N3, demand isn't slowing — but it's all round-wafer CoWoS downstream; I don't buy that it scales to the very large packages coming (glass panels are the endgame, longer-term).* → [[TSM]].
- **SK Hynix weighs a ~$150B US IPO of its Solidigm NAND unit** (Reuters) — dilutive optics sent SK Hynix shares lower — same week it **validated its HBM5 architecture on TSMC's CoWoS** and won TSMC Partner of the Year for the 2nd year. *Vik: focus on the HBM5-on-CoWoS validation; power + design-process co-optimization now matter most (the base-die story), and value keeps sliding toward the logic designers while the memory stack drifts back toward commodity.* → [[000660.KS]].
- **Qualcomm at Snapdragon Summit 2026**: PC architecture redesigned with Google for agentic AI; 1-bit AI model support on wearable platforms; next-gen Snapdragon X takes a big performance step + opens Linux; **actively evaluating Samsung's 2nm** to diversify beyond TSMC; 6G commercial roadmap targets 2029; new Spectra ISP runs three 64MP sensors. → [[QCOM]].
- **Infineon buys AI-power specialist C2i Semiconductors** (digital control/telemetry for server power stages; terms undisclosed) + **samples a record 120A/phase voltage regulator** for dense AI-accelerator racks; automotive softness adds urgency to the DC push. *Vik: feeds the vertical-power-delivery/SIVR path — the question is whether it takes sockets from MPS.* → [[IFX]].

### Sector watch (call-relevant)
- **Foundry/EDA:** Synopsys launches an **autonomous engineering-agent platform** + expands its TSMC collaboration for next-gen AI system design (TSMC OIP) → [[SNPS]]; **ASML confirms 2027 EUV output fully sold out**, High-NA firming, opens an India office, European revenue share at zero → [[ASML]]; DB HiTek raises foundry prices up to 30% on Chinese demand.
- **Optics:** **GlobalFoundries reports strong demand for Chinese optical-module chips** and is expanding into the segment → [[GFS]]; CtrlS deploys Ciena optics across five Indian cities; MaxLinear launches Puma 9 (DOCSIS 4.0).
- **Power:** Wolfspeed launches a premium 200mm SiC substrate; Schneider a water-cooled distribution unit; **Eaton acquires COL Group (€810M)** [already logged 2026-09-25 — duplicate corporate action] → [[ETN]].
- **Memory:** Montage mass-produces a 5th-gen DDR5 RCD at 8,000 MT/s (first for a Chinese supplier); Saimemory + Shinko co-develop HBM-class low-power memory; **CXMT puts 5th-gen DRAM into production**.
- **Compute:** **Supermicro begins shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 racks** (1.8MW in-row CDUs, 1,152-GPU units) [already logged 2026-09-23 — duplicate milestone] → [[SMCI]]; FuriosaAI funding crisis (Korea Public Growth Fund delays ₩400B); Fluidstack raises $1.5B (Jane Street), valuation → $18B.
- **Data centers:** **Nscale closes $3.36B pre-IPO convertible** (Third Point; $1B from Nvidia pending), targeting a $35B NY IPO → reinforces [[NVDA]]; Crusoe cancels its $1.25B Boom-supersonic-turbine plan (power-sourcing pivot); Submer launches Corenix immersion-cooled modular DC.
- **Policy:** Singapore launches "SG Semiconductor"; **China weighs allowing ByteDance & Alibaba to buy new Nvidia chips** as Huawei Ascend falls short of domestic demand → reinforces [[NVDA]]; US & China release product lists for a $30B bilateral tariff deal (first concrete sectoral rollback).
