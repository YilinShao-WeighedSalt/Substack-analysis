## Open question
Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on Nov-12, 2026 after a board conduct probe. Both covering sources reacted the same day and drew materially different conclusions about what it means for the stock.

**SemiAnalysis — structurally bearish** ([[semianalysis-2026-11-12-nvidia-and-the-world-without-jensen]]): Nvidia "was never built for him not to be there." The hardest-to-transfer advantages were *personal to Jensen* — the neocloud backstop / "Jensen put" ($530B gross off-B/S guarantees), the Korea-HBM (~$3.0–3.3/Gb HBM4 vs $3.7–4.1 for rivals) and Taiwan-logic supply lock-ups won CEO-to-CEO, decade-long "zero-billion-dollar" founder bets, and his role as AI's open-source spokesman. Without him, suppliers de-prioritize Nvidia and SA models **>70% of 2028 capacity could be non-Nvidia**; the neocloud ecosystem risks a "large contraction" if the put is gone. Logged **SHORT $173.73** (structural, not a formal trade short). Also ties the exit to Washington — Jensen's departure clears the way for RASA and harsher China controls.

**Semi Doped — near-term constructive / NEUTRAL** ([[semidoped-2026-11-12-jensen-huang-resigns]]): demand is real, the Rubin Ultra / Feynman roadmap is intact, interim CEO Colette Kress should run "maintain mode," and "nothing changed overnight." The *real* risk is long-term — a leadership vacuum inviting infighting and a slide into spreadsheet-CEO bureaucracy (the Intel cautionary tale) — not the next year. Logged **NEUTRAL $173.73**. Both sides agree the clearest beneficiary is **AMD** (Lisa Su should "attack, not switch teams").

## What would resolve it
- **FQ3 earnings Nov-17** and the first forward guidance/commentary without Jensen.
- Whether a successor (internal **Ian Buck** / external **Rene Haas**) can hold the supply-chain lock-ups, the backstop program, and hyperscaler allocation discipline.
- Whether **RASA / tighter China controls** actually pass now that the lobbying counterweight is gone.
- Tell to watch (SA's own falsifier): **GPU rental rates rolling over** → backstops triggered → Nvidia exposed to price risk. Track vs the non-Nvidia 2028-capacity-share estimate.

Shared price mark: NVDA **$173.73** (Nov-13), down ~24% over 3 months and ~6.9% since the Nov-10 NEUTRAL ($186.64).

## 2026-11-19 update — FQ3 beat validates "roadmap-intact" near-term
Nvidia's **FQ3 (first without Huang) beat and raised**: rev **$115.8B (+103%)**, Q4 guide **$130B**, and Kress **raised FY28 growth to 75% from 70%** — the forecast the bear case feared would be scrapped. **$390B supply commitments** (mostly multiyear memory) and a called **margin trough**; board won't reinstate Huang; +4% AH. → **Semi Doped's roadmap-intact side wins the near term** (logged **LONG $155.46**, stance neutral→constructive). **But the bear is not refuted:** SemiAnalysis's case is long-term (no successor bench, the HBM/logic supply stranglehold + $530B backstop decaying *with* Jensen), and in the same window **SA cut its own 2028 accelerator forecast to $758B (−28%)** with Nvidia taking most of the cut, while the **BIS remote-access rule** and the **H200-to-China halt** add the policy headwinds SA tied to Jensen's exit. SA's SHORT ($173.73) stays **open**. Net: near-term demand unbroken (roadmap-intact ✓), structural thesis still open; watch GPU-rental rates + the non-Nvidia 2028-capacity share. NVDA $155.46 (Nov-18 close, lowest since ~Jul-2025 despite the beat).

## 2026-11-25 update — the whipsaw, and an SEC probe neither side priced in
A third actor entered: the **SEC**. Reuters reported enforcement staff are scrutinizing Nvidia's accounting (customer-HQ revenue geography — Singapore ~half of revenue — and circular financing); NVDA fell to $125.53, then the board **reinstated Huang** under White-House pressure for a **+31.5% session to $165.05**. Scorecard: SemiAnalysis's Nov-12 structural **SHORT ($173.73)** is **+5.0% in its favor** at $165.05 — the governance turmoil (forced exit → probe → political reinstatement) is arguably the bear's "institutional decay" case playing out in real time, even though the trigger wasn't succession. SemiDoped stays **NEUTRAL $165.05**: near-term roadmap intact, but the open SEC/circular-financing/foreign-intel probes keep it from re-upping the FQ3-beat LONG. The roadmap-intact side is *not* refuted (demand unbroken, FY28 raised to 75% at the Nov-18 print); the structural-bear side gains the governance-instability evidence. Unresolved; the tells are now (a) whether the SEC probe forces a restatement or China-revenue write-down, and (b) whether Huang's return actually re-locks the supply chain or the compliance overhang (new NatSec/Export committee, China testimony) permanently caps the China TAM.
