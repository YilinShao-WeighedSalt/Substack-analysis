# Open contradiction — is ASML about to break Besi's hybrid-bonding moat?

**SemiAnalysis (bear, Oct-15):** From 27 bonding patent families + a track-based tool design (SuperTrak conveyance, ORION alignment, EUV-grade particle control), SA concludes **ASML is building a die-to-wafer (D2W) hybrid bonder** and can win **logic first** (TSMC/Intel) selling bonder+scanner as a premium pair. HBM D2W (the expected volume driver) slips to **2029+ "if at all,"** Korean challengers catching up → **SHORT [[BESI]] €163.40** (the single high-growth moat now contested by the litho monopoly with a 20-yr installed base at every fab). Vindicated IA's Sep-6 "RIP BESI."

**Semi Doped (bull, Oct-20 & Oct-22):** *Vik:* the selloff is a **patent-count story** — ASML has filed patents but has **no product and no date**, while **Besi's orders more than doubled YoY** and then printed **+96% in Q3** (Oct-22), with CEO Blickman **flatly dismissing ASML as a direct hybrid-bonding rival** ("fundamentally different positions in the packaging workflow"). Die-to-wafer HBM bonding slips to 2029 regardless; backlog conversion on track. → **LONG [[BESI]] €162.45**.

**The crux:** both agree HBM-D2W volume is a 2029 story and that ASML is *entering*. They disagree on (a) whether ASML's scanner-grade alignment + particle-control edge actually transfers to a volume bonder on a realistic timeline, and (b) how much of Besi's value is the HBM wave vs. the logic-D2W + AI-packaging order surge already in hand. **Resolution watch:** ASML product/date announcements and first tool orders; Besi's 2027-28 order trajectory and whether ASML (or Applied, a 9% Besi owner with a Kinex JV) wins a named volume socket.

**Scorecard so far:** SA's Oct-15 SHORT (€163.40) and SD's Oct-16 NEUTRAL (€163.40) both opened at the 3-mo low; shares sit ~€162 on Oct-23 — roughly flat, unresolved. Besi's +96% order print is a near-term point for the bulls; the structural threat is unproven either way.
