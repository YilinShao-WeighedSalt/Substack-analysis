# Memory longs vs. the sovereign/shareholder tension

A genuine cross-publication divergence surfaced this run on SK Hynix ([[000660.KS]]) and Samsung ([[005930.KS]]).

## Semi Doped — persistent LONG (operating-metrics view)
Semi Doped has logged a near-unbroken string of LONGs on both Korean memory majors through 2026: shortage guided to 2028–2030, record buybacks (Samsung ₩110T, SK Hynix $29B cancellation), 60–70% of output committed to AI LTAs, $4B Indiana HBM fab, HBM4E base-die leadership. The Jul-31 batch just resolved as wins (000660 +3.4%, 005930 +4.9%). The thesis is bottom-up: pricing power + AI-memory windfall = earnings and shareholder returns compounding.

## SemiAnalysis — NEUTRAL caution (capital-allocation / margin view)
In the 2026-09-01 Korea sovereign-AI piece, SemiAnalysis flags a *shareholder-alignment* risk the operating metrics miss:
1. **Favorable HBM pricing to Nvidia.** It believes Nvidia has likely secured a SOCAMM LTA plus favorable HBM pricing and 2027 volume commitments from SK Hynix — good for volume, a give on margin.
2. **National-champion role.** Korea's ~$919B / 18.4GW sovereign buildout leans on Samsung/SK Hynix as strategic national assets; capex and priorities set for sovereignty may not maximize per-share returns.

## The crux
Both can be right on different horizons: Semi Doped captures the 2026–27 supercycle earnings; SemiAnalysis flags that the *terms of trade* with a monopsony-ish Nvidia plus state-directed capex could cap the shareholder upside even as the businesses boom. 

## Resolve-by signals
- Reported HBM4/HBM4E ASP trajectory into 2027 (are prices firm, or is Nvidia extracting concessions as volume scales?).
- Whether Korean memory capex is set by return-on-capital or by sovereign mandate (watch buyback/dividend follow-through vs. fab-spend acceleration).
- Relative share performance of 000660/005930 vs. the memory earnings trend — a widening gap would validate SemiAnalysis's caution.
