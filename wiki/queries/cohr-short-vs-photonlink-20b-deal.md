# Coherent: persistent IA short vs. SD's billion-dollar order book

**The tension (Nov 2026).** Two of the tracked publications now point opposite directions on [[COHR]]:

- **Irrational Analysis — SHORT (reaffirmed through October).** IA's engineering thesis is that
  **Coherent's UHP laser fails the <1 MHz effective-linewidth / RIN spec** required to win the
  co-packaged-optics (CPO/OCI-MSA) external-laser sockets — so it "won't win the laser sockets"
  that [[LITE]] dominates. IA has carried a COHR short since the Sep-21 ECOC "emergency" memo
  (short $310.39 → resolved +23.5% WIN on Oct-23) and reaffirmed it in the Oct-19 OCP recap
  ($232.90) and Oct-24 "Dancing in the Rain" ($209.29). The short has been one of IA's best trades.

- **Semi Doped — LONG read (Nov-5).** SD reports Coherent **beat estimates and disclosed a 5-year,
  roughly $20B PhotonLink supply deal with an unnamed AI company**, revenue starting early 2028 —
  i.e., a very large, contracted order book regardless of the CPO-laser-socket question. (Coherent
  separately keeps winning transceiver/datacom volume.)

## Why they can both be partly right
- IA's short is **specific**: Coherent loses the *external-laser* socket in CW-SiPho CPO to Lumentum's
  UHP monopoly. That can be true while Coherent still books huge **transceiver / datacom / PhotonLink**
  revenue elsewhere — different products, different margins.
- The PhotonLink deal is a **revenue** signal (2028+), not evidence Coherent cracked the <1MHz laser spec.
  If the counterparty is buying transceivers/optical modules rather than UHP CPO lasers, both views hold.

## What resolves it
- Whether Coherent is named (or benchmarked) into a CPO **external-laser** win at the <1MHz/RIN spec
  (would break IA's short) vs. the PhotonLink deal turning out to be transceiver/datacom volume (consistent
  with IA's short + SD's bull).
- COHR's gross-margin mix as PhotonLink ramps (laser vs module share).
- The next 30-day price resolution of SD's **LONG $169.76 (Nov-5)** vs IA's standing short.

**Price marks at open (2026-11-07):** **COHR $169.76** (down from IA's $310 Sep-21 short entry — the short
has worked; SD's long opens near the Black-November low).
