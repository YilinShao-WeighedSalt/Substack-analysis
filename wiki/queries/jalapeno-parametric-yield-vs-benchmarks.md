# OpenAI Jalapeño — a genuine publication disagreement

OpenAI's **Jalapeño** inference chip (TSMC N3P, **Broadcom** co-design) is read oppositely by two sources:

- **SemiAnalysis (Aug 25, "OpenAI Jalapeño: Better Than Nvidia Blackwell"):** bullish — independent benchmarks ~1.5–1.9× perf/W vs Nvidia inference; validates OpenAI + Broadcom's custom-silicon program and ESUN scale-up. Supportive of [[AVGO]]'s ASIC franchise.
- **Irrational Analysis (Oct 4, parametric-yield deep-dive):** bearish on *execution* — SA's own reported **25% A0→B0 perf/watt delta** is "an absolutely massive red flag on fire." A 25% post-characterization shift means a **catastrophic parametric-yield failure** ("A0 is broken"); 5–10% = modest, 15% = severe, 25% ≈ disaster. On N3P (world's 2nd-best PDK) that implicates either Broadcom or OpenAI's AI-generated RTL. IA faults SA for passing the datapoint along without flagging its significance.

## Why it matters
Not a clean directional [[AVGO]] stock call on either side — SA is bullish the *chip*, IA is bearish the *silicon execution*. The resolution is empirical: does a **B0 respin** close the gap and ship at volume (SA's framing), or does the A0 delta signal a deeper design/foundry problem that slips the program (IA's framing)? Watch for B0 benchmarks, ESUN deployment scale, and any Broadcom commentary. Feeds [[ai-accelerator-competition]] (does a first-gen hyperscaler ASIC really beat Blackwell on perf/W) and [[foundry-process-node]] (N3P PDK quality).
