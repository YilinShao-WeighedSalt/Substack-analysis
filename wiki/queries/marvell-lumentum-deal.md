# Marvell–Lumentum: deal logic vs. standalone-moat value

**The event (late Oct 2026):** Marvell (MRVL) in advanced talks to acquire Lumentum (LITE),
reported by Bloomberg/Reuters Oct 28. Expected all-stock with a modest premium (CNBC's Faber);
LITE +11% on the report (valuing it ~$91B), MRVL −8/−9%. Bernstein: a deal that size is "likely
impossible to fund entirely with cash." Follows Marvell's 2025 Celestial AI ($3.25B) photonics buy.
Could be announced as early as the first week of November.

## The two sides

- **Strategic / pro-deal (Semi Doped + Cignal AI, Oct 29–30 podcast):** vertical integration is the
  driver — combining Marvell's DSPs/switching silicon with Lumentum's lasers, 200G EMLs, and optical
  circuit switch (OCS) yields a single-vendor **1.6T co-packaged-optics stack** to challenge Broadcom
  across AI networking. Open questions raised: Marvell would then **compete with its own DSP customers**;
  financing/integration of a ~$91B target; and a possible **bidding war** (Nvidia a wildcard). SD frames
  it as news, NEUTRAL on both tickers.

- **Anti-deal / standalone-moat (Irrational Analysis, Oct 30):** IA — long LITE for its **UHP
  co-packaged-optics laser near-monopoly** — is "livid," vows to "do everything possible to kill this
  deal," and views Lumentum as **worth more standalone** than absorbed into Marvell (whose optics
  execution IA has repeatedly derided, e.g. "legendary incompetent cheating" on 400G SerDes TDECQ).
  Reaffirms **LONG LITE**; **NEUTRAL/overhang MRVL** (its Oct-8 moderate-bear at $287 is tracking,
  MRVL now ~$196).

## What resolves it
- Whether a definitive deal is announced, the exchange ratio/premium, and any counter-bid (Nvidia, Broadcom defensively).
- Regulatory/antitrust path (Marvell would consolidate DSP + optical engine; customer-conflict concerns).
- Post-close: does Marvell preserve LITE's qual-only UHP-laser moat or does customer conflict erode it?

Price marks at open (2026-11-01): **LITE $840.70 · MRVL $196.40**.
