## Open question
Both publications looked at the same AI-infrastructure credit stack the same week (Oct-19) and drew opposite conclusions about its soundness.

**Irrational Analysis (bear, "Bubble Vibes"):** de-levered ~$2M before Anthropic's buyside day; sees **Anthropic's valuation collapse ($2T→$1.5T in 10 days)**, OpenAI unable to raise at $1.4T, **coding ARR plateauing**, and "violent" moves in **neocloud convertible notes** with rumored institutional blow-ups (VARA, a Jane Street desk, HRT). Explicit **sub-prime / mortgage-bond / CDO analogy** — "contracts are just pieces of paper." Corroborated same day by Semi Doped: **Anthropic offering >10% junk-yield chip paper** and **Microsoft walking from Nscale's Essex site** days before its $3B IPO.

**SemiAnalysis (bull, Datacenters 2027):** the buildout is *accelerating* — **>$1T of binding/non-cancellable leases signed YTD 2026** (15-20yr take-or-pay, $30-50B TCV/GW), the broadest lessee pool ever, **most debt already raised** (their new Compute/Capital/Markets Model), funding **+50GW in 2027**. The leases are the leading indicator that de-risks the debt.

## The crux
Are the datacenter leases genuinely **binding / take-or-pay** (SA's claim, which would make the debt money-good) or is the credit **sub-prime-style** (IA's claim — counterparties like Anthropic/OpenAI still scaling into steep liabilities, financing increasingly circular)? The MSFT-quits-Nscale event is one data point that a named anchor tenant *can* walk (grid-delay force majeure), which cuts toward IA; SA would counter that the binding leases it tracks are structured to survive exactly that. 

## What would resolve it
- A neocloud/lab **default or covenant break** on GW-scale lease debt → IA.
- 2027 capacity delivering roughly on SA's +50GW track with leases honored → SA.
- Watch: Anthropic's next raise clearing (or not) near $1.5T; neocloud convertible/CDS spreads; whether more anchor tenants walk on grid delays.
