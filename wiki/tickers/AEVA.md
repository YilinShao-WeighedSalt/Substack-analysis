# AEVA — Aeva Technologies

FMCW-LiDAR company repositioned by irrationalanalysis as a stealth **InP / high-power SOA** play.
The thesis: LiDAR is effectively dead as an end market, but the handful of ex-LiDAR firms are the
only ones who know how to build the high-power **SOA (Semiconductor Optical Amplifier)** that the
NPO/CPO optics roadmap needs. Public info strongly indicates **Aeva manufactures via Seivers as its
InP fab partner**; the author independently verified Aeva's public claims and used them to build his
COGS/InP model. He calls Aeva "a more interesting buy than Seivers" (and than being long the SOA
theme generally), while noting Seivers will make more money off Aeva than it ever did off Ayar Labs.
