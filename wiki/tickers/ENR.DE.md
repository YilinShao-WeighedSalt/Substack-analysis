## Thesis evolution
First logged 2026-09-11 from SemiAnalysis's behind-the-meter (BTM) power deep dive. Siemens Energy is named — with [[GEV]] — as an incumbent gas-turbine winner of the datacenter off-grid buildout. But the piece's central point is that the surprise share-gainers are the ~22 makers of reciprocating engines (INNIO/Jenbacher, Caterpillar, Cummins, Wartsila) and solid-oxide fuel cells ([[BE|Bloom Energy]], 3.8 GW of orders) — not the turbine incumbents. Turbine demand is real and premium, but the two incumbents are also most exposed to the emerging secondary market for turbines from speculative/failed orders. Held NEUTRAL: a genuine beneficiary, but ceding relative share within its own theme.
