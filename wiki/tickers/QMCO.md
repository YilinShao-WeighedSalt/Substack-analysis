## Thesis evolution
First appearance (2026-10-01). Irrational Analysis added Quantum (QMCO) to its small-size "Degen" bucket after a reader ping, framing it as a dark-horse beneficiary of two AI side-effects: (1) the data explosion spilling past HDD economics into cheap-and-slow tape, and (2) AI-driven cybersecurity chaos pushing organizations toward truly offline, air-gapped archival — where tape's 30-year offline durability beats HDD/SSD bit-rot. A speculative, low-conviction position by IA's own sizing language.
