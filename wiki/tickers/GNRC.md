## Thesis evolution
First appearance in the wiki (2026-09-17). Generac — long known as a residential/commercial
standby-generator maker — enters the AI-datacenter power story via a large multi-year backup-
generation supply agreement with **Amazon**. The bull case is the same demand vector driving
[[BE]] and the reciprocating-engine OEMs on [[datacenter-power]]: grid interconnection is the
binding constraint, so hyperscalers are locking in **firm, multi-year, on-site generation**
rather than buying spot. Generac's angle is diesel/gas backup gensets rather than fuel cells
or prime-power recips — a lower-tech but proven, fast-to-deploy tier. The +33% premarket move
signals the market had priced little AI-datacenter optionality into the name.
