Jargon decoder

Effective (β-separation) linewidthIntegral of a laser's phase noise above the β-line; what actually sets optical-link quality. Coherent showed the flattering *intrinsic* number instead — IA's whole short.
NPO / CPONear-/Co-Packaged Optics — moving the optical engine next to (or onto) the switch/GPU package; needs a clean, narrow-linewidth laser to work.
VCSEL (1060nm)Vertical-cavity laser; the industry is migrating 850→1060nm for AI die-to-die optics, which triggers a 6–12mo GR-468 reliability requal.
ECOCEuropean Conference on Optical Communication — the September event where LITE/COHR/MRVL/Semtech showed their optics hands.
Nscale / neocloudGPU-rental infra co (like CoreWeave/Nebius); Nscale's $35B IPO rides a $103B backlog anchored by Anthropic + Microsoft.
VPD (vertical power delivery)Feeding the GPU from *underneath* the board instead of across it — how Vicor's royalty-licensed architecture survives extreme rack densities.
High-NA EUVNext-gen 0.55-NA lithography (ASML); 'stitching' = joining exposure fields to pattern dies larger than one High-NA shot — Intel's unsolved gate.
A14 / 14ATSMC's 1.4nm node (A14) vs Intel's competing 14A; TSMC pulled A14 pilot to Q1-2027 to defend its lead.
CXL / XL-FLASHMemory-expansion fabric; Kioxia's XL1 puts fast NAND on a CXL card as a cheaper DRAM-capacity substitute in AI servers.
MoE active parametersOnly a subset of a mixture-of-experts model runs per token; Anthropic's models are rumored *less* sparse than OpenAI's → worse inference economics (IA).
Backstop universeSemiAnalysis's name for Nvidia's ~$530B off-balance-sheet guarantees that let neoclouds borrow at investment-grade pricing.
