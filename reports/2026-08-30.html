Jargon decoder

Jalapeño — OpenAI's first custom inference ASIC (co-designed with Broadcom); a clean-sheet general-purpose accelerator that independent benchmarks put at ~1.5–1.9× the efficiency-per-watt of Nvidia's current inference silicon.
NVHBM — Nvidia's custom HBM variant that moves the memory controller off the compute die into the HBM base die → +30% bandwidth, −15% power, ~25% freed XPU area; Nvidia standardizes it across memory vendors and sells more content.
Vera Rubin — Nvidia's next GPU platform (Rubin GPU + Vera CPU); Jensen pegs a full-stack rack at ~$40B/GW and calls it the fastest revenue ramp in the company's history.
NVLink Fusion — Nvidia's program letting third-party CPUs/XPUs (Amazon's Trainium first) plug into its rack-scale NVLink fabric — the on-ramp for custom silicon into Nvidia racks.
ESUN — Broadcom-backed open scale-up networking standard for linking accelerators inside a rack; the open alternative to Nvidia's proprietary NVLink, used by Jalapeño.
HBM4 — Next-gen high-bandwidth memory stacked beside the compute die — the supply-constrained ingredient of every AI accelerator; the reason SK Hynix is breaking ground on a US fab.
CoWoS — TSMC's chip-on-wafer-on-substrate advanced packaging that stitches GPU + HBM together; the capacity chokepoint behind TSMC's widening foundry lead.
Nscale — A non-hyperscaler "neocloud" infrastructure provider; Anthropic's 6-year $45B deal locks in ~460MW of Vera Rubin capacity without owning the silicon.
MatX — A custom-AI-chip startup; Anthropic walked away from a ~$7B buyout (into a partnership instead) once Nscale secured its GPU capacity.
YMTC — China's state-backed NAND maker targeting top-3 by 2027; the competitive pressure driving Kioxia + SanDisk's $31B Kitakami expansion.
Foundry share — TSMC-vs-Samsung split of contract chip-making; TSMC widening the gap (Samsung ~7%) raises real questions about Samsung's advanced-node roadmap.
MOSFET — The metal-oxide-silicon transistor (Kahng & Atalla, 1959) — ~99.9% of all transistors ever built; this run's TIL history, and the substrate of the whole story.
