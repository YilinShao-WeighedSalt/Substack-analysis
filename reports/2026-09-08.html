Jargon Decoder terms used this run, one line each

HBM de-specCustomers asking for fewer memory layers (12-hi→8-hi/4-hi) per HBM stack — a demand signal that they've found ways around the DRAM makers' premium roadmap.
Hybrid bondingCopper-to-copper chip stacking at ~10µm pitch (denser than microbumps); de-spec means 8-hi HBM skips it — the bear case for tool-maker BESI.
TPUv7 'Ironwood'Google's 7th-gen AI chip; now sold/rented externally, benchmarked by SemiAnalysis at up to 50% better perf-per-dollar vs Nvidia B200/B300 on inference.
TorchTPUNew native-PyTorch backend letting vLLM/SGLang treat TPUs as first-class devices — the software bridge that makes TPUs usable outside Google. Open-sources ~mid-Oct.
perf-per-dollar (tok/$)Tokens served per dollar of total cost — the metric that matters to inference buyers, where TPU's lower hourly cost offsets lower raw throughput.
Boardfly (TPUv8i)Google's new dragonfly-style network for the inference TPU — cuts network hops ~16→7, adds native FP4 to challenge Nvidia Rubin NVL72.
CXL flash-for-DRAMKioxia XL1: NAND placed under DRAM via CXL to replace a slice of costly DRAM in AI servers — first such product aimed at production AI, tied to Nvidia's CMX.
parametric yieldFraction of chips that hit spec on speed/power (not just 'work'); IA's Cerebras short bets the doubled clocks hide an unsolved ~20% final-yield problem.
GR-468The optics reliability qual standard; new 1060nm VCSELs (Lumentum/Coherent) need a fresh 6-12mo requal, a near-term speed bump vs AMS OSRAM's proven 850nm.
Multi-vendor disaggSplitting prefill/decode across different chip vendors' hardware; IA argues it's temporary — everyone will go vertical (Nvidia, Google, Amazon, AMD).
