Jargon Decoder

Transceiver ban
Draft Trump/FCC curb on new-model Chinese optical transceivers (~50% of world supply); framed as security but a transceiver is a dumb PHY behind signed-key auth — cut supply, don't fix a hack.
Active (6-axis) alignment
Robotic laser-to-fiber positioning at sub-micron precision — China's real, hard-to-reshore optical-assembly moat that a ban can't quickly backfill.
InP (indium phosphide)
III-V substrate for lasers & high-power optics; ~80% of output now flows to AI with no consumer fallback → shortage "worse than DRAM" (IA). AXTI is the pure substrate play.
Openlight PDK
Tower's InP-on-silicon-photonics hetero process kit — puts the laser onto the PIC, so Tower content rises even without advanced packaging. Beats GloFo on waveguide insertion loss.
SOA / high-power CPO laser
Semiconductor Optical Amplifier — the scarce on-chip light source for co-/near-packaged optics. IA: a Broadcom + Lumentum duopoly; Coherent ~18mo away, AAOI "has nothing".
CPO / NPO
Co-/Near-Packaged Optics — move the optical engine next to the switch ASIC to cut power & latency vs pluggable modules.
HBF (High-Bandwidth Flash)
NAND stacked HBM-style over UCIe PHYs; first spec from SK Hynix + SanDisk. Flash bulls want it to crunch NAND like HBM crunched DRAM. IA: "solution looking for a problem".
Micro-LED vs micro-VCSEL
Two chip-scale light sources for links. IA verdict: VCSEL viable at 32–64G NRZ; micro-LED "a hoax" — incoherent light = catastrophic phase noise, fails even at 4G.
Parametric yield
Share of chips that hit target speed at acceptable voltage/power (vs merely functioning). IA's Cerebras bear crux — suspects it was never solved, capping final yield ~20%.
TileRT
Software that compiles the whole decode graph into one persistent GPU kernel for ultra-low-latency inference — reframes "speed" as a tier you rent from your GPU fleet, pressuring inference-ASIC TAM (Cerebras/Groq).
Value-based compute pricing
Charging for GPU clusters by the revenue they can print (~$50B/GW/yr), not cost-plus — SpaceX/Elon's playbook behind the 10GW-in-2027 claim.
Infra-debt financing
Funding datacenters like toll roads — predictable token "tolls" → low-rate institutional loans. Behind Anthropic's $10B deal with 6-month-old Volta, and Nvidia's $500B "banker" platform.
Taalas
Startup (just bought by AMD) that burns model weights directly into a chip's top metal masks — ~2-3mo turnaround. IA reads the buy as FPGA/embedded IP, not datacenter-AI.
