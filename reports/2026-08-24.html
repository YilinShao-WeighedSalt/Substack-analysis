The bull case for the ASIC flight and the bull case for Nvidia's moat are, for now, both true.

Jargon decoder

Custom ASIC / SPVCustomer-specific AI accelerator built to escape Nvidia's GPU margin; the special-purpose vehicle keeps the $70B chip-program debt off Broadcom's own balance sheet.
CUDA moatNvidia's software stack (kernels, vLLM/TensorRT-LLM/Dynamo support) that makes its GPUs faster in practice than raw specs suggest — the gap AMD keeps trying to close.
Agentic inferenceMulti-turn, long-context, tool-calling LLM traffic (à la Claude Code) that now dominates production; far harder to serve than the old single-shot 8k-in/1k-out prompt.
KV-cache offloadSpilling attention key/value cache to CPU DRAM to fit longer contexts; Nvidia exploits it more aggressively than AMD in the AgentX benchmark.
CoWoSTSMC chip-on-wafer-on-substrate advanced packaging — the binding capacity constraint on AI-accelerator output; LG just entered with maskless Laser Direct Imaging to help relieve it.
1.6nm (A16)TSMC's next leading-edge logic node; 'development wrapped' = design complete, Q4 = mass-production start.
Memory upcycleAI-driven HBM/DRAM shortage handing Samsung & SK Hynix record profits — now recycled into record-scale buybacks & dividends (₩110T at Samsung).
LPU'Language Processing Unit' — Groq's inference-only chip design; the rumored (and denied) basis for a China-legal Nvidia part.
Open vs closed gapHow far open-weight models (Kimi K3, GLM 5.3) trail the closed frontier (Fable 5, GPT-5.6); SemiAnalysis finds the catch-up time halves each model era.
