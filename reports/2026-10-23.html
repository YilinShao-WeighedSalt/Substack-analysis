Jargon decoder

Hybrid bonding
Copper-to-copper chip stacking with no solder bumps — denser than bumps, the key process for HBM & 3D logic. Besi is the volume tool leader; ASML is now building a rival.
D2W / W2W
Die-to-wafer vs wafer-to-wafer bonding — the two hybrid-bonding flavors; D2W already runs in volume for logic (TSMC/Intel), HBM D2W keeps slipping.
ABF / FC-BGA substrate
The resin build-up board a chip package sits on; AI packages need far bigger, denser ones → the quiet bottleneck behind SEMCO, Unimicron, Kinsus, Ibiden.
HBM4E base die
The logic chip at the bottom of a memory stack. Routing it to a leading foundry (TSMC 3nm / Samsung 4nm) sends value to whoever designs it.
Non-cancelable compute
Cloud bills a lab owes regardless of usage — Anthropic's S-1 puts up to $750B on record → a floor under accelerator & HBM orders.
Ascend 950 (Huawei)
China's leading AI accelerator; benchmarks ~A100-to-MI300X class silicon but DeepSeek's software now runs it near its ceiling.
CUDA moat
Nvidia's software lock-in. This week's challenges: Amazon TorchNeuron (Trainium), Google TorchTPU, and an AMD+Intel x86 oneDNN pact in PyTorch.
Vertical power delivery (VPD)
Feeding current straight up into the die from below — Vicor's edge, and a driver of advanced packaging as rack power densities climb.
4F² DRAM
A denser DRAM cell China's CXMT is pushing without EUV; Micron's CTO just said it won't follow that path.
WFE
Wafer-fab equipment (ASML/Lam/AMAT). "Shortage through 2028" means backlog holders sit at the front of the queue.
