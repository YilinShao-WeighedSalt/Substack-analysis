Jargon decoder

Agentic inferenceServing multi-turn AI agents: long contexts, heavy KV-cache reuse, bursty sub-agents — the workload Rubin was co-designed for.
tokens/$ TCOTokens produced per dollar of all-in serving cost — SemiAnalysis's core figure of merit for comparing accelerators.
perf/MW (tok/s/MW)Tokens per megawatt: since powered datacenters are the bottleneck, more tokens per watt = more revenue per site.
P90 interactivityThe streaming speed (tokens/sec) a user sees on the slowest 10% of responses — a serving SLA target.
Vera Rubin NVL72Nvidia's next platform after Blackwell: Rubin GPU + Vera CPU + NVLink 6, co-designed across six products for agents.
QTL cliff (Apr-2027)Qualcomm's licensing (QTL) revenue reset — the point where Apple's in-house modem removes a chunk of QCOM's highest-margin income.
mmWaveHigh-frequency 5G bands (24GHz+); hard to build a modem for — Apple hitting it early is what frees it from Qualcomm.
Shoreline efficiencyBandwidth per mm of chip edge (Tbps/mm) — how much I/O you can cram along a die's perimeter.
Parallel port vs SerDesParallel = many slow wires (edge-hungry); SerDes = few very fast wires. Cerebras's wafer I/O is the primitive former.
Parametric yieldShare of chips that meet spec on measured parameters (speed/power), not just 'work' — IA's core Cerebras short thesis.
Behind-the-meter (BtM)Datacenter power generated on-site, bypassing the grid — how SA argues the buildout dodges permit moratoriums.
Datacenter moratoriumA temporary local/state freeze on new DC permits — SA: freezes new applications, rarely revokes approvals, so ~2.3GW actually delayed.
2.1D / organic bridgeA packaging tier between flat 2D and interposer 2.5D; an organic (not silicon) bridge routes around Intel's EMIB patents.
CoWoSTSMC's advanced 2.5D packaging that stitches GPU + HBM onto one substrate — the industry's tightest bottleneck.
High-NA EUVASML's next-gen lithography (0.55 numerical aperture) for sub-2nm; anamorphic optics cap throughput, hence the 110-unit ceiling.
Fabric-attached memoryPooled DRAM reached over a CXL/PCIe fabric (Astera Leo X) so many accelerators share one memory tier.
Robot model layersPlanning (≤20Hz, offloadable to a datacenter) sits atop action/servo loops (100Hz+, must stay onboard the robot).
