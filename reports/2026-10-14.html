Jargon decoder

3D XPoint / PCM — Intel's "Optane"/Micron's "QuantX" phase-change memory: speed between DRAM and NAND, byte-addressable and persistent; both makers killed it by 2022 — IA argues today's DRAM shortage could justify a revival.

4F² DRAM cell — a denser memory-cell layout (4× the minimum feature²); CXMT adopted it to pack more bits per wafer without EUV, its path to server DDR5 vs Samsung/SK Hynix.

Maia 300 — Microsoft's 2nd-gen inference ASIC, multi-chiplet on TSMC N2P; multi-GW Azure by 2027 to cut Nvidia dependence — ~40% more tokens/s, 7× rack throughput vs Maia 200.

Hyper-NA EUV — ASML's next lithography step beyond High-NA: 5nm single-exposure patterning, removing costly multi-patterning at sub-2nm nodes.

Vertical power delivery (VPD) — feeding current to a chip from directly underneath instead of the side; Vicor licenses the IP and collects a royalty regardless of GPU vendor — the basis of its ITC patent suit.

SRMO / micro-VCSEL — Short-Reach Micro-Emitter Optics: many slow optical emitters in parallel ("wide-but-slow") to bridge the 50m copper-to-pluggable gap; Credo's OCP standard, a pivot off its stalled µLED bet.

ITC Section 337 — a US trade complaint that can win import bans on infringing products; Vicor named ~20 AI-server makers (Foxconn, Quanta, Delta) as respondents.

Structera X (CXL) — Marvell's CXL memory-expander (here gen-2, first 6-channel) that adds DRAM bandwidth/capacity to a server over the CXL bus — a high-margin answer to inference memory limits.

CoWoS pass-through — TSMC's advanced-packaging capacity is tight and pricey; those cost hikes now flow straight to buyers (Intel +20%, Qualcomm +10% on CPUs).

App-direct mode — the 3D-XPoint mode that exposes persistence to software (KV-cache/weights survive a reboot) — the only deployment that captured the tech's real value; "memory mode" wasted it as a DRAM cache.

EPIC Center — Applied Materials' $5B collaborative R&D campus (its largest-ever capex) to compress AI-era process-development cycles; aims to double sales without proportional headcount.

USFP — a new Nvidia+Cisco pluggable-optics form factor shown at OCP, alongside Meta's 102.4T IceCube switch — the AI network fabric standardizing in the open.
