Jargon decoder

Hybrid bonding (D2W / W2W)
Copper-to-copper die stacking at pitches too fine for solder bumps; die-to-wafer places single chiplets, wafer-to-wafer bonds whole wafers — the key AI-packaging enabler.
Besi
Dutch firm that today ships the only volume die-to-wafer hybrid-bonding tools (at TSMC/Intel); ASML's entry attacks its single high-growth moat.
Overlay / alignment accuracy
How precisely a die lines up on the wafer; hybrid bonding needs <50nm, and ASML's litho stages already hold sub-1nm — its claimed edge.
EUV / High-NA EUV
ASML's monopoly extreme-ultraviolet lithography; 'first-ever price increases' + sold-out 2027-28 slots confirm the pricing power.
4F² DRAM
A denser memory-cell layout needing no EUV; China's CXMT and now Samsung are pursuing it — though 4F² here ≈ Korea's current 6F², so not the density leap feared.
FeNAND / compute-in-memory
SK Hynix NAND that runs AI multiply-accumulate inside the flash cell via ferroelectric material, cutting the data round-trip to HBM/DRAM.
Neocloud
A GPU-rental operator (CoreWeave, Nebius) financing chips against hyperscaler contracts; CoreWeave's CDS blowout is the first real stress-test of the model.
CDS (credit-default swap)
Market insurance on a bond defaulting; a >60% implied default probability is a vote of no confidence in the borrower.
Terafab
Elon Musk's proposed $100B+ Texas megafab; TSMC says it will take part only in a fab it controls and runs.
Nuvia / Arm license
Qualcomm's CPU-core team; a Delaware jury found Arm breached the license but awarded only $2 — letting Qualcomm keep its Nuvia-derived rights.
KYEC
King Yuan Electronics, a Taiwan test house; Nvidia's $1.4B prepay reserves US burn-in/test capacity — the new back-end chokepoint as Rubin's power climbs.
