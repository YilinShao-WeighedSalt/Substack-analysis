Jargon decoder

CPO / NPO
Co-/Near-Package Optics — lasers moved onto (or beside) the switch/GPU package to cut power & latency vs pluggable transceivers. The prize both LITE and COHR are chasing.
InP
Indium Phosphide — the III-V substrate lasers are grown on; ~80% of output now feeds AI with no consumer fallback, so it's the binding shortage behind the whole optics squeeze.
EML
Externally-Modulated Laser — the high-power, narrow-linewidth datacom laser Lumentum dominates for CPO.
VCSEL
Cheap surface-emitting laser for short reach. Irrational's core dig: running it at 200G-PAM4/lane can't pass reliability — best case is 32–64G NRZ.
ELS / ELSFP
External Laser Source — laser kept in a swappable module instead of soldered into the optical engine; if one dies you swap a part, not the whole board. Lumentum's winning path.
Mode hop
A laser wavelength "flicker" that drops the link. A wide mode-hop-free range is Lumentum's hidden, datasheet-invisible moat.
RIN
Relative Intensity Noise — laser amplitude-noise spec (lower is better). Lumentum ≈ −155 dBc/Hz vs a −145 industry spec = 10× quieter.
GR-468
The Telcordia laser reliability qualification a 200G-PAM4 VCSEL, per Irrational, will fail.
MOPA
Master-Oscillator-Power-Amplifier laser architecture Coherent is pivoting to — but ~18 months from stability.
CoWoS
TSMC's 2.5D GPU packaging. Yield just cleared 99%, so ABF substrate & memory are now the next bottlenecks.
ABF substrate
Ajinomoto Build-up Film package substrate — ~$100M/line, 18–24 months to add, no EUV needed; clears before memory does.
HBF
High-Bandwidth Flash — the NAND analog of HBM; first OCP spec came from SK Hynix + SanDisk.
CXMT
China's DRAM champion — DDR5 yield ~90% (2 pts behind Samsung), now ~$524B market cap, briefly China's most valuable listed company.
Active alignment
Precise six-axis laser-to-fiber alignment; the hard-to-reshore edge that keeps Chinese optics competitive.
