Jargon decoder
3D DRAM
Stacking DRAM cells vertically (like NAND did) to beat the density wall — the memory holy grail, but years from volume. TSMC denied building it for Nvidia.
HBM
High-Bandwidth Memory — stacked DRAM beside the GPU; the scarce bottleneck of the AI build. Its margin edge over plain DRAM is now narrowing.
800V DC / SST
Feeding racks 800-volt DC straight from a solid-state transformer, skipping lossy AC/UPS stages — needed for Nvidia's 600kW+ Kyber racks. China has run DC datacenters for ~20 yrs.
SGLS
Source-grid-load-storage — a Chinese model where the datacenter builds its own wind/solar + battery + private line instead of drawing public grid. Envision's Galaxy covers 60%+ of load from own wind.
EUV photoresist
The light-sensitive chemical that patterns the most advanced chips; a near-duopoly. JSR is #2 and the only one shipping both CA and metal-oxide EUV resist — why Mitsui paid ¥1.84T.
Vera Rubin
Nvidia's next GPU platform after Blackwell. Channel check: volume ramp slipped Dec → Q1 2027; ~5–6k racks in 2027 at ~$8M each.
Neocloud
AI-only GPU-rental clouds (CoreWeave, Nscale, Nebius) that finance chips on debt. Fragile when leases don't renew — MSFT just walked away from a 2023 one.
MOU → PO
A memorandum of understanding is non-binding intent; only ~30–40% of neocloud first-phase MOUs become binding purchase orders, vs near-100% for cash-paying hyperscalers.
Terafab
Elon Musk's new fab venture targeting ~1M wafers/month by 2032 (700k memory / 300k logic) — ≈Samsung's entire DRAM output today. SD is skeptical. Intel reportedly aiding 2nm/1.4nm logic.
NVLink Fusion
Nvidia opening its rack interconnect so third-party NPUs (e.g. Rebellions R200) plug in alongside its GPUs — prefill on GPU, decode on NPU.
Entity List
The US export-control blacklist. Megaspeed — the cloud firm tied to Huang's affair — surfaced in Entity-List talks but hasn't been added.
CoWoS
TSMC's advanced packaging stitching GPU + HBM onto one interposer — the real AI-capacity choke point, and why a TSMC DRAM detour would complicate its HBM base-die role.
