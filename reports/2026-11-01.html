Jargon Decoder
Substrate vs. wafer limit
IA's Intel charge: Intel blamed chip-package substrate shortages, but IA argues it's leading-edge logic wafers — those take ~6 months start-to-finish; substrates don't. A planning miss dressed up as a parts shortage.
SKU yield-harvesting
Selling a chip's partially-defective dies as lower-tier parts. IA's tell: if truly substrate-limited, Intel would ship only top-ASP SKUs — that it didn't implies a wafer shortfall.
CoWoS
TSMC's Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate advanced packaging — the dominant way GPU/accelerator dies are joined to HBM stacks. Capacity is the binding constraint; hence buying AUO's fabs for floor space.
MLCC
Multi-layer ceramic capacitor — tiny passives that smooth power to AI chips; ~hundreds of thousands per GPU rack. SEMCO + Murata are the chokepoint vendors racing to lock 2027 supply.
FC-BGA / ABF substrate
The high-end package substrate that carries a big AI die; Ibiden/Unimicron/SEMCO lead. Lead times stretching to ~30 months — the "next bottleneck" trade.
Co-packaged optics (CPO) / vertical integration
Putting the optical engine next to the switch silicon. Marvell buying Lumentum would fold LITE's lasers into Marvell's DSPs for a single-vendor 1.6T stack — straight at Broadcom.
Neocloud backstop
Nvidia guaranteeing debt behind a neocloud's (Nscale's $45B Anthropic) compute contract. Bulls: a parent co-signing a mortgage. Bears: circular financing papering over weak end-demand.
HBM pricing power
High-Bandwidth Memory stacked on AI chips. With supply sold out to 2027 and the shortage "worsening," SK Hynix/Samsung keep raising prices — memory is the upcycle's singular profit engine.
TC bonder vs. hybrid bonding
Thermocompression bonders (Hanmi, 50% margins) stack HBM today; the richer margins hold only while HBM stays on TC and doesn't move to copper-to-copper hybrid bonding.
Azure growth +48%
Microsoft's cloud revenue growth rate — the rebuttal to Alphabet's miss: proof at least one hyperscaler is converting AI capex into revenue right now.
