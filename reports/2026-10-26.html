Jargon decoder

Distillation / 'alcoholic ARR'Training a small 'student' to copy a big 'teacher' at ~1/10th the R&D cost; IA argues a large slice of Anthropic/OpenAI ARR IS rival labs (Chinese, Meta) paying to distill them — 'dirty' revenue the IPO is trying to back out.
Trainium3 / TorchNeuronAmazon's 3rd-gen accelerator + its native-PyTorch backend: swap .to('cuda') for .to('neuron') instead of the old XLA graph-capture duct tape — the shift that broadens Trainium from inference into training.
FC-BGA substrateFlip-chip ball-grid-array: the build-up board directly beneath an AI accelerator. A hard capacity chokepoint (Ibiden 70%+ of high-end), so customers now buy equity in supplier lines to lock allocation.
Neuron Explorer profilerAWS's event-timeline kernel profiler, co-designed with Trainium; separates HW units per row so pipeline bubbles show at a glance — SA (and Anthropic's perf lead) rate it above Nvidia Nsight / AMD.
Rare-earth magnets (NdFeB)Neodymium/dysprosium permanent magnets in the motors that drive wafer-handlers and litho stages; China controls ~95% of finished magnets, so TSMC's purge request is a multi-year motor requal across the whole tool base.
Neocloud credit spreadExtra yield demanded to lend to GPU-rental clouds (CoreWeave, IREN). Long-dated GPU leases funded with short-term debt = acute refinancing risk; AI-sector spreads hit a 17-month high, handing shorts ~$6B.
AEC (active electrical cable)Copper cable with signal-conditioning silicon in the connector; IA's AAOI thesis hinges on 1.6T AEC not reaching ~6m, which would force optics (and high-power lasers) into xAI's Vera Rubin reach.
