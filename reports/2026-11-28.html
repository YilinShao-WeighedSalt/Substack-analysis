Jargon decoder — terms used this run
Trainium3Amazon's 3rd-gen custom AI chip (AWS Annapurna), built ground-up for ML — now rivaling Nvidia on cost-per-token.
perf/$ (TCO)Tokens generated per dollar of total cost of ownership — the number that sets an accelerator's inference gross margin.
AgentX / InferenceXSemiAnalysis's benchmark that replays real coding-agent sessions to rank accelerators on realistic serving workloads.
HBM base dieThe logic chip at the bottom of an HBM memory stack that talks to the accelerator; who designs it owns the stack's spec.
3D DRAMStacking DRAM cells vertically (NAND-style) to beat the density wall; SK hynix targets ~2031, 100+ layers.
XtackingYMTC's wafer-bonding NAND (array + logic built separately, then bonded) — now the industry path for high-layer NAND; Samsung & SK licensed it.
CPOCo-packaged optics — the optical engine sits beside the switch/accelerator die to cut power; needs external laser light sources (AOI).
SSD-as-GPU-memoryLetting a GPU read flash directly (Nvidia SCADA + Kioxia) to dodge scarce HBM — a cheaper memory tier for lookups.
Remote-access ruleNew BIS rule: renting AI compute to restricted firms counts as exporting the chips (90-day license window) — the SE-Asia shipment halt flows from it.
MATCH ActProposed US law forcing allies (Netherlands/Japan) to match US export controls or face unilateral US limits on their tools; eyed for Dec defense bill.
Section 301 / 232US trade tools — 301 targets "unfair" overcapacity policy, 232 covers national-security goods; semis were exempted from the new Korea tariff.
ArF dry lithoOlder ~65nm argon-fluoride scanner; Canon re-enters as a 2nd source for memory/power chips while ASML is capacity-constrained.
