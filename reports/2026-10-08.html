Jargon decoder terms used this window · one line each

Terafab
Musk's $119B Texas fab (Tesla/SpaceX/xAI only); built & run by Musk's companies on licensed TSMC process, Intel in support — a foundry model never done before.
API-equivalent value
SemiAnalysis's yardstick for a subscription: how many dollars of pay-per-token API usage the plan's limits actually buy — the fair way to compare Claude vs ChatGPT plans.
Revenue per MW
An AI lab's dollars earned per megawatt of compute; heavily-subsidized subscriptions (10% of revenue but >40% of inference compute) drag it down ~$36M.
Circular financing
Funding loops between vendor and customer (SpaceX borrows $40B to buy Nvidia; Broadcom lends for Anthropic chips) that inflate demand on interlocking balance sheets.
BTM power
Behind-the-meter generation — gas turbines / fuel cells sited on the datacenter to skip multi-year grid-interconnection queues; the only near-term fix for the power ceiling.
MLCC
Multi-layer ceramic capacitor — ~600K per AI rack; a server-build chokepoint where Samsung Electro-Mechanics now holds >40% of AI-server supply.
FC-BGA / ABF substrate
The high-density package board beneath a big AI chip; an emerging bottleneck driving SEMCO's $5B capacity expansion.
WFE
Wafer-fab equipment (deposition/etch/litho). Tokyo Electron's ¥1T profit forecast + field-engineer hiring = a tool-order backlog stretching into 2027.
SerDes
Serializer/deserializer — the high-speed chip-to-chip link. Irrational's Oct ranking keeps Broadcom #1 and Marvell a step behind.
Pre- / post-FEC BER
Bit-error rate before / after forward-error-correction; the quality bar for an optical link — Marvell demoed 3e-6 pre / 1e-11 post (target ~2e-13).
TFLN
Thin-film lithium niobate, a fast optical-modulator material; Irrational derides it as an over-hyped "meme material" pitched by PhDs.
NVHBM / base die
The logic chip under an HBM stack; when Nvidia designs it, profit shifts from the memory maker toward Nvidia — the "Micron cope" debate.
