04 Jargon Decoder

Black NovemberThe Nov-5/6 2026 selloff: Nasdaq −5.5% (worst in 19 mo), SOX −12%, triggered by a bipartisan datacenter backlash after Democrats swept Congress and flipped datacenter-state governorships.
Marvell–Lumentum$115B all-stock merger (announced Nov-2): Marvell pairs its DSPs/switching with Lumentum's InP lasers & optics. 4.5 MRVL shares per LITE share; close targeted H1-2028.
µVCSEL vs µLEDRival 'slow-and-wide' optical scale-in light sources. Credo's micro-VCSEL can push ~20–30m reach; Avicena's micro-LED needs ~6x the fibers and must hit 10m to be viable.
NPO / CPO / ELSFPNear-/Co-Packaged Optics put the optical engine next to the switch ASIC. ELSFP = OIF's standardized external-laser module — IA argues it means Lumentum needs no vertical integration.
HBC (Qualcomm)High Bandwidth Compute: puts logic in the base die under an LPDDR stack (vs HBM, which only stacks DRAM on a 2.5D interposer). QCOM claims 6x bandwidth-per-watt; ships in AI250 mid-2027.
4F² DRAMA denser DRAM cell layout (vs today's 6F²) — the 'holy grail' of DRAM density, hard to manufacture. CXMT claims it's validated, pursuing a density edge without EUV.
Project KairosSamsung's new 2nm/4nm foundry fab for HBM logic base dies. The base die is now a foundry part — Samsung is the only HBM maker that also owns a leading-edge logic fab (SK Hynix uses TSMC).
TPUv7 'Ironwood'Google's 7th-gen inference TPU. On SemiAnalysis's AgentX benchmark it delivers competitive-to-best tokens-per-dollar vs Nvidia GB300 for external customers.
MEGTRON laminatePanasonic's low-loss copper-clad laminate for AI server/switch boards. The Hsinchu plant fire (Nov-5, 2 dead) took out ~4% of global high-end multilayer supply, already sold out.
National Stabilization FundTaiwan's state market-support vehicle (up to NT$500B). Authorized to deploy for the 10th time as TSMC — >40% of Taiwan's market cap — hit limit-down in Black November.
Behind-the-meter (BTM)On-site power (gas turbines, fuel cells, BESS) that bypasses the grid and permitting — the workaround for the datacenter-power/permitting squeeze (NTT-Bloom 900MW, SpaceX Colossus).
