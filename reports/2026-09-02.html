Jargon decoder
Sovereign AI — a nation building & controlling its own frontier model + compute, so it isn't hostage to US labs (Anthropic/OpenAI) or foreign chip supply.
Commoditize your complements — make the thing next to your product cheap & abundant (here, open AI models) so demand for your product (Nvidia GPUs) rises.
HBM4 / HBM4E — 4th-gen High-Bandwidth Memory — DRAM stacked beside the GPU; the scarce ingredient gating accelerator throughput. "E" = faster follow-on.
HBM base die — logic chip at the bottom of an HBM stack handling power + I/O; now migrating to logic foundries (why SK Hynix eyes Intel).
SOCAMM — Nvidia's compression-attached CPU-side memory module for Grace/Rubin; an LTA locks both supply and price.
High-NA EUV — ASML's next-gen litho (0.55 numerical aperture vs 0.33) for sub-2nm patterning; ~$400M per tool, now in first high-volume logic.
NVLink Fusion — Nvidia opening its NVLink interconnect so partners' custom ASICs/CPUs can plug into the Nvidia rack — the hook in the MediaTek deal.
Custom ASIC / XPU — application-specific accelerator (TPU, Trainium, OpenAI's Jalapeño) hyperscalers build to cut Nvidia dependence.
CXMT — ChangXin Memory, China's top DRAM maker, now attempting HBM3E. "Risk production" = trial output; yields/economics unproven.
Convertible bond — debt that can convert to equity — Nvidia's $3.5B into MediaTek could become a stake if converted.
