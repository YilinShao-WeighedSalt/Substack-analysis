Jargon Decoder

4-hi / 8-hi / 12-hi HBMNumber of DRAM dies stacked in one HBM cube. Shorter stacks = less capacity but the same bandwidth — so a much cheaper $/bandwidth.
HBM de-specThe industry walking back from 12-hi (and once-planned 16-hi) toward 8-hi, now 4-hi — because inference is bandwidth-bound, not capacity-bound.
KVCache offloadingPushing colder attention keys/values out of HBM into cheaper DDR DRAM; relieves the capacity pressure that shorter HBM stacks create.
Tokens / HBM-waferNew figure-of-merit: a 4-hi stack yields ~2–3× the bandwidth (≈ tokens) harvestable per scarce HBM wafer vs 8-/12-hi.
Looped transformerScaling a model by re-running inputs through layers (depth) instead of adding weights — needs less HBM capacity. Confirmed for GPT-6 Astra.
Off-B/S backstop (AICP / RVG)Nvidia mechanisms — revenue floors, lease guarantees, residual-value guarantees — that let non-investment-grade neoclouds borrow at IG pricing.
RPO (backlog)Remaining Performance Obligation — contracted future revenue not yet delivered. Oracle's leapt to $664B.
800 VDC grid-to-chipPower architecture (Eaton) that eliminates AC↔DC conversion stages between the grid and the rack, cutting losses and space.
