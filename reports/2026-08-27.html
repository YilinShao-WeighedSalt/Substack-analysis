Jargon decoder

Jalapeño — OpenAI's first custom inference ASIC (built with Broadcom); N3P, HBM4, MXFP4 math. First non-TPU/Trainium ASIC to beat merchant GPUs on a third-party benchmark.
perf/W · tokens/MW — throughput per watt. In a power-limited datacenter, "throughput per watt is revenue" (Jensen) — the metric OpenAI designed Jalapeño around.
Vera Rubin — Nvidia's next-gen HBM4 platform, shipping to customers now. The fair comparison for Jalapeño (not the older HBM3e Blackwell/GB300).
HBM4 — 4th-gen high-bandwidth memory; ~15.4 TB/s per stack on Jalapeño. SemiAnalysis believes the stacks are Samsung's — a rare Samsung HBM design-win.
PDD (prefill-decode disaggregation) — splitting inference into separate prompt-reading vs token-generating pools. Jalapeño skips it, keeping a fungible fleet that never strands idle chips.
MTIA 300 / TPU v10 — Meta's and Google's newest in-house accelerators. MTIA moves the network fabric onto the chip; TPU v10 goes multi-vendor via MediaTek for the first time.
Codex-written kernels — Jalapeño's low-level kernels are authored by OpenAI's own model, not humans. Proof AI is now compressing chip bring-up from years to months.
zHBM / 3D-DRAM — memory stacked directly on/into the compute die (Samsung zHBM; d-Matrix bonds DRAM face-to-face at 36µm). Attacks the bandwidth wall past conventional HBM.
chipflation — memory-cost inflation bleeding into consumer prices; Amazon raised Echo/Kindle/Fire TV up to 60%, blaming memory.
YMTC / CXMT — China's NAND and DRAM champions. YMTC targets top-3 NAND share by 2027; CXMT just qualified LPDDR6 for Xiaomi — direct pressure on Micron/SK Hynix.
SIVR / VPD / IVR — on-package voltage regulation (Infineon's C2i buy; Navitas' Claros buy) delivering power right at the GPU to survive AI rack densities.
Groq 3 LPX — Nvidia's inference rack now in mass production at Samsung Foundry (4nm) — a credibility win for Samsung's foundry recovery.
