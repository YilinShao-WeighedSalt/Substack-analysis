Jargon decoder

HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory)
DRAM stacked vertically next to a GPU for huge bandwidth; the scarcest, highest-margin AI memory. SK Hynix leads via MR-MUF packaging.
LTA (Long-Term Agreement)
Multi-year supply-and-price contract; SK Hynix/Samsung signing them with hyperscalers to lock in shortage-era pricing to ~2028.
MLCC
Multi-layer ceramic capacitor — tiny passive part; AI servers need thousands. The 47µF/1005 unit is the current bottleneck (7× demand growth).
Immersion DUV
Deep-ultraviolet lithography using a water lens; prints 28nm single-pass, ~7nm via multi-patterning. China's new domestic tool ≈ ASML's 2008 model.
QTL
Qualcomm's patent-licensing arm — its highest-margin cash engine. The Apple licensing deal expires Apr-1-2027, the crux of the modem 'war'.
Modem design-out
A phone maker replacing Qualcomm's cellular modem with its own; Apple's C1 ramp is the multi-year QCOM revenue cliff.
Modular / prefab DC
Building datacenters from factory-made blocks (power skids, cooling CDUs, white-space pods) bolted together on-site — ~36% faster, ~8% cheaper.
Content per MW
Dollars a vendor captures per megawatt of datacenter. Vertiv's full-stack modular push doubles it from ~$3.5M to ~$7M/MW.
BTM (behind-the-meter)
On-site power (fuel cells, gas) that skips the multi-year grid-interconnect queue; Bloom Energy's core AI-datacenter pitch.
CDS (credit default swap)
Insurance on a borrower's debt; widening hyperscaler CDS spreads = debt markets nervous about AI-capex 'circular financing'.
EDA
Electronic design automation (Cadence/Synopsys duopoly) — the software to design chips; revenue tracks tape-out volume, rising with the AI-ASIC boom.
Nvidia DSX
Nvidia's reference blueprint for an entire AI-factory (compute+power+cooling+civil) as a digital twin — standardizes the modular datacenter.
