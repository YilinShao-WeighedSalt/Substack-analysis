Jargon decoder — terms used this run

BIS remote-access rule
New US rule (effective Nov 19) that treats renting AI compute like shipping a chip: any datacenter worldwide needs a license to give >12M "aggregate TPP" to China-HQ'd firms — closes the overseas-datacenter loophole and formally kills the Biden "AI Diffusion Rule".
Aggregate TPP
"Total Processing Performance" — the compute metric the export rule is written against; 12M TPP is the per-customer license trigger.
Vera Rubin
Nvidia's GPU platform after Blackwell; booked ~$20B in its first production quarter (FQ3) and topped MLPerf Training — the ramp behind the $130B Q4 guide.
HBM4 ($/Gb)
4th-gen High-Bandwidth Memory stacked on each accelerator; TrendForce sees $3–5.50/Gb in 2027 vs ~$2.05 now (+122% avg) and no conventional-DRAM price cut all year — HBM eats 3–4× the wafers.
CoWoS / on-substrate
TSMC's chip-on-wafer-on-substrate packaging for GPUs; so capacity-starved TSMC is handing the "on-substrate" step to affiliate Xintec (inside a TSMC fab) and buying AUO fabs for glass substrates.
ABF substrate
Ajinomoto-Build-up-Film board under each GPU — a chronic chokepoint; Kinsus is growing Nvidia-dedicated capacity >3.5× (Nvidia funding >$1B).
TPU 8TX / 8i Max
Two new Google TPU systems (not new silicon) built on the V8 chip: 8TX = confidential-compute with an AMD EPYC host; 8i Max = memory-maxed for inference.
TPU Sync
Google's inference-orchestration layer — its answer to Nvidia's Dynamo, and a step toward selling TPUs to outside customers.
MI455X / Helios
AMD's next Instinct GPU and rack; OpenAI just booked 457 MW of it via neocloud TensorWave (first named supplier under the OpenAI–AMD deal).
"Bank of Nvidia"
Nvidia's financing of GPU-cloud startups; now "disciplined and selective" — UBS says a pullback could pull $50B+ from the market, and lenders (QTS) are already cutting debt sales.
Wafer bonding (HBM5)
Samsung's chosen next-gen DRAM track — stacking memory via direct wafer-to-wafer bonding for HBM5.
