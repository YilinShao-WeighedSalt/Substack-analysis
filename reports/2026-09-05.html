Jargon Decoder · terms used this week, one line each

Hybrid bonding — direct copper-to-copper die stacking at fine pitch; higher density than solder bumps but yield-hard. TSMC just deferred it for HBM.
Microbump — the older solder-based die interconnect (TSMC sits at ~6µm pitch); coarser but reliably high-yield — why TSMC is staying on it for HBM.
CoWoS — TSMC's chip-on-wafer-on-substrate advanced packaging; the industry's AI-chip bottleneck that everyone is trying to route around.
EMIB-T — Intel Foundry's embedded silicon-bridge packaging, now gaining traction as a CoWoS alternative — the crux of the "big tech courts Intel packaging" story.
zHBM / 3D-stacked HBM — stacking DRAM vertically on logic to boost capacity in the same footprint; thermally hard. Samsung is publicly staking ground here vs SK Hynix's lead.
HBM4 — next-gen high-bandwidth memory; the US is pushing Korea to accelerate it, where China's gap stays widest.
CXMT — China's leading DRAM maker, now ~10% of global DRAM revenue — a threshold Samsung/SK took decades to reach.
Project Hefei — the plan a Korean court found lifted a 620-step Samsung DRAM recipe into CXMT — the "theft case" behind China's DRAM sprint.
NAND-as-DRAM substitution — Kioxia (and Micron/YMTC) pitching high-speed NAND for AI-inference tiers where latency tolerance is loose and $/GB rules.
"Compilers 2.0" — using an LLM as a stochastic optimizer to write GPU kernels (STOKE lineage) — verified vs a numpy spec, so nobody reads the code. Wrote OpenAI's Jalapeño MLA kernel.
Vera Rubin — Nvidia's next-gen accelerator platform after Blackwell — the silicon Figure AI is buying 100k of via Nscale for robotics.
XPU — Broadcom's term for its custom AI accelerators — Hock claims <½ the cost-per-GW of an Nvidia GPU for the workloads they target.
High-NA EUV — ASML's most advanced lithography tool; reportedly reaching production-grade availability by end-2026, with China ~a decade behind.
MLCC / ABF substrate — multi-layer ceramic capacitors and packaging substrate — both tightening (SEMCO eyeing ~30% MLCC hikes) as AI-server demand strains passives.
