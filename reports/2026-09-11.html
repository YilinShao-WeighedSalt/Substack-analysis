Jargon decoder

High-NA EUV
Next-gen extreme-ultraviolet lithography with a wider 0.55 lens aperture — prints finer features in one pass. ~$350-400M/machine; TSMC now committed for 2030, Samsung for DRAM by 2028.
Behind-the-meter (BTM)
Power generated on-site, bypassing the public grid — 'off-grid' / 'islanded'. Faster to stand up than waiting years for a grid interconnection; SemiAnalysis tracks 75 GW of firm orders.
Reciprocating engine / genset
A gas piston-engine generator (0.5-20 MW/unit) — the surprise BTM winner over big turbines; 22 OEMs now have multi-hundred-MW datacenter orders, up from 12 a year ago.
Solid-oxide fuel cell
Bloom Energy's on-site power unit; carries nearly all BTM fuel-cell orders (3.8 GW) and permits more easily than combustion turbines.
Custom AI ASIC
A chip designed by a cloud/model firm (Trainium, TPU, OpenAI's Jalapeño) instead of buying a merchant Nvidia GPU — the wave QCOM-Amazon and OpenAI-Samsung just widened.
NVLink Fusion
Nvidia's scale-up interconnect opened to third-party accelerators so they plug into Nvidia racks — d-Matrix's Raptor and MediaTek both wired in; the chip only fully performs inside Nvidia's fabric.
HBM
High-bandwidth memory — DRAM stacked beside the GPU; the AI-compute bottleneck. A GPT-6-class model launch reads straight through to HBM demand.
Cost per token / perf-per-$
The inference-economics yardstick. Google's TPUv7 Ironwood came in ~50% better perf-per-dollar than Nvidia B200/B300 in the first third-party test.
Mature node
An older, larger process (UMC, SMIC) — re-rating on AI-adjacent power/analog demand rather than leading-edge logic.
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)
A model that activates only a subset of 'expert' sub-networks per token, cutting inference cost — DeepSeek's V4.1 Flash is a 552B-param MoE priced at fractions of a cent per million tokens.
Acqui-hire / license-not-buy
Structuring a deal as a license rather than an acquisition to stay under merger-review thresholds — the DOJ is now probing whether Nvidia's Groq deal did exactly that.
FC-BGA / glass substrate
Advanced package substrates that carry the chip; microcrack yield is the hard part. Korean rivals (Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek) are racing to glass by 2028.
